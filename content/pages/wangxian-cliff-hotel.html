Overview

From Granite Mine
to Viral Fairyland

Wangxian Valley's cliffside village was rebuilt between 2015 and 2020 on the site of an abandoned granite mine, its homestays and restaurants staggered up a genuine 110-metre cliff face using traditional Gan architectural methods.

Rammed-earth walls, blue stone slabs and mortise-tenon joinery — classic building techniques from northeastern Jiangxi — were used to construct dwellings paying homage to the hermit traditions of Eastern Han dynasty Taoist monks, who once sought distraction-free enlightenment in vertical rock faces like this one. Opened on 31 January 2022, the cliff homestay offers over ten glass-walled guestrooms alongside cliffside restaurants and high-altitude viewing platforms built directly into the White Cliff. No two rooms are alike — some carry marble-walled decor for a more luxurious feel, others rustic wooden furniture reviving the simplicity of village life — but all share the same vertiginous outlook over the valley below and the Dragon's Back ridge in the distance.

A large complex, not a single small guesthouse. Despite the name suggesting a small bed-and-breakfast, this is a substantial hotel complex with rooms spread throughout the valley — worth knowing when planning where exactly to book.

Experiences

A Glass Walkway,
388 Metres Long

Wangxian Valley's dramatic granite scenery, ancient bridges and cliffside walkways have made it one of Jiangxi's most photographed destinations.

Thrill

The Glass Walkway

A 388-metre walkway along the cliff face, with a frosted glass section giving 360-degree views into the canyon below.

Heritage

Qingyun Bridge

A gently arched stone bridge whose name, Bridge of Ascending to the Clouds, reflects its auspicious local reputation.

On Foot

The Immortal-Seeking Path

A trail combining bluestone paving with cliffside plank walkways, following the valley's natural contours past streams and waterfalls.

Culture

Wangxian Folk Town

Handicraft workshops, folk performances and local Gan-family snacks fill the valley's reconstructed old streets.

Nature

Wangxian Waterfall & Triple Waterfall

A series of cascades scattered through the valley, including the namesake Wangxian Waterfall and the Triple Waterfall, Sancui Shui.

History

Lingguang Street & Historic Halls

Preserved village architecture, including Yang Mansion and Hu's Family Ancestral Hall, tells the story of the valley's pre-mine history.

Location

Wangxian Valley,
Jiangxi's Fairyland

Wangxian Valley sits in Guangxin District, Shangrao, adjacent to the main peak of Lingshan Mountain and facing Mount Sanqing, a roughly 50-kilometre drive from central Shangrao.

  • Located at Wangxian Township, Guangxin District, Shangrao, Jiangxi Province.
  • Adjacent to Lingshan Mountain's main peak, facing the UNESCO-listed Mount Sanqing.
  • A large valley resort with rooms spread throughout the site, connected by shuttle service.
  • Reconstructed between 2015 and 2020 on the site of a formerly abandoned granite mine.
Fly

Sanqingshan Airport connects to Wangxian Valley by road, a comparable distance to nearby Gexian Village.

Rail

Shangrao's rail stations link to China's wider high-speed network, with a road transfer to the valley.

Arrive

A homestay service centre on Lingguang Street coordinates check-in and shuttle transfers throughout the valley.
