Day by Day

Your Itinerary

Windhoek, Namibia

Day 1 | Chauffeur Meet & Greet & Luxury Urban Estate Tour

Met directly at arrivals by your private guide, who escorts you to your custom air-conditioned 4x4 Land Cruiser — featuring on-board Wi-Fi, an artisanal minibar stocked with premium beverages, plush leather seating, and every comfort for the journey ahead. For arrivals before 4:00 PM, an exclusive private tour of Windhoek explores the historic Christuskirche and the Tintenpalast gardens. The day culminates in a boutique wine-pairing dinner and a briefing with your private guide on the days ahead.

Hilton Hotel, Windhoek
Lunch, Dinner
Sossusvlei — Desert Whisper

Day 2 | Scenic Drive to Sossusvlei — Remhoogte Pass

Departing Windhoek, your luxury 4x4 winds through the spectacular Remhoogte Pass — breathtaking panoramic views of the dramatic escarpment and the vast Namib Desert stretching endlessly ahead. Carefully selected vantage points for refreshments and photographs throughout the 4.5-hour drive. Check-in at Desert Whisper: an exclusive-use luxury villa with unparalleled privacy and panoramic desert views. Premium sundowners as the desert transforms into gold and crimson.

Desert Whisper Exclusive Villa
Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Day 3 | The Red Dune Masterpieces — Deadvlei at Dawn

Departing before dawn via the private lodge gate into Namib-Naukluft National Park — bypassing all public queues to reach Deadvlei as the first sun illuminates the apricot dunes. Walk through the stark white clay floor surrounded by 900-year-old black camel thorn trees in absolute solitude. Climb the spine of Big Daddy before a luxury white-linen champagne breakfast under a shaded camel thorn tree in the middle of the desert, far from any other tourists. The afternoon is reserved for relaxation at Desert Whisper.

Desert Whisper Exclusive Villa
Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner
Swakopmund — The Strand Hotel

Day 4 | Through Canyons to the Coast — Kuiseb Canyon & Swakopmund

A smooth 4-hour drive across desert plains and through the prehistoric Kuiseb Canyon — a dramatic riverbed cutting through the ancient landscape. Your guide points out unique desert-adapted flora including the iconic welwitschia plants that can live for over a thousand years. As you approach the coast, the landscape shifts dramatically. Check-in at The Strand Hotel — a luxurious beachfront property with expansive sea suites and uninterrupted ocean views. A private in-room coastal spa session in the afternoon.

The Strand Hotel, Swakopmund
Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Day 5 | Luxury Ocean & High Dunes — Walvis Bay Catamaran & Sandwich Harbour

Morning aboard a private catamaran in Walvis Bay: wild pelicans and Cape fur seals alongside the vessel, fresh Atlantic oysters, and cold Cap Classique sparkling wine. The afternoon brings a thrilling private 4x4 drive conquering the massive sand dunes of Sandwich Harbour — expert drivers navigating towering dunes plunging into the Atlantic Ocean. Atop the highest dunes for a photographic session capturing this surreal landscape. Gourmet seafood dinner at the hotel.

The Strand Hotel, Swakopmund
Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner
Erongo — Spitzkoppen Lodge

Day 6 | Into the Erongo Boulders — Ancient San Rock Art

Depart Swakopmund for a 2.5-hour drive inland to the Erongo region through scenic rolling hills and ancient volcanic formations. Check-in at Spitzkoppen Lodge — luxury chalets built directly into giant granite boulders with unparalleled views and absolute privacy. Afternoon guided walk to ancient San rock art created thousands of years ago. These remarkable petroglyphs provide a fascinating glimpse into the spiritual and daily lives of Namibia's earliest inhabitants. A private sundowner with panoramic mountain views.

Spitzkoppen Lodge
Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Day 7 | The Arches of Spitzkoppe — Cocktail Bar Under the Rock Arch

Exploring the surreal geology of Spitzkoppe — the "Matterhorn of Namibia." A photographic tour of the famous Rock Arch, a breathtaking natural formation that has become an iconic symbol of Namibian beauty. The luxury highlight: a private bartender setting up a premium cocktail station directly beneath the Rock Arch as the sun sets behind the granite pillars, painting the sky in shades of gold, crimson and purple. Hidden caves and dramatic formations explored before a gourmet dinner under the stars.

Spitzkoppen Lodge
Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner
Okonjima — Bush Suite

Day 8 | Big Cat Conservation — Okonjima Leopard Tracking

A 2.5-hour drive on seamless tar roads to Okonjima Nature Reserve — one of Namibia's premier conservation destinations. Check-in at the ultra-exclusive Okonjima Bush Suite: a private villa with a private chef and host, offering the ultimate in privacy and personalised service. Private safari to track radio-collared leopards with expert conservationists who share deep knowledge of these elusive predators. Gourmet dinner prepared by your private chef, served under the brilliant African sky.

Okonjima Bush Suite
Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Day 9 | Exclusive San Bushmen Cultural Walk — Tracking, Fire & Botany

A deeply immersive non-commercial cultural experience with the San (Bushmen) hunter-gatherers — one of Africa's most ancient and resilient indigenous groups. San elders on a gentle tracking walk share their extraordinary knowledge of the natural world: deciphering near-invisible animal tracks, locating hidden water tubers beneath dry earth, traditional hunter-gatherer botany, and ancient fire-making ceremonies. This dignified encounter ensures total privacy and mutual respect. Farewell dinner prepared by your private chef.

Okonjima Bush Suite
Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner
Return — Windhoek

Day 10 | Seamless Return to Windhoek & Complementary Spa Session

A relaxed breakfast in the tranquility of the reserve before departing in your private 4x4 Land Cruiser for a smooth 3-hour transfer directly to Windhoek on the main national tar highway. Transfer to the Hilton Hotel for a complementary spa session. A private city tour of Windhoek is offered on this day if it was missed upon arrival. International departure the following day.

Hilton Hotel, Windhoek (day use)

Journey Details

Minimum Age

Varies by camp, activity and national park

  • Camps and lodges — Most safari camps accept children from six years, with shared game-drive vehicles usually from eight to twelve. Many properties will take younger children on a privately booked vehicle.
  • Walking safaris — Guided walking safaris carry a firm minimum of fifteen or sixteen years across most reserves. This is set by the licensing authority, not the camp, and is never waived.
  • Gorilla and chimpanzee trekking — Uganda and Rwanda both enforce a strict minimum of fifteen years. It is a national park regulation and there are no exceptions.
  • Hot-air balloon safaris — Operators generally require children to be at least seven, and tall enough to brace unaided in the basket on landing.
  • Family and exclusive-use camps — A number of our camps run dedicated family units, child-minding and tailored activity programmes, and will relax their usual age limit for a sole-use booking.
  • Talk to us first — Age limits vary from property to property and from activity to activity. Speak to our experts before you book, and we will pre-arrange the details with each lodge around the ages of the children travelling with you.

Safari Duffel

Duffel, safari hat, vest and hooded jumper

  • Your duffel — A soft-sided {{brand}} duffel, sized for the 15 kg limit on the light aircraft that move you between camps. Rigid suitcases cannot be carried on those flights at all.
  • What is inside it — A branded {{brand}} safari hat, a safari vest, and a hooded jumper for cold dawn starts and balloon mornings.
  • Yours to keep — The bag and everything in it is a gift, not a loan. Nothing is returned at the end of your journey.
  • Packing around it — Soft luggage, neutral colours, and layers. Laundry is included at nearly every camp, which is why 15 kg goes further than it sounds.

Note

Vast distances mean long overland transfers

Namibia's dramatic landscapes are spread across huge distances — some days include extended drive times or light-aircraft transfers between camps. Nights can be very cold in the desert regardless of daytime heat.

  • Early starts — Safaris begin very early in the morning. Your guide will confirm what time you will be collected from your camp or lodge.
  • Tailoring this journey — If you would like this package with more or fewer days, arranged for a group, a family or a solo traveller, or with add-ons, reach out and we will tailor it for you.
  • Route changes — Your guide or driver may alter the route in certain circumstances, but every destination set out in the itinerary will be covered.

Documents

Visa-free for most nationalities

  • Passport — Valid for at least six months beyond your departure date, with several entirely blank pages. Namibian immigration is notably strict about blank pages.
  • Visa on arrival since April 2025 — From 1 April 2025 US passport holders, and citizens of a number of other previously exempt countries, must take a visa on arrival valid for up to 30 days. Many European, Commonwealth and African nationals still enter visa-free.
  • Take the eVisa instead — A pre-arranged eVisa is available and well worth having — the visa-on-arrival queue at Hosea Kutako can be long after a night flight.
  • Travelling with children — An unabridged birth certificate showing both parents is required for every traveller under 18. Where a child travels with one parent only, or with neither, a sworn parental consent affidavit is also needed.
  • We file it for you — Whether you are flying in from overseas or travelling from elsewhere on the continent, we complete and submit your visa application on your behalf. You hand us your documents once. We do the filing, chase the approval, and carry the confirmations in your travel pack so nothing rests on you remembering a reference number at a counter.
  • Most of Africa is open to you — The great majority of our African destinations are now either visa-free or issue a visa on arrival, so an embassy queue is rarely in your future at all. Where a visa on arrival does apply, we prepare and lodge the paperwork ahead of you — you reach the desk with a finished file rather than a blank form and a biro.
  • Before you travel — Entry rules change frequently and without much notice. Your specialist confirms the current requirements for every passport in your party at the time of booking, and checks them again before you fly.

Guaranteed Departures

Private, tailor-made departures on your dates, or join a small group

  • Once confirmed, you travel — When your departure date is confirmed, it is guaranteed. We do not cancel, merge or reschedule a confirmed departure for commercial reasons, however the final numbers fall.
  • What confirms a departure — A departure becomes confirmed once the agreed number of travellers has been reached and paid for in line with the agreed payment schedule. Places held without payment do not count towards it.
  • If the minimum is not reached — Where a group departure has not reached its agreed numbers, we tell you early rather than late, and you choose: move to the next available date, continue at a revised small-group rate, or take a full refund of everything you have paid.
  • Solo journeys — Solo and tailor-made itineraries carry no group minimum at all. Your dates are confirmed the moment your deposit is received.
  • Visas — We prepare and submit your visa application on your behalf. Please note that the granting of a visa rests solely with the immigration authority of the country concerned, and is never guaranteed by us.
  • Medication — We arrange malaria prophylaxis, together with anti-nausea and anti-anxiety tablets, for your journey. Any prescription medicine should be confirmed with your own doctor before you travel — they remain the final authority on what is suitable for you.
  • Africa is not always hot — Despite the stereotype, Africa moves through distinct seasons and mornings at altitude can be genuinely cold. A hooded jumper is provided for cold conditions, and for every guest joining a hot-air balloon safari.
  • Dietary requirements — If you have strict dietary needs, please tell us at the time of booking. This includes vegans, guests in recovery, non-drinkers, and anyone following a prescription from their doctor.
  • Travel insurance — We facilitate travel insurance for our clients. If you are already covered, please say so during booking so we can take it into account in your final quote.
  • Tailoring your quote — Some of our inclusions — hot-air balloon safaris, for example — may not be what you want. Point these out and we will adjust your final offer accordingly.
  • The Maasai massage & spa recovery — Guests travelling the safari circuit by road rather than by air should expect long and tiring days. The rough terrain of the jungle gives the vehicle a constant jolting ride, known affectionately as the Maasai massage. A spa session is provided at the end of your safari to recover.
  • What to pack — Neutral colours are the rule on safari: khaki, olive, stone and brown. Avoid black and dark blue, which draw tsetse flies, and leave camouflage at home entirely — it is reserved for the military and illegal for civilians in several African countries. Bring layers for cold dawn game drives, long sleeves and trousers for dusk when the mosquitoes are out, closed walking shoes, a sun hat and sunglasses. Lodge dinners are smart-casual; nothing formal is needed.
  • Your safari duffel & kit — The duffel bag is used when flying into the parks, where luggage is limited to 15 kg per person. A branded {{brand}} safari hat and safari vest are also provided.
