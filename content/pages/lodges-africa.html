Where the story of safari is still being written.

Africa carries the bulk of this collection for a reason — this is where the words ‘safari lodge’ were coined, and where more than a hundred and thirty properties still work to earn them, from a private concession in Laikipia to a landmark hotel on Cape Town's waterfront.

At one end sit the exclusive-use camps and exclusive concessions — Singita, &Beyond, Segera, Malori — where the land itself is the point, and a handful of guests have it entirely to themselves. Alongside them are the classic tented camps of Kenya and Tanzania, the desert lodges of Namibia's Skeleton Coast and NamibRand, and the river camps of Botswana's Okavango Delta and Zimbabwe's Zambezi.

At the other end are the cities that bookend a safari or stand as destinations of their own — Marrakech's Royal Mansour, Cairo's Four Seasons at Nile Plaza, Cape Town's Silo Hotel, Nairobi's Hemingways. Ten countries, three coastlines and a desert in between mean this collection covers more ground than any other region we work in.
