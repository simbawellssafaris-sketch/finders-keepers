Overview

A Reimagined
Serengeti Icon

Sitting high on the Nyaboro Hills with front-row seats to the Great Migration, Serengeti Explorer is Elewana’s newest and largest addition to the Serengeti, opened in 2024.

Built on the grounds of the former Serengeti Sopa Lodge and entirely reimagined, Serengeti Explorer occupies one of the most central yet private settings in the park — accessible to the Moru Kopjes, the Great Migration corridors, and year-round resident game. With 70 Luxury Explorer Rooms and 5 Suites across elevated tiers, it is a considerably larger property than most Serengeti camps, built to combine world-class wildlife access with an extensive range of in-property facilities rarely found this deep in the bush, including the Serengeti's first on-site game-viewing hide.

Location

South-Central
Serengeti

Serengeti Explorer sits high on the Nyaboro Hills escarpment in the western corridor of Serengeti National Park, overlooking the park’s south-western plains.

  • The lodge’s elevated position gives sweeping views over the plains, with the Great Migration typically passing through March to June.
  • Moru Kopjes, a short drive away, is home to the Serengeti’s only population of black rhino as well as ancient Maasai rock paintings.
  • The lodge is roughly a 45-minute transfer from Seronera Airstrip, itself an hour’s flight from Arusha.
  • Serengeti Explorer is part of Elewana’s wider circuit discount programme alongside Serengeti Pioneer Camp and other Elewana lodges.
