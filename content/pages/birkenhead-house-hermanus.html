Overview

Built on the Site
of a Shipwreck's Namesake

Birkenhead House takes its name from the British troop ship that sank in Walker Bay in 1852, its cliff-top position today making it one of the best land-based whale-watching spots in the world.

Eleven individually styled rooms are spread across the Front, Middle and Back House, each mixing modern and antique furnishings, shell-covered armoires and contemporary artwork with a distinctly beach-house sense of fun. Front House rooms sit closest to the restaurant with uninterrupted ocean views; rooms further back trade a partial view for extra privacy. A central swimming pool and a smaller plunge pool at the front give guests two distinct places to take in Walker Bay, while a telescope in the main sitting room helps spot southern right, humpback and the rarer Bryde's whales as they pass.

Part of The Royal Portfolio. Birkenhead House shares its ownership with La Residence in Franschhoek and The Silo in Cape Town, all part of Liz Biden's Royal Portfolio collection.

Experiences

Southern Right Whales,
From the Cliff

Hermanus is widely regarded as one of the best places on Earth for land-based whale watching, and Birkenhead House sits directly above the action.

Season

Southern Right Whale Season

From June to November, whales pass close enough to shore to watch comfortably from the house's decks and rooms.

On the Water

Boat-Based Whale Watching

Hermanus Whale Cruises and other operators run harbour-boat excursions for closer encounters.

Nearby

Dyer Island & Shark Alley

A 40-minute drive to Gansbaai reaches Dyer Island's penguin and seal colonies, and the shark cage-diving grounds of Shark Alley.

On Foot

Fernkloof Nature Reserve

Over 1,100 plant species have been identified within this reserve's mountain trails, a short drive from the house.

Wine

Hemel-en-Aarde Wine Route

One of South Africa's most acclaimed Pinot Noir regions lies just beyond Hermanus, a natural pairing with a coastal stay.

Foraging

Beach Foraging

Guests can forage mussels and sea urchins from the rocks below the house, then have the chef prepare the catch.

Dining

Location

Hermanus,
the Whale Capital

Birkenhead House sits on a cliff top on the outskirts of Hermanus, overlooking Walker Bay roughly ninety minutes from Cape Town.

  • Perched directly above Walker Bay, one of the world's best land-based whale-watching locations.
  • A short walk to nearby beaches, just below the house's rim-flow pool.
  • A 90-minute scenic drive from Cape Town along the coast.
  • Close to Gansbaai's shark cage-diving grounds and the Hemel-en-Aarde wine route.
Fly

Cape Town International Airport is roughly 90 minutes from the house by car.

Drive

A scenic coastal drive along the R44 brings guests directly into Hermanus.
