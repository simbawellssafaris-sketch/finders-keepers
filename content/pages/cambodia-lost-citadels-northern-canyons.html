Itinerary

Siem Reap, Cambodia

Day 1 | Nairobi → Siem Reap — Arrival in the Ancient Plains

VIP handling at Nairobi's Jomo Kenyatta Airport, flight to Siem Reap-Angkor International Airport (SAI). VIP pickup, private luxury 4x4 SUV north through rural landscapes into the ancient dry-zone plains.

Check into The Privilege Floor by Lotus Blanc — Luxury Suite — or an exclusive-use luxury mobile safari camp. Private guided tour of the local area if arriving before 4:00 PM. Welcome dinner.

The Privilege Floor by Lotus Blanc
Dinner
Koh Ker

Day 2 | The Seven-Tiered Pyramid of Koh Ker

Drive deep into the northern plains to Koh Ker — a monumental 36-metre-high seven-tiered sandstone step pyramid rising out of dense jungle, looking completely like a Mayan structure in the heart of Cambodia.

Built by King Jayavarman IV in the 10th century as a rival capital to Angkor. The surrounding temples and hidden ruins of this forgotten city. Return for gourmet dinner.

The Privilege Floor by Lotus Blanc
Breakfast, Dinner
Preah Vihear

Day 3 | The Sovereign Cliff Temple of Preah Vihear

Travel up the steep switchbacks of the Dângrêk mountain range to the UNESCO World Heritage Site of Preah Vihear — ancient stone sanctuaries perched on a 525-metre vertical cliff face with the Cambodian and Thai plains stretching to the horizon.

Premium vintage wines and appetizers served on the edge of the 525-metre cliff beside the ancient stone sanctuaries, watching the sun set over the plains. One of the most extraordinary sundowners in Asia.

The Privilege Floor by Lotus Blanc
Breakfast, Dinner
Sambor Prei Kuk

Day 4 | Lost Brick Temples of Sambor Prei Kuk

The pre-Angkorian octagonal brick temples of Sambor Prei Kuk (UNESCO) — dating to the 7th century, ancient stone carvings hidden inside deep forest clearings, the brick towers rising from the jungle floor.

Your historian explains a civilisation that predates Angkor itself. The surrounding primary forest and hidden ruins. Return for gourmet dinner.

The Privilege Floor by Lotus Blanc
Breakfast, Dinner
Preah Khan of Kompong Svay

Day 5 | Preah Khan of Kompong Svay — Remote Temple Expedition

An exclusive 4x4 day-expedition to the massive remote temple complex of Preah Khan of Kompong Svay — collapsed towers completely untouched by standard commercial tourism, the jungle reclaiming the stones.

A hot bento breakfast set up inside an open-air stone gallery facing a quiet forest pond before anyone else arrives. The profound silence of a 12th-century capital far from any tourist trail.

The Privilege Floor by Lotus Blanc
Breakfast, Dinner
Siem Reap

Day 6 | Return to Siem Reap — Amansara

Drive back to Siem Reap and check into Amansara — Pool Suite. A relaxed afternoon inside the private garden courtyard pool, decompressing from the remote north.

Gourmet dinner at the estate.

Amansara, Siem Reap (Pool Suite)
Breakfast, Dinner

Day 7 | Stone-Carving Masterclass with a Master Artisan

A private masterclass at an exclusive artisan training centre — chiselling traditional Apsara celestial dancers into sandstone blocks guided by a master carver. Creating a small carving to take home.

The geometric art of Khmer stone carving, unchanged in technique for a thousand years. Return for a relaxed evening.

Amansara
Breakfast, Dinner

Day 8 | Resort Spa & Torch-Lit Khmer Bonfire Banquet

A relaxed afternoon spa massage at Amansara using local organic argan and ginger oils.

A dramatic bonfire dinner on an open-air stone terrace overlooking the resort's private gardens, warmed by a roaring central bonfire.

Amansara
Breakfast, Dinner

Day 9 | Exclusive Khmer Pottery & Heritage Tour

An isolated mountain village cooperative with indigenous master potters and historians — the 900-year history of clay firing used during the Angkorian empire, traditional kiln engineering.

A private performance of folk string music and oral storytelling. Celebratory farewell dinner at Amansara paired with vintage wines.

Amansara
Breakfast, Dinner

Day 10 | Return to Nairobi — Departure

A relaxed luxury breakfast. Smooth chauffeur transfer to Siem Reap-Angkor International Airport. Complementary spa session if time permits. International flight back to Nairobi.

Journey Details

Travel Kit

Duffel, sun hat, rain shell and day pack

  • Your duffel — A soft-sided {{brand}} duffel, sized for domestic flights and easier than a hard case on train platforms, ferry jetties and old-town lanes.
  • What is inside it — A wide-brim sun hat, a lightweight packable rain shell for tropical downpours, and a foldable day pack for temple mornings and market afternoons.
  • Yours to keep — The bag and everything in it is a gift, not a loan.
  • Packing around it — Slip-on shoes save time at temples, where you remove them repeatedly, and one set of clothing covering shoulders and knees is essential for religious sites.

Note

Temple visits are best planned around the heat

Angkor Archaeological Park is at its most comfortable in the early morning — most itineraries schedule temple visits for sunrise or late afternoon to avoid the midday heat.

  • Early starts — The best of this journey happens at dawn — before the heat, the crowds and the haze. Your guide will confirm what time you will be collected from your hotel.
  • Tailoring this journey — If you would like this package with more or fewer days, arranged for a group, a family or a solo traveller, or with add-ons, reach out and we will tailor it for you.
  • Route changes — Your guide or driver may alter the route in certain circumstances, but every destination set out in the itinerary will be covered.

Documents

Visa on arrival or e-Visa, from $30

  • Passport — Valid for at least six months beyond your arrival date, with at least one blank page.
  • Visa — The Type T tourist eVisa costs US$30 for a 30-day single entry; a visa on arrival is also available at Phnom Penh and Siem Reap. ASEAN nationals enter free.
  • e-Arrival Card — Since January 2025 every air arrival must submit the Cambodia e-Arrival Card within the seven days before landing.
  • Your Angkor pass — Angkor passes carry your photograph, taken on site, cannot be transferred, and are checked at every temple gate. Your guide arranges this on your first morning.
  • Temple dress — Shoulders and knees must be covered to climb to Angkor Wat’s upper level. Visitors are turned away for this daily.
  • We handle the paperwork — We complete and submit your visa, eVisa or travel-authorisation applications on your behalf. You supply the documents once; we do the filing, track the approval and hold the confirmations for you.
  • Where you must appear in person — A few consulates insist on biometrics or a short interview that nobody can sit on your behalf — China requires fingerprinting for some nationalities, as do European and United States visas. We still prepare the entire file; we simply book you a single appointment and brief you beforehand so one visit settles it.
  • Before you travel — Entry rules change frequently and without much notice. Your specialist confirms the current requirements for every passport in your party at the time of booking, and checks them again before you fly.
