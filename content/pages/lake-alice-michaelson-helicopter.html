Overview

The high country, without the days it usually costs.

Most climbers on the standard routes never see either lake.

Lake Alice sits at around 3,550 metres, a still sheet of water below the peaks. Lake Michaelson is higher and harder still — up near 4,000 metres in the Gorges Valley, just below Vivienne Falls, ringed by cliff and scree.

A helicopter turns both into a single morning: a low pass over the Lewis Glacier en route, a landing and picnic stop at one or both lakes, then the descent back to the foothills before lunch — a trip that would otherwise take the better part of a week on foot.
