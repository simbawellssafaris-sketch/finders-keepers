Overview

Six Floors,
Above a Museum

The Silo occupies the elevator tower of Cape Town's historic 1924 grain silo, its bulging pillowed-glass windows designed by Heatherwick Studio rising directly above the Zeitz Museum of Contemporary Art Africa.

Once the tallest building in Sub-Saharan Africa, the grain elevator processed South African maize for export until operations ceased in 2001; today its lower floors house Zeitz MOCAA, Africa's largest collection of contemporary art, while six floors above hold The Silo's 28 rooms and suites. Owner Liz Biden's interiors are bold and eclectic — Persian carpets, Egyptian chandeliers, giraffe prints and acid-bright velvets set against transparent tables and contemporary African artworks. Every room's multi-faceted glass bay, up to 5.5 metres high, frames Table Mountain, the city or Table Bay harbour, with the bathtub itself positioned to share the view.

Part of The Royal Portfolio. The Silo sits alongside La Residence and Birkenhead House within The Royal Portfolio, Liz Biden's small collection of design-led South African hotels.

Location

The Heart
of the Waterfront

The Silo rises above Cape Town's V&A Waterfront, the city's most visited precinct, with Table Mountain, the harbour and Robben Island all visible from its upper floors.

  • Located in Silo Square, within the historic grain silo complex at the V&A Waterfront.
  • Five minutes' walk to the Waterfront's shops, galleries and restaurants.
  • The Robben Island ferry departs a short walk from the hotel's entrance.
  • Designed by Heatherwick Studio, with local architecture by Rick Brown and Associates.
