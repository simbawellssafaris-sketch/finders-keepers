Overview

Inside the
Caldera

Every suite, the pool, the spa and the restaurant face the caldera.

Canaves Oia is built directly into the volcanic cliffs of Oia, and the arrangement is total: every suite, the infinity pool, the indoor-outdoor spa and the restaurant all face out across the caldera. It sits in the heart of Oia rather than out along the road, which keeps it clear of the cruise-tour traffic while leaving the village’s galleries and restaurants walkable. Suites are freshly renovated in a chic modern register, with balconies holding plunge pools above the Aegean.

The spa is inside a cave. Canaves Spa is built into a natural cave, with two treatment rooms, a Rasul steam bath, a sauna and an outdoor couples’ treatment room facing the caldera.

Location

Oia,
Santorini

The most photographed village in the Cyclades, on the northern rim of the caldera.

  • Built into the volcanic cliff in the heart of Oia.
  • Away from the cruise-tour routes, but walkable to the village’s restaurants and galleries.
  • Facing directly out over the flooded caldera and the volcanic islands within it.
  • Roughly 30 minutes from Santorini Airport, or from the port at Athinios.
