Overview

Nineteen
Twenty-Four

Twenty-five suites and two cottages across seven hectares on the river.

An Irishman named Alan Pye pitched white canvas tents along the Waikato in 1924 because the trout fishing here was the best in the country, and that is how Huka Lodge began. It became a luxury lodge in 1984 under Alex van Heeren, and remains the benchmark against which New Zealand lodges are measured. Twenty-five suites and two private cottages sit across seven hectares of riverbank; the Alan Pye Cottage, named for the founder, has its own pool, outdoor dining and a dedicated chef and butler. The river runs emerald blue past the lawn, and less than a kilometre downstream it forces itself through a volcanic gorge and becomes the Huka Falls.

Dinner moves each night. The lodge sets dinner in a different place every evening — the wine cellar, the riverbank, the trees. You are asked in the afternoon where you would like to eat.
