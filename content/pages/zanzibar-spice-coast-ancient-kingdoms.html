OverviewThis circuit focuses heavily on Zanzibar's historic trading empire — connecting the architectural palaces of Stone Town with private luxury spice estates and deep-water dhow cruising along the northern coast. Designed for luxury travellers who want to explore the island's rich cultural heritage, aromatic spice plantations, and turquoise ocean lagoons. Guests explore ancient coral-stone labyrinths, walk through century-old spice estates viewing cloves, nutmeg, and vanilla vines, and cruise along the Nungwi Peninsula on a handcrafted dhow. An exclusive northern reef sea walk, the iconic Rock Restaurant, and a private Swahili pottery and spice masters cultural encounter complete the circuit.

Stone Town

Day 1 | Arrival in Stone Town — Sultanate Elegance

VIP tarmac greeting at Abeid Amani Karume Airport. Private luxury SUV into the heart of the historic capital. Check-in at Park Hyatt Zanzibar — elegant oceanfront palace blending Swahili architecture with modern luxury. Indian Ocean views, coral-stone architecture, and the serene beauty of the historic harbour. Early arrivals explore Stone Town's narrow winding streets, carved wooden doors, and the historic waterfront. Welcome dinner at the hotel.

Day 2 | Stone Town Architecture Walk — Arab Fort & Rooftop Sundowner

Private Stone Town architecture walk with a private historian: the Arab Fort — a massive 17th-century coral-stone fortification — and the Sultan's Palace ruins once home to the Sultan of Zanzibar. The House of Wonders: a 19th-century palace once the tallest building in East Africa. The historic Freddie Mercury Museum dedicated to the Queen frontman born in Stone Town. Premium teas and Swahili spiced canapés on a private rooftop terrace watching the sun dip into the Indian Ocean behind old minarets.

Nungwi Peninsula

Day 3 | Aromatic Spice Estates & Northern Coast Check-In

Drive north through the island's interior for a private tour of a century-old spice estate — viewing cloves, nutmeg, cinnamon, and vanilla vines growing in their natural habitat with rich aromatic scents filling the air. Guide explains the history of the spice trade in Zanzibar, cultivation and harvesting techniques, and the cultural significance of each spice. Tasting fresh spices and learning about their uses in traditional Swahili cuisine. Afternoon: continue north to the Nungwi Peninsula. Check-in at Kilindi Zanzibar.

Note

Tides govern the best beach and water experiences

Zanzibar's beaches are tidal — swimming conditions vary significantly between high and low tide depending on which coast you're staying on. Your specialist will recommend the best-suited beach for your priorities.

Documents

Visa on arrival or e-Visa, from $50

  • Passport — Valid for at least six months beyond your arrival date, with at least two blank pages for stamps.
  • Visa — Zanzibar is part of Tanzania, so the Tanzanian eVisa or visa on arrival covers you. Single entry is US$50; US passport holders are issued the US$100 multiple-entry visa.
  • Compulsory island insurance — Since October 2024 every non-resident visitor must hold Zanzibar’s own inbound travel policy, sold only by the Zanzibar Insurance Corporation at US$44 per person for 92 days — half price for ages 3 to 17, free under 3. Your own international policy does not replace it, and the QR code is checked at the airport. We arrange this for you.
  • Dress away from the beach — Stone Town and the fishing villages are conservative. Shoulders and knees covered once you leave the resort grounds.
  • Yellow fever — A vaccination certificate is required if you are arriving from, or have transited, a country with yellow fever risk.
  • We handle the paperwork — We complete and submit your visa, eVisa or travel-authorisation applications on your behalf. You supply the documents once; we do the filing, track the approval and hold the confirmations for you.
  • Where you must appear in person — A few consulates insist on biometrics or a short interview that nobody can sit on your behalf — China requires fingerprinting for some nationalities, as do European and United States visas. We still prepare the entire file; we simply book you a single appointment and brief you beforehand so one visit settles it.
  • Before you travel — Entry rules change frequently and without much notice. Your specialist confirms the current requirements for every passport in your party at the time of booking, and checks them again before you fly.
