Overview

A Cradle of
Chinese Civilisation

Xi'an was once home to thirteen imperial Chinese dynasties, and The Ritz-Carlton brings a sense of that history into its central business district location, merging traditional elements with modern luxury throughout.

Designed by Remedios Studio, the hotel's interiors draw on architectural and design motifs from Xi'an's northern Silk Road trade routes, patterned by local artisans into shared social spaces for visitors and residents alike. The lobby's soaring ceilings, grand chandeliers and four-sided glass fireplace set an immediately serene tone, carried through to 283 rooms and suites dressed in rich plum accents and plush furnishings, each with floor-to-ceiling windows over the city. An entire floor is dedicated to The Ritz-Carlton Spa, complete with a Zen garden, private couples' suites and an indoor pool, while the rooftop bar Flair offers cocktails and Southeast Asian tapas with sweeping night views of the city.

Committed to sustainability. The Ritz-Carlton, Xi'an focuses on energy and water conservation, waste reduction and sourcing sustainable ingredients, alongside community outreach programmes across the city.

Experiences

Terracotta Warriors,
City Walls & the Muslim Quarter

Xi'an's central business district position puts the city's UNESCO-listed treasures and its buzzing food streets within easy reach.

Iconic

The Terracotta Warriors

A UNESCO World Heritage Site holding thousands of life-sized clay soldiers, guarding the tomb of China's first emperor since the third century BCE.

Heritage

The Ancient City Walls

One of the best-preserved city wall systems in China, its ramparts popular for cycling and evening walks.

Culture

The Muslim Quarter

A historic neighbourhood of narrow lanes filled with street food, spice markets and the Great Mosque of Xi'an.

Landmark

Giant Wild Goose Pagoda

A seventh-century Buddhist pagoda built to house scriptures brought back from India, a short drive from the hotel.

Wellness

The Ritz-Carlton Spa

An entire floor devoted to wellness, with a Zen garden, private couples' suites and an indoor pool.

Evenings

Flair Rooftop Bar

Cocktails, a sushi counter and Southeast Asian tapas overlooking the city skyline after dark.

Location

The Heart of
Xi'an's Business District

The Ritz-Carlton, Xi'an sits in the Gao Xin central business district, providing privileged access to the city's historic and cultural sites.

  • Located in Gao Xin District, Xi'an's central business area.
  • Convenient access to the Terracotta Warriors, Great Wild Goose Pagoda and the ancient City Walls.
  • Roughly 44 minutes, or 36.8km, from Xi'an Xianyang International Airport.
  • Close to the Muslim Quarter and the Great Mosque of Xi'an for cultural exploration.
