Overview

Where Sunrise
Meets Sunset

Known as the Island of the Sun, Pangulasian is the only El Nido Resorts island offering unobstructed views of both sunrise and sunset, its 750-metre white sand beach backed by tropical forest and Bacuit Bay's dramatic limestone cliffs.

Opened in December 2012, Pangulasian holds just 42 villas across contemporary Filipino, cutting-edge 'green' design, perched anywhere from 15 to 59 feet off the ground and reached by buggy or wooden boardwalk. Beach Villas sit directly on the sand near the resort's centre; Pool Villas each hold a 22-square-metre private pool connected to the balcony; and a cluster of four Kalaw Villas shares a secluded cove, pool and sala for intimate gatherings. The island's forest hosts macaques, monitor lizards and hornbills, while more than 20 dive sites and El Nido's famous Big and Small Lagoons sit within easy boat range — all overseen by Ten Knots Development Corporation, whose environmental and social responsibility initiatives have earned repeated industry recognition.

Part of a three-island family. Pangulasian shares Bacuit Bay with El Nido Resorts' other two properties, Miniloc Island and Lagen Island, and guests can dine or use facilities across all three during their stay.

Location

Bacuit Bay,
El Nido's Private Islands

Pangulasian sits in Bacuit Bay, reached by a scheduled flight to Lio Airport in El Nido followed by a boat transfer, part of the same archipelago as El Nido Resorts' Miniloc and Lagen islands.

  • Located in Bacuit Bay, among El Nido's 45 islands and islets.
  • Accessible via a 55-minute flight from Manila to Lio Airport, followed by a coordinated land and boat transfer.
  • Chartered flight options are available from Clark, Cebu and Bohol for a more direct route.
  • Close to Miniloc Island and Lagen Island, the other two El Nido Resorts properties in Bacuit Bay.
Fly
