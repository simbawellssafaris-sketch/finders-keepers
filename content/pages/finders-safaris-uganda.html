Uganda is where adventure and wilderness meet. Known as the 'Pearl of Africa', Uganda combines lush rainforests, snow-capped mountains, powerful rivers, and extraordinary wildlife into one remarkably diverse destination.

The country's greatest attraction is Bwindi Impenetrable Forest, where travelers trek through dense jungle to encounter endangered mountain gorillas in their natural habitat — encounters that rank among the most moving wildlife experiences anywhere in the world. Uganda also offers chimpanzee tracking in Kibale Forest, classic Big Five safaris in Murchison Falls and Queen Elizabeth National Parks, and thrilling adventures along the Nile River, including white-water rafting and boat cruises.

At {{brand}}, we believe Uganda delivers experiences unlike any other destination for travelers seeking a more adventurous and immersive side of Africa. From a single transformative hour with mountain gorillas in Bwindi, to chimpanzee tracking through Kibale's rainforest canopy, to riding the rapids of the Nile beneath Murchison Falls, every journey here is designed for those drawn to wilderness in its rawest form.

The Source of the Nile & Wildwaters

10 Days · Small Group Journey · Min 7

A highly sophisticated slow-paced itinerary focusing entirely on luxury fine dining, roaring river rapids, and high-end island wellness. Featured Accommodation

Entebbe, on the shores of Lake Victoria

Hotel No.5 — ten rooms in a walled garden, ten minutes from the airport

Hotel No.5 sits in a quiet, walled garden suburb of Entebbe, and it makes an easy case for staying here rather than pressing on to Kampala. It is ten minutes from the airport, which matters at both ends of a Uganda journey — the late arrival and the early departure — and it is intimate where the alternatives are large.

All ten rooms open onto tropical gardens and a swimming pool, with large windows, four-poster beds and a calm, boutique finish. Several are split across two levels, with a bedroom upstairs and an open-plan kitchenette and living space below. A standalone two-bedroom Residence gives families room to spread out. There is a small spa and a gym on site.

Beyond the garden walls, Entebbe offers rather more than a transit town should: the shores of Lake Victoria on foot or by boda-boda, the Uganda Wildlife Education Centre for a first look at the species you are about to track, and a boat out to Ngamba Island, where orphaned and rescued chimpanzees live under sanctuary protection.
