Overview

Meet Nairobi before you meet the wilderness.

Before heading into Kenya’s great wilderness areas, take some time to experience the city where your journey begins.

Our Nairobi City Tour introduces you to the city’s history, wildlife, culture, food and unmistakable energy.

Depending on your arrival time and the day’s schedule, your guide will accompany you through selected Nairobi experiences before you return to your hotel and prepare for the next chapter of your safari.

Experience

A little bit of Nairobi, before the safari.

Nairobi National Museum

Discover Kenya’s history, culture, art and natural heritage at the Nairobi National Museum.

Giraffe Centre

Come face-to-face with one of Kenya’s most iconic animals and learn more about giraffe conservation.

Museum of Illusions

Step into a world where perception plays tricks on you. A fun and interactive stop for photographs, curiosity and a few surprises.

Mamba Village

Where appropriate for the day’s itinerary, visit Mamba Village as another introduction to Nairobi’s attractions and local experiences.

Nairobi City

Experience Nairobi itself — its streets, neighbourhoods, architecture, energy and everyday rhythm.

Culture

Experience the Nairobi beyond the guidebook.

Nairobi isn’t just a collection of attractions. It is a living city — fast-moving, creative, colourful and full of character.

Your guide helps you experience the city beyond the usual tourist stops.

Matatu Culture

Kenya’s matatus are brightly customised public transport vehicles that have become a distinctive part of Kenyan urban culture. Your experience may include seeing and experiencing that culture where appropriate and safe.

Street Life

Experience the energy and character of Nairobi’s streets.

Local Stories

Your guide can share stories, context and insights about the places you visit.

Everyday Nairobi

See how the city actually moves, rather than experiencing it only through traditional tourist attractions.

Food

Taste a little bit of Kenya.

Food is one of the fastest ways to get to know a place.

Depending on the day’s route and available time, your city experience can include an opportunity to sample Kenyan street food and local flavours.

Local Street Snacks

The small things Nairobi eats on the move, bought where the queues are.

Traditional Bites

Kenyan staples, tried in modest portions so you can taste more than one.

Fresh Local Foods

Produce and preparations that reflect what the season is actually giving.

Street-Food Spots

Places Nairobi goes rather than places arranged for visitors.

Your guide will recommend suitable local options based on the route and experience. Specific foods and venues vary with the day, the season and what is open.

Photography

Your first day in Kenya, captured.

Your city tour isn’t just something you’ll remember. Your guide will also capture moments along the way. From your first glimpse of Nairobi to the attractions, food, streets and spontaneous moments in between, photographs become part of your arrival experience.
