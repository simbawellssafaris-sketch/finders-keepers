Overview

A Village,
Rebuilt in Reed

Okuti's mosasas — a tribal word meaning house of reeds — are a genuine departure from the classic canvas tent, built as curved reed-and-thatch structures along the Maunachira River.

Rebuilt in the style of a contemporary African village and refreshed in pale neutral tones, Okuti sits on the banks of the Maunachira River exactly where it flows through Moremi's Xakanaxa Lagoon — widely rated among the finest game-viewing areas in Africa. The camp's beehive-shaped main lodge holds a dining room and separate reading room, its doors opening onto a lower deck with a swimming pool overlooking the lagoon. Seven mosasas, including two family units, accommodate up to eighteen guests, all connected by raised wooden walkways over the grass and water below.

One of Botswana's most family-friendly camps. Okuti welcomes children from age seven, with two dedicated family mosasas — a rarity among Moremi's more adults-only camps, and a large part of what makes it a lasting favourite.

Experiences

Land, Lagoon
& Everything Between

Okuti's position on the Maunachira River gives guests the choice of open 4x4 game drives or motorboat cruises through the Delta's channels.

By Day

Game Drives

Open safari vehicles explore Moremi's varied habitats — pans, floodplains, forests and mopane woodland — with excellent, consistent year-round sightings.

On the Water

Motorboat Cruises

Motorboat trips onto the Okavango's channels and open lagoons, ideal for birdwatching and closer looks at hippo and crocodile.

Birding

The Heronries

An hour's boat ride reaches the world-famous heronries of the Godikwe and Xobega Lagoons, alive with breeding storks and herons.

Short Trips

Mokoro Excursions

Brief 30-minute mokoro trips near camp, for a gentler introduction to traditional Delta travel.

Family

Child-Friendly Activities

Okuti's family mosasas and welcoming approach to children from age seven make it one of Moremi's more accessible camps for families.

Traditional performances and storytelling around the open boma fire, under a sky thick with stars.

Location

The Beating Heart
of the Delta

Okuti sits within Moremi Game Reserve, often described as the beating heart of the Okavango Delta, where the Maunachira River flows through the Xakanaxa Lagoon.

  • Moremi Game Reserve blends floodplains, waterways, lagoons, grasslands and forest across the Delta's central and eastern reaches.
  • Black and white rhino have been reintroduced to Moremi, making it a genuine Big Five destination.
  • Night drives and off-road driving are not permitted in this part of Moremi, in keeping with the reserve's conservation rules.
  • Nearly 500 bird species have been recorded in and around the reserve, with Okuti's own count exceeding 400.
Fly

Light aircraft from Maun land at Xakanaxa airstrip, roughly a 20-minute flight.

Transfer

A short game-drive transfer of about 20 minutes brings guests from the airstrip into camp.

Combine

Okuti pairs well with Moremi's Camp Moremi or a wider Delta and Chobe circuit.

Family Safari

Built for Every Generation

With two dedicated family mosasas and a warm welcome for children from age seven, Okuti remains one of the easiest Moremi camps to bring the whole family to.
