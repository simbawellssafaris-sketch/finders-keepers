Overview

Fourteen
Suites

A maximum of thirty-two guests, on a private bend of the Mara River.

Olonana holds a private stretch of the Mara River inside the reserve, which is the whole argument for it — game comes to water, and the river does the work. There are fourteen suites, each with floor-to-ceiling glass opening onto a deck above the water, and the camp takes a maximum of thirty-two guests. Three of the suites carry a second bedroom, which is what makes it work for families travelling with children. In migration season the crossings happen on this reach of river, sometimes within sight of the deck.

Three family suites, and they go first. Only three of the fourteen suites have the additional bedroom. On family dates they are the first thing to book and the first thing to sell out.
