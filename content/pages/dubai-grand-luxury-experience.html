Itinerary

Dubai

Day 1 | Departure From Nairobi & Arrival In Dubai

Upon your arrival at Jomo Kenyatta International Airport in Nairobi, you will receive VIP handling as you check in for your international flight to the United Arab Emirates. You will board a flight to Dubai International Airport (DXB). Upon arrival, you will receive VIP meet-and-greet handling, expediting you through customs and baggage claim with ease. A private chauffeur will transfer you to Fairmont Dubai, a landmark hotel on Sheikh Zayed Road.

You will look forward to the stunning views of the Dubai skyline from your suite, with the iconic Burj Khalifa visible in the distance. If you arrive before 4:00 PM, you will be treated to a private guided tour of the area, exploring the vibrant streets of Downtown Dubai. The day culminates in a relaxed welcome drink at the hotel, with leisure time at the rooftop pool or an optional evening drive through Sheikh Zayed Road and Downtown Dubai, the city's neon lights creating a dazzling display.

Welcome Drink

Day 2 | Modern Dubai & The Icons

After breakfast, you will begin your introduction to Dubai's extraordinary skyline at the Museum of the Future, one of Dubai's most distinctive architectural and technological landmarks, with its striking torus shape and calligraphy-covered façade. In the afternoon, you will continue to Dubai Mall, allowing time for luxury shopping, an optional visit to the Dubai Aquarium & Underwater Zoo, and lunch at one of the mall's premium restaurants.

In the late afternoon, you will proceed to the Burj Khalifa for your ascent to the observation levels, with breathtaking panoramic views across Downtown Dubai, Palm Jumeirah, the Arabian Gulf, Dubai Marina and the desert beyond. As the sun sets, you will remain in Downtown for the spectacular Dubai Fountain experience, with dinner served overlooking the display.

Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner
Palm Jumeirah

Day 3 | Palm Jumeirah From The Sky

Today is dedicated to seeing Dubai from above. In the morning, you will be transferred to the helicopter terminal for a scenic flight over Dubai, with spectacular aerial views of Palm Jumeirah, Atlantis, Burj Al Arab, Dubai Marina, the Jumeirah coastline and the skyline unfolding beneath you like a living map.

In the afternoon, you will explore Palm Jumeirah by road, stopping for photographs at key viewpoints before continuing towards Atlantis. You will spend the afternoon enjoying the beach — swimming, sunbathing, jet skiing, parasailing, paddleboarding, kayaking, or simply relaxing in a private beach cabana, with the warm waters of the Arabian Gulf and the Dubai skyline as your backdrop. Dinner will be served around Palm Jumeirah or Dubai Marina.

Fairmont Dubai →
Breakfast, Dinner

Day 4 | Atlantis Adventure

Today is a full day dedicated to one of Dubai's ultimate resort experiences. You will transfer to Atlantis The Palm and begin with Aquaventure Waterpark, enjoying waterslides, rapids, pools, the beach, the lazy river and a host of aquatic attractions, including the iconic Leap of Faith, which plunges through a shark-filled lagoon.

In the afternoon, you will explore The Lost Chambers Aquarium, discovering marine life inspired by the mythology and architecture of Atlantis, including sharks, rays and thousands of colourful fish swimming through ancient ruins-themed exhibits. In the late afternoon, you will relax at the resort and enjoy the Palm coastline, with an optional luxury dinner at Atlantis or Palm Jumeirah available.

Fairmont Dubai →
Breakfast, Lunch
Old Dubai

Day 5 | Old Dubai & The Soul Of The City

Today provides a contrast to Dubai's futuristic skyline. You will begin at the Dubai Frame, an architectural landmark providing views towards both historic and modern Dubai, with stunning panoramic views from the glass bridge, Old Dubai on one side and the modern skyline on the other. In the late morning, you will continue into Old Dubai, exploring the Al Fahidi Historical District with its traditional architecture, heritage houses, art galleries and local cafés.

In the afternoon, you will take a traditional abra ride across Dubai Creek, continuing through the traditional souks — the Gold Souk, the Spice Souk, perfume shops and traditional markets, with the glittering gold, vibrant colours and bustling energy of the creek and its wooden boats. The evening offers an optional dinner at a traditional Emirati restaurant.

Fairmont Dubai →
Breakfast, Lunch
Arabian Desert

Day 6 | Dubai Desert Experience

Today you leave the skyscrapers behind. The morning is yours to relax at Fairmont Dubai, with breakfast, pool, spa, shopping or personal leisure. In the afternoon, you will depart for the Dubai desert, experiencing a 4x4 dune drive, dune bashing, sandboarding, desert photography, sunset over the dunes and a camel experience, your skilled driver navigating the towering sand dunes as the sun sets over the golden landscape.

In the evening, you will continue to a premium desert camp, experiencing a traditional Arabic welcome with Arabic coffee and dates, henna, falconry, traditional entertainment, an Arabian dinner and stargazing, the stars shining brilliantly above and the sounds of traditional music filling the air. For the luxury version, you may upgrade to a private desert safari and premium dining setup. You will return to Dubai after dinner.

Breakfast, Dinner (Desert Camp)

Day 7 | Dubai By Hop-On Hop-Off

Today is a slower sightseeing day. You will board the Dubai Hop-On Hop-Off sightseeing bus, using the route to experience Dubai's major landmarks without rushing between private transfers — Downtown Dubai, Jumeirah, Dubai Marina, Palm Jumeirah, Burj Al Arab and Jumeirah Beach, with the flexibility of exploring at your own pace.

In the afternoon, you will explore Souk Madinat Jumeirah, enjoying Arabian architecture, boutique shopping, restaurants, waterfront views and traditional-style souks with the iconic Burj Al Arab rising in the background. At sunset, you will continue to the Jumeirah coastline for sunset, followed by dinner overlooking the Burj Al Arab.

Kite Beach

Day 8 | The Ultimate Dubai Beach Day

Today is intentionally less sightseeing-heavy — the luxury beach escape within the Dubai itinerary. In the morning, you will head towards Jumeirah or Kite Beach, enjoying swimming, sunbathing, paddleboarding, kayaking, beach photography and a beachside breakfast, with the warm waters of the Arabian Gulf, soft white sand and stunning skyline views. An optional Beach Adventure Package is available, including jet ski, parasailing, banana boat, flyboarding, kayaking and paddleboarding.

In the afternoon, you will enjoy a private beach club or cabana experience, with lunch beside the Arabian Gulf. In the evening, you will transfer to Dubai Marina, walking along the promenade and enjoying dinner overlooking the yachts and skyline.

Dubai Marina

Day 9 | Dubai Marina & Sunset Yacht

This is the glamorous finale of the trip. The morning is yours to relax at Fairmont Dubai, with an optional spa treatment, rooftop pool, breakfast or shopping. In the afternoon, you will explore Dubai Marina, continuing along the coastline towards Palm Jumeirah.

At sunset, you will board a luxury yacht for a sunset cruise, passing Dubai Marina, Bluewaters Island, Ain Dubai, Palm Jumeirah, Atlantis, Burj Al Arab and the Jumeirah coastline, watching Dubai's skyline transform as the sun disappears over the Arabian Gulf. The onboard experience includes welcome drinks, canapés, music, sunset photography, a swimming stop where conditions permit, and a premium dinner option. You will return to the marina and transfer back to Fairmont Dubai.

Departure

Day 10 | Final Dubai Experience & Departure

On your final morning, you will enjoy a relaxed breakfast at Fairmont Dubai. Depending on your departure time, you may choose one last taste of Dubai's extraordinary offerings: final luxury shopping at Dubai Mall, a relaxed breakfast and waterfront walk at Dubai Marina, a final beach experience at Jumeirah, or last-minute traditional shopping at the Gold & Spice Souks.

You will then be transferred to your hotel for a complimentary spa session to relax and rejuvenate after your incredible journey, with a private guided tour of Dubai offered at your leisure if you were not taken on one on Day 1. You will then be transferred to Dubai International Airport for your connecting international flight back to Nairobi.

Journey Details

Minimum Age

Varies by activity — desert and helicopter especially

  • Dune bashing — Most operators accept children from around four years in a shared 4x4, seated with an adult.
  • Helicopter tours — Generally open to all ages, though very young children may find the noise and motion unsettling.
  • Aquaventure Waterpark — The larger slides carry height and age minimums set by the park, typically from around twelve years.
  • Yacht cruise — No formal minimum age, though young children should be confidently supervised on deck.
  • Talk to us first — Tell us the ages travelling with you and we will flag anything that needs adjusting before you book.

What To Pack

Light layers, modest wear, and one warm layer for the desert night

  • City days — Light, breathable clothing; malls and hotels are heavily air-conditioned, so bring a layer.
  • Modest dress — Shoulders and knees covered for Old Dubai, the souks and any mosque exteriors.
  • The desert camp — Days are hot but desert nights turn surprisingly cold — bring a jumper or light jacket.
  • Beach & yacht days — Swimwear, a cover-up, and reef-safe sunscreen.
  • Footwear — Comfortable walking shoes for the souks and Old Dubai's uneven lanes.

Note

Best travelled November to March, when temperatures are kindest

Dubai is enjoyable year-round, though outdoor activities such as the desert safari and beach days are considerably more comfortable between November and March, when daytime temperatures sit in the pleasant twenties.

  • Summer travel — June to September is very hot; outdoor time is best kept to early morning or evening, and hotel rates are noticeably lower.
  • Tailoring this journey — If you would like this package with more or fewer days, extended to Abu Dhabi, or arranged for a family or group, reach out and we will tailor it for you.
  • Route changes — Your guide or driver may alter the order of activities in certain circumstances, but everything set out in the itinerary will be covered.

Documents

Visa on arrival for most nationalities

  • Passport — Valid for at least six months beyond your arrival date, with at least two blank pages for stamps.
  • UAE entry — Citizens of Kenya and many other nationalities receive a visa on arrival or visa-free entry to the UAE; requirements vary by passport and can change without much notice.
  • We check it for you — Your specialist confirms the current entry requirement for every passport in your party at the time of booking, and again before you fly.
  • Onward flight — Immigration may ask to see your return or onward flight confirmation, which we provide in your travel pack.

Guaranteed Departures

Private, tailor-made departures on your dates, or join a small group

  • No group minimum — This itinerary is private and tailor-made throughout, so your dates are confirmed the moment your deposit is received.
  • Once confirmed, you travel — When your departure date is confirmed, it is guaranteed. We do not cancel or reschedule a confirmed departure for commercial reasons.
  • Flexible party size — This journey can be arranged for a solo traveller, a couple, a family or a larger private group.
  • Visas — We confirm your entry requirements ahead of travel. Please note that the granting of entry rests solely with UAE immigration authorities, and is never guaranteed by us.
  • Alcohol — Alcohol is served at hotels, licensed restaurants and on the yacht cruise, but is not sold in ordinary shops. Public intoxication is taken seriously under UAE law.
  • Dress & conduct — Dubai is cosmopolitan, but modest dress is expected in malls, mosques and Old Dubai, and public displays of affection are best kept low-key.
  • Dietary requirements — If you have strict dietary needs, please tell us at the time of booking. Halal, vegetarian and vegan options are widely available.
  • Travel insurance — We facilitate travel insurance for our clients. If you are already covered, please say so during booking so we can take it into account in your final quote.
  • Tailoring your quote — Some of our inclusions — the helicopter tour, for example — may not be what you want. Point these out and we will adjust your final offer accordingly.
  • Photography — Avoid photographing government buildings, military sites and local people without their consent.
  • What to pack — Light layers for the city, modest coverage for Old Dubai and the souks, a warm layer for the desert night, and comfortable walking shoes. Smart-casual is expected for hotel dining.
