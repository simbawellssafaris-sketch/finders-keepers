What This Is

An extra night, given rather than sold.

Lodges and camps run to seasons. There are weeks of the year when every tent is spoken for months ahead, and weeks when they are not — and in the quieter stretches, a good lodge would rather have you in the room for four nights paying for three than have the room stand empty.

That is where free nights come from. They are offers made by individual properties, on particular dates, and passed on to you in full. When one is available on the journey you are looking at, we will build it in and show you exactly what it saves.

We want to be straightforward about this: it is not always the case. Free nights are not a standing feature of every safari, and we would rather set the expectation honestly than have you arrive at a quote expecting one. What we can promise is that when an offer exists on your dates, you will hear about it.

Most common Occasional Rare — peak season

Why these months. January to May covers the green season across much of East and Southern Africa. The bush is thick and lush, the light is dramatic, newborn game is everywhere and the birding is at its best. Camps are quieter, so lodges are more generous.

July to October is the peak — the Mara crossings, the dry-season game viewing — and lodges have no empty rooms to give away. Offers in those months are rare, and we will not pretend otherwise.

How It Applies

What to expect when an offer is available.

Selected Lodges

Offers come from individual properties, not from us across the board. Which camps are running one changes through the year.

Selected Journeys

An offer only helps if the property sits naturally in your itinerary. We will not bend a route around a free night if the result is a worse journey.

Selected Dates

Most offers apply to a defined window and a minimum stay. Shifting your dates by a few days sometimes brings one into reach.

Passed On In Full

Where a lodge gives a night, it comes off your cost. We do not retain the value of an offer made to you.

Free night offers are made by individual lodges and camps and are subject to availability, minimum stay requirements, defined date windows and the property’s own terms. They are not available on all journeys, all properties or all dates, and cannot be applied retrospectively to a confirmed booking. Park, conservancy and government fees remain payable on complimentary nights unless the offer states otherwise. Your specialist will confirm precisely what is included before you book.
