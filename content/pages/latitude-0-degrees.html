Overview

Kampala’s Social
and Design Hub

Sitting among indigenous orchid-filled trees atop Makindye Hill, Latitude 0° pairs sweeping views of Lake Victoria and the city with some of Kampala’s most talked-about dining and social spaces.

Roughly 6km from Kampala's central business district and 45 minutes from Entebbe International Airport, Latitude 0° takes its name from its position almost exactly on the equator. The hotel's 47 rooms and suites make use of natural light and tree canopy views, finished with Swahili and Ugandan art, luxurious linens and bespoke artwork. Beyond the rooms, the property functions as a genuine social hub for the city — two restaurants, three bars, co-working spaces and a calendar of music, art and business events, making it as popular with Kampala residents as with travellers passing through.

Location

Makindye Hill,
Kampala

Latitude 0° sits atop Makindye Hill, roughly 6km from Kampala’s central business district and 45 minutes from Entebbe International Airport.

  • The hotel’s elevated position gives sweeping views over Kampala and Lake Victoria, a rarity for a city-centre property.
  • Makindye is a leafy, established Kampala suburb, a short drive from the city’s main commercial and diplomatic districts.
  • Latitude 0° is a popular base for business travellers, with co-working spaces and conference facilities on site.
  • The hotel is well placed as a first or last-night stop for travellers heading onward to Uganda’s national parks.
