Itinerary

Phuket

Day 1 | Arrival in Phuket — Jungle Sanctuary

VIP tarmac assistance at Phuket International Airport, then a private guide transfer into the hills of Patong. Check into Dinso Resort & Villas Phuket — a striking architectural sanctuary wrapped in dense jungle foliage, designed around the Wabi-Sabi philosophy.

Welcome dinner at the resort.

Dinso Resort & Villas Phuket
Dinner

Day 2 | Phuket Old Town & Promthep Cape Sundowner

A private architectural tour through Phuket Old Town's elegant Sino-Portuguese mansions with a private historian. Hidden temples, local markets, and the unique blend of Chinese, Portuguese, and Thai culture.

In the late afternoon, premium craft cocktails are served from a private canvas bar setup on a secluded cliffside ridge at Promthep Cape as the sun sets over the Andaman Sea.

Dinso Resort & Villas Phuket
Breakfast, Dinner

Day 3 | Krabi Island Boat Tour — Champagne Brunch on the Beach

A private luxury speedboat to Krabi's dramatic waters for a tailored island boat tour — the limestone monoliths of Koh Poda, Chicken Island, and the sandbar bridging Koh Tup.

A luxury white-linen champagne brunch is set up by the private crew at Phra Nang Cave Beach before public tours arrive — dining in absolute privacy with limestone cliffs as the backdrop.

Dinso Resort & Villas Phuket
Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Day 4 | Phuket — Spa & Southern Thai Culinary Masterclass

A morning at leisure inside the private jungle villa, followed by a premium 90-minute Thai massage at the resort's luxury spa using traditional techniques and organic oils.

The afternoon brings an exclusive hands-on Southern Thai culinary masterclass with the executive chef — learning the secrets of bold Southern Thai flavours. Dinner features the fruits of the masterclass.

Dinso Resort & Villas Phuket
Breakfast, Dinner
Cebu, Philippines

Day 5 | Flight to Cebu, Philippines

After breakfast, a regional flight to Cebu with VIP tarmac handling at Mactan-Cebu International Airport. A private Philippine chauffeur drives down the coastal highway to the beach enclave of Moalboal.

Check into Badian Beach Resort or a private luxury oceanfront villa. A relaxed dinner overlooking the Philippine Sea.

Badian Beach Resort, Moalboal
Breakfast, Dinner

Day 6 | The Sardine Run & Turtle Marine Sanctuary

Directly into the water from a private boat for one of the most spectacular marine experiences in the world: the Moalboal Sardine Run — millions of sardines in perfect swirling geometric formations just metres from shore. Wild sea turtles graze on seagrass beds alongside.

The day culminates in a lavish seafood barbecue bonfire dinner on the beach, lit by hanging lanterns, with the sound of waves throughout.

Badian Beach Resort, Moalboal
Breakfast, Dinner

Day 7 | Kawasan Falls Canyoneering

A jungle walk to tackle the world-famous Kawasan Falls Canyoneering Adventure — navigating a breathtaking limestone gorge with impossibly blue turquoise water pools, natural rock water-slides, and optional 30-foot cliff jumps.

A hot gourmet breakfast basket is served on a wooden platform overlooking the 40-metre main cascade of Kawasan Falls while refuelling.

Badian Beach Resort, Moalboal
Breakfast, Dinner

Day 8 | Oslob Whale Sharks & Osmeña Peak

A private morning boat excursion in Oslob to swim safely alongside whale sharks — the ocean's gentle giants growing up to 12 metres long, gliding through the water filter-feeding on plankton.

In the afternoon, a hike up Osmeña Peak, the highest point in Cebu, for panoramic views of the jagged green hills stretching to the horizon.

Badian Beach Resort, Moalboal
Breakfast, Dinner

Day 9 | Indigenous Cebuano Heritage & Basketry Tour

A deeply respectful cultural immersion day with indigenous Visayan master weavers, learning about the historic geometry of hand-weaving traditional puso cases and abaca storage baskets.

The day concludes with a celebratory multi-course bonfire dinner around a grand beachside bonfire with live acoustic music.

Badian Beach Resort, Moalboal
Breakfast, Dinner
Bangkok

Day 10 | Return to Bangkok — JW Marriott Opulence

A relaxed breakfast overlooking the Philippine ocean, then a road transfer to Cebu Airport for a regional flight back to Bangkok's Suvarnabhumi Airport. private guide transfer from the tarmac to the JW Marriott Hotel Bangkok.

A relaxed final afternoon in five-star city opulence — premium spa, luxury amenities, and a celebratory multi-course farewell dinner.

JW Marriott Hotel Bangkok
Breakfast, Dinner

Day 11 | Bangkok — Smooth International Departure

A relaxed luxury breakfast at the hotel. If a city tour was not taken on arrival, a private guided tour of Bangkok's historic temples, the Grand Palace, and vibrant markets.

Private transfer to Suvarnabhumi Airport for the international flight home.

Travel Kit

Duffel, sun hat, rain shell and day pack

  • Your duffel — A soft-sided {{brand}} duffel, sized for domestic flights and easier than a hard case on train platforms, ferry jetties and old-town lanes.
  • What is inside it — A wide-brim sun hat, a lightweight packable rain shell for tropical downpours, and a foldable day pack for temple mornings and market afternoons.
  • Yours to keep — The bag and everything in it is a gift, not a loan.
  • Packing around it — Slip-on shoes save time at temples, where you remove them repeatedly, and one set of clothing covering shoulders and knees is essential for religious sites.

Note

Monsoon timing varies by coast

The Andaman coast (Phuket, Krabi) and Gulf coast (Koh Samui) have opposite monsoon patterns, so the ideal season depends on which coast your itinerary includes.

  • Early starts — The best of this journey happens at dawn — before the heat, the crowds and the haze. Your guide will confirm what time you will be collected from your hotel.
  • Tailoring this journey — If you would like this package with more or fewer days, arranged for a group, a family or a solo traveller, or with add-ons, reach out and we will tailor it for you.
  • Route changes — Your guide or driver may alter the route in certain circumstances, but every destination set out in the itinerary will be covered.

Documents

Visa-free for most nationalities

  • Passport — Valid for at least six months beyond your arrival date, with at least two blank pages for stamps.
  • Thailand — A 60-day visa exemption covers UK, EU, US, Canadian and Australian passport holders. The Thailand Digital Arrival Card is compulsory and must be filed within the 72 hours before you travel.
  • Philippines — Visa-free for 30 days for the same nationalities, with compulsory eTravel registration within 72 hours before arrival and again before departure. Both are free.
  • Proof of onward travel — Both countries, and the airlines serving them, require a confirmed onward ticket inside your permitted stay.
  • Medication — Several common codeine and pseudoephedrine medicines are controlled in Thailand. Carry prescriptions in original packaging with a doctor’s letter.
  • We handle the paperwork — We complete and submit your visa, eVisa or travel-authorisation applications on your behalf. You supply the documents once; we do the filing, track the approval and hold the confirmations for you.
  • Where you must appear in person — A few consulates insist on biometrics or a short interview that nobody can sit on your behalf — China requires fingerprinting for some nationalities, as do European and United States visas. We still prepare the entire file; we simply book you a single appointment and brief you beforehand so one visit settles it.
  • Before you travel — Entry rules change frequently and without much notice. Your specialist confirms the current requirements for every passport in your party at the time of booking, and checks them again before you fly.
