Day by Day

Cocoon Collection Maldives

Day 1 | Nairobi → Maldives — Seaplane Arrival

Depart Nairobi aboard Emirates or Qatar Airways, connecting smoothly into Velana International Airport in Malé. Resort representatives greet you at arrivals and escort you to the seaplane terminal for one of the most extraordinary arrival experiences in travel: a flight above the Maldives atoll, looking down at scattered islands, turquoise lagoons, coral reef systems, and overwater villas as the Indian Ocean stretches to every horizon. The seaplane descends toward Cocoon Collection Maldives and touches down on the lagoon. Private buggy transfers carry you to your overwater or beachfront villa. Tropical welcome drinks, cold towels, and the sound of the ocean beneath your feet. As the sun sets over the Indian Ocean, an elegant dinner is served at the resort's oceanfront restaurant with live acoustic music accompanying the meal.

Day 2 | Floating Breakfast, Lagoon & Beach Club

The morning begins with the floating breakfast — one of the Maldives' most celebrated rituals. Tropical fruits, pastries, fresh juices, and champagne arranged on a floating tray in your private villa pool while the turquoise ocean fills every view. The rest of the morning is for the lagoon itself: swimming directly from your villa steps, kayaking through calm, crystal-clear water above the reef, or simply lying on the private sun deck letting the warmth work its way through. By afternoon, the beach club is calling — tropical cocktails, infinity pools, stylish oceanfront loungers, and the social energy that makes Cocoon different from a purely secluded resort. Sunset photography on the beach as the sky transforms. Dinner by the water as the stars emerge above the atoll.

Day 3 | Island Hopping & Authentic Maldivian Culture

A speedboat carries you through the atoll's turquoise channels to the local Maldivian islands nearby — a world that exists completely apart from the resort experience. A traditional fishing village where the boats are painted in blues and greens and the harbour is busy from before dawn. A local market with fresh tuna, dried fish, woven palm frond baskets, and handcrafted jewellery. A mosque with a coral stone facade built centuries before the first resort appeared in the atoll. A local restaurant where a fresh seafood lunch is served overlooking the ocean without a five-star markup attached to it. Your guide explains the Maldivian fishing traditions, the dhoni boat-building techniques, and the coconut-based culture that has sustained these islands for over a thousand years. Return to the resort in the late afternoon. Dinner at the main restaurant.

Day 4 | Luxury Yacht Sunset Cruise

Board a luxury catamaran or yacht in the early afternoon for a sailing experience through the surrounding atoll. The vessel moves between untouched sandbanks, past coral formations visible through the clear water, and into the open Indian Ocean beyond the atoll edge. Snorkelling stops above vibrant coral gardens along the way — tropical fish, reef sharks patrolling the deeper sections, a sea turtle moving unhurriedly through the coral overhead. Luxury dining and tropical cocktails are served on board from mid-afternoon. As the sun begins its descent, the yacht positions itself in open water for the sunset — the sky moving through shades of gold, orange, and deep crimson, the ocean reflecting it all. Spinner dolphins regularly appear alongside the bow at this hour, leaping in the bow wave as the light fails. Return to the resort as the stars emerge. Dinner at the beach restaurant.

Day 5 | Coral Reef Exploration & Marine Adventure

A professional marine guide leads a guided snorkelling experience across the atoll's most vibrant reef sections — coral formations in every shape and colour, clouds of tropical reef fish, the more elusive species that require knowing exactly where to look. Manta rays are frequently encountered at certain reef cleaning stations, hovering almost motionless as cleaner fish work over their wings. Hawksbill turtles move through the coral with an unhurried dignity that makes them feel ancient, which they are. Optional scuba diving can be arranged for those who want to go deeper — the reef wall descends steeply and the deeper sections hold species rarely seen in shallower water. The afternoon is at leisure. Water sports — jet skis, paddleboarding, parasailing — or simply floating in the warm lagoon above the reef until the light changes. Dinner at the overwater restaurant.

Day 6 | Overwater Spa & Wellness Day

A day given entirely to rest and rejuvenation. The resort's spa is positioned partially overwater, its treatment rooms opening to views of the lagoon and the sound of the ocean below. Your included treatment offers a choice of aromatherapy massage, Balinese deep tissue, hot stone therapy, flower bath, reflexology, or a hydrotherapy session — each drawing on Asian and tropical wellness traditions and using island-sourced ingredients. After the spa, the afternoon is for the villa — the private deck, the lagoon below, the floating cocktails, the particular pleasure of doing absolutely nothing in an exceptionally beautiful location. A gourmet fine dining dinner beneath the stars concludes the day.

Day 7 | Beach Club & Private Dining

Cocoon's beach club is one of the resort's most distinctive features — a stylish oceanfront space with infinity pools, signature cocktails, tropical DJ sets, and the kind of social energy that feels more like a luxury beach destination than a traditional Maldives resort. The morning and afternoon are for the beach club at whatever pace suits. Private beach dining can be arranged for this evening for couples wishing to close the day with a personalised experience — a table set at the water's edge, lanterns placed in a semicircle in the sand, floral arrangements, a personalised menu prepared by the resort's chef, and the Indian Ocean as the only backdrop. Optional professional photography sessions can also be arranged to capture the evening.

Day 8 | Ocean Adventures & Water Sports

For those who want adrenaline — jet skis across the lagoon's open water, parasailing high above the atoll with the resort's island visible below, paddleboarding through the calm channels between coral heads, and kayaking to the atoll's further reaches. Optional underwater dining experiences and deep-sea fishing excursions can be arranged for those who want to add them. For those who prefer a slower pace — one final snorkelling session, a morning reading on the villa deck, or another treatment at the spa. The afternoon is at leisure. A floating cocktail served inside the villa pool as the sun begins to descend. Dinner at the beach restaurant as waves arrive softly along the shoreline.

Day 9 | Romantic Farewell Dinner & Final Evening

The final full day in paradise is intentionally slow. Whatever remains on your wish list from the nine days — a last swim in the lagoon, one more morning watching the light shift across the overwater villas, a final set at the beach club — today is for finishing it. As evening falls, an extravagant farewell dinner is set up along the shoreline: lantern pathways leading to a table set with white linen, candles, flower petals, and a personalised menu. Live music plays softly. The waves arrive at intervals, barely disturbing the sand at the edge of the table. The night sky above the atoll — completely free of light pollution — is extraordinary. The dinner takes as long as it takes.

Day 10 | Departure to Nairobi

A final tropical breakfast at the resort. Private speedboat or seaplane transfers are timed precisely to your international departure schedule — you arrive at Velana International Airport, Malé, with time to spare, without rushing. The connecting flights back to Nairobi carry you away from the Indian Ocean. Nine nights on one of the world's most beautiful island resorts conclude.

Documents

Free 30-day visa on arrival

  • Passport — Valid for at least six months beyond your arrival date, with at least two blank pages for stamps.
  • Visa — A free 30-day visa on arrival is granted to every nationality. There is nothing to apply for in advance.
  • Traveller Declaration — Every arriving and departing passenger files the Maldives Traveller Declaration online within 96 hours of travel. It is free, and the QR code is scanned at immigration.
  • What cannot come in — Alcohol, pork products and religious items for non-Islamic worship cannot be imported. Alcohol is served freely on the resort islands but not brought in — bags are screened at Velána and anything found is held until you depart.
  • We handle the paperwork — We complete and submit your visa, eVisa or travel-authorisation applications on your behalf. You supply the documents once; we do the filing, track the approval and hold the confirmations for you.
  • Where you must appear in person — A few consulates insist on biometrics or a short interview that nobody can sit on your behalf — China requires fingerprinting for some nationalities, as do European and United States visas. We still prepare the entire file; we simply book you a single appointment and brief you beforehand so one visit settles it.
  • Before you travel — Entry rules change frequently and without much notice. Your specialist confirms the current requirements for every passport in your party at the time of booking, and checks them again before you fly.
