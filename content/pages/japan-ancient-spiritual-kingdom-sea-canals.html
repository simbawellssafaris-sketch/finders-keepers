Itinerary

Mount Koya (Koyasan), Japan

Day 1 | Nairobi → Osaka → Mount Koya — Temple Arrival by Helicopter

VIP handling at Nairobi's Jomo Kenyatta Airport, flight to Kansai International Airport (KIX) in Osaka. VIP tarmac reception, then board a private helicopter for a spectacular flight over the urban landscape into the misty cedar peaks of Mount Koya (Koyasan).

Check into Soji-in Temple Lodging — VIP Imperial Room. The serene atmosphere of a working Buddhist temple, cedar trees, the sound of monks' chanting. Private guided tour of the temple grounds. Welcome dinner.

Soji-in Temple Lodging, Mount Koya
Dinner
Okunoin Cemetery, Mount Koya

Day 2 | The 10,000 Lanterns of Okunoin

Explore Okunoin — Japan's largest and most sacred cemetery — with a private monk guide. Walking past 200,000 moss-covered stone stupas under giant cedar trees to the Torodo Hall, illuminated by 10,000 permanently burning brass lanterns.

The mausoleum of Kobo Daishi, founder of Shingon Buddhism. A traditional vegetarian Shojin Ryori dinner at the temple.

Soji-in Temple Lodging, Mount Koya
Breakfast, Dinner
Kumano Kodo

Day 3 | Dawn Monk Chanting & Kumano Kodo Pilgrimage

Rising before dawn to witness the evocative deep-voiced morning chanting and fire rituals of the Koyasan monks.

Afternoon on a private crowd-free section of the ancient Kumano Kodo Pilgrimage Trail — stone staircases cutting through forest that has been walked by pilgrims for a thousand years. Hot local plum wine and Shojin Ryori delicacies from a private stone platform overlooking a raked rock garden at sunset.

Soji-in Temple Lodging, Mount Koya
Breakfast, Dinner
Naoshima Art Island, Seto Inland Sea

Day 4 | Helicopter to Naoshima Art Island

Private helicopter flight west across the Seto Inland Sea to the world-famous Naoshima Art Island — miniature pine-topped islands and turquoise water from above.

Check into the ultra-exclusive Benesse House Oval Suite, designed by Tadao Ando. The Seto Inland Sea visible through every window. Afternoon exploring the island's coastline. Gourmet dinner.

Benesse House Oval, Naoshima
Breakfast, Dinner
Naoshima

Day 5 | The Yellow Pumpkin & Chichu Underground Museum

Walk to Yayoi Kusama's iconic Yellow Pumpkin on a concrete pier over the sea — the striking contrast of bright yellow against the blue. A private crowd-free guided tour of the Chichu Art Museum, built completely underground to let natural light illuminate masterworks by Claude Monet.

A lavish champagne brunch prepared by a private chef on a high promontory overlooking the glassy Seto Inland Sea. Return for a relaxed evening.

Benesse House Oval, Naoshima
Breakfast, Dinner
Dorokyo Gorge

Day 6 | Hidden Gem — Dorokyo Emerald Gorge

A private boat excursion to the Dorokyo Gorge — navigating a quiet emerald-green river channel cutting between sheer vertical cliff walls covered in moss and ancient mountain azaleas.

The towering cliffs and lush vegetation creating one of Japan's most dramatically beautiful hidden landscapes. Swimming in the clear water. Return for gourmet dinner.

Benesse House Oval, Naoshima
Breakfast, Dinner
Honmura, Naoshima

Day 7 | Art House Project — Living Architecture Walk

The historic fishing village of Honmura — a private architectural walk through old timber houses transformed internally into modern structural art installations. Each house features a different artist's vision fused with traditional Japanese architecture.

Your guide explains the Art House Project and the artists involved. Return for a relaxed evening.

Benesse House Oval, Naoshima
Breakfast, Dinner

Day 8 | Private Yacht — Seto Inland Sea & Island Bonfire

A full day cruising the quiet channels of the Seto Inland Sea on a private luxury yacht — navigating past miniature pine-topped islands, the extraordinary seascape of the inner sea.

A celebratory multi-course bonfire dinner on the island's edge under a brilliant blanket of stars.

Benesse House Oval, Naoshima
Breakfast, Dinner

Day 9 | Exclusive Washi Paper & Indigo Dyeing Heritage Tour

An island cooperative with indigenous master paper-makers and indigo dyers — filtering mulberry bark into heavy Washi paper sheets, traditional organic indigo fermentation vat techniques.

A private performance of historic folk storytelling. Celebratory farewell dinner at Benesse House paired with vintage sake.

Benesse House Oval, Naoshima
Breakfast, Dinner

Day 10 | Helicopter to Osaka — Departure

A relaxed luxury breakfast over the sea channels. Private helicopter back to Kansai International Airport. Complementary spa session in Osaka. Optional city tour. International flight back to Nairobi.

Note

Some experiences are highly seasonal

Cherry blossoms (late March–early April) and autumn colours (November) are the most popular — and most crowded — times to visit. Your itinerary is timed to make the most of seasonal highlights where included.

  • Early starts — The best of this journey happens at dawn — before the heat, the crowds and the haze. Your guide will confirm what time you will be collected from your hotel.
  • Tailoring this journey — If you would like this package with more or fewer days, arranged for a group, a family or a solo traveller, or with add-ons, reach out and we will tailor it for you.
  • Route changes — Your guide or driver may alter the route in certain circumstances, but every destination set out in the itinerary will be covered.

Documents

Visa-free for most nationalities

  • Passport — Valid for at least six months beyond your arrival date, with at least two blank pages for stamps.
  • Visa exemption — Japan waives the short-stay visa for 74 countries and regions, including the UK, the EU, the US, Canada and Australia, generally for 90 days.
  • Visit Japan Web — Filing immigration and customs in advance through Visit Japan Web produces two QR codes and takes you through the fast lanes at Narita and Haneda. It is free and saves a long queue.
  • Medication is taken seriously — Japan prohibits several medicines sold over the counter elsewhere, including many pseudoephedrine decongestants and codeine painkillers. Bringing them in, even in hand luggage, is a criminal matter, and larger quantities of prescription drugs need a Yakkan Shoumei import certificate arranged in advance.
  • We handle the paperwork — We complete and submit your visa, eVisa or travel-authorisation applications on your behalf. You supply the documents once; we do the filing, track the approval and hold the confirmations for you.
  • Where you must appear in person — A few consulates insist on biometrics or a short interview that nobody can sit on your behalf — China requires fingerprinting for some nationalities, as do European and United States visas. We still prepare the entire file; we simply book you a single appointment and brief you beforehand so one visit settles it.
  • Before you travel — Entry rules change frequently and without much notice. Your specialist confirms the current requirements for every passport in your party at the time of booking, and checks them again before you fly.
