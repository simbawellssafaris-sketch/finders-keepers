Overview

Post-Colonial
Elegance

Built into the medina walls of Essaouira, Heure Bleue Palais restores a 200-year-old riad into an intimate 33-room hotel, one of the more exclusive addresses in this seaside town.

Rooms are centred around an impressive inner courtyard filled with palm trees and banana plants, candlelit at dinner for a genuinely magical setting away from the summer heat. Decor throughout leans dark and stately — African, Portuguese, colonial English and Oriental styles run through different rooms, with heavy carvings, rich fabrics and stained-glass windows recalling the building's 19th-century origins, when Essaouira had only recently changed its name from Mogador. A rooftop terrace with panoramic views over the port and old town holds the hotel's real showpiece: a large heated pool, alongside lounges, a cinema room and a colonial-style English bar.

A member of Relais & Châteaux. Heure Bleue Palais belongs to the prestigious Relais & Châteaux association, and its Moroccan cookery school is run by resident chef Ahmed Handour.

Location

Essaouira's
Historic Medina

Heure Bleue Palais sits on a cobbled street within Essaouira's UNESCO-listed medina, a short walk from the port, souks and main square.

  • Within the old city walls, a two-minute walk from the souks and Essaouira's main square.
  • A five-minute walk to Souk Lagzhal and a ten-minute walk to Tagharte beach.
  • The Supratours bus terminal, the main link to Marrakech, is a five-minute walk away.
  • Essaouira's medina is a UNESCO World Heritage Site, known for its ramparts and windswept Atlantic setting.
Drive

Essaouira is roughly a 2.5 to 3-hour drive from Marrakech.

Bus

Supratours coaches run regularly between Marrakech and Essaouira, a short walk from the hotel.

Fly

Essaouira-Mogador Airport offers limited connections, with Marrakech's airport the more common gateway.
