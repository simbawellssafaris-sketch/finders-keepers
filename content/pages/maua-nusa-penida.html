Overview

An Island That
Stayed Unknown Too Long

MĀUA Nusa Penida takes its name from a sacred andesite carving found on a New Zealand farm — Māori for togetherness — a fitting name for a resort built to blend into the wild island beneath it.

Nusa Penida remained an underdiscovered destination for years despite sitting just a 30-minute ferry from Sanur, and MĀUA's design makes the most of that raw beauty: eco-friendly architecture with minimal visible concrete, most structures camouflaged directly into the hilltop landscape above Gamat Bay. Twenty-four rooms and 26 private pool villas mix wabi-sabi textures and natural materials with the refined architectural lines of Le Corbusier, most offering multi-tiered, bird's-nest views layered across forest canopy, sculpted cliffs and open ocean horizon. West-facing villas take in the resort's real signature — sunsets that turn rambutan red, dragon-fruit pink and papaya orange over Gamat Bay and Mount Agung in the distance.

An adults-only resort. MĀUA Nusa Penida operates as an adults-only property, making it a considered choice for couples and quiet escapes rather than family travel.

Location

Gamat Bay,
Nusa Penida

MĀUA sits on a secluded hilltop above Gamat Bay on Nusa Penida, an island roughly 30 minutes by ferry from Sanur, Bali, and 20 minutes' drive from the Nusa Penida ferry terminal.

  • Located on a hilltop above Gamat Bay, in Sakti, Nusa Penida, Klungkung Regency.
  • Roughly 30 minutes by ferry from Sanur, plus a 20-minute drive from the ferry terminal.
  • Nusa Penida's roads are narrow and winding — a car with a driver is recommended over self-drive scooters.
  • Close to Crystal Bay, Kelingking Beach and Angel's Billabong, the island's best-known landmarks.
Fly

Ngurah Rai International Airport (Denpasar) connects to Sanur Harbour, roughly 45 minutes by car.

Ferry

A 30-minute speedboat crossing from Sanur reaches Nusa Penida's ferry terminal.

Arrive

A 20-minute drive, or a scenic helicopter transfer from the airport, completes the journey to the resort.
