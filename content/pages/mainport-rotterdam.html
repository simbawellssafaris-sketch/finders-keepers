Overview

Two Hundred
and Fifteen

Two hundred and fifteen rooms on the river, each floor themed to a different continent.

The Mainport opened in 2013 on the Maas riverbank in the middle of Rotterdam, a city that rebuilt itself from nothing after 1940 and has been the most architecturally adventurous place in the Netherlands ever since. Feran Thomassen’s interiors match that: dark stone floors, black accent walls, and each guest floor given over to a different continent through its wallpapers. The 215 rooms have Illy machines, whirlpool baths and mirror-integrated bathroom televisions; the higher categories add a private spa bath and a Finnish sauna inside the room. Downstairs, Spa Heaven runs a panorama sauna over the skyline, a hamam and a Turkish steam room.

The in-room sauna is real. Selected categories include a private Finnish sauna and spa bath in the room itself. If that is the stay you want, it has to be booked by category.
