Overview

The Next-Generation
Safari Villa

Set just behind Sasakwa, Singita’s flagship hill, Milele takes exclusivity to another level as a five-suite, ten-guest villa with its own dedicated staff and total privacy.

The Singita Grumeti Reserve spans over 350,000 acres of private land bordering the Serengeti — restored since 2003 by the non-profit Grumeti Fund from a severely degraded landscape into thriving wilderness, including the 2019 reintroduction of nine critically endangered Eastern black rhino. Milele's five individually designed suites, each inspired by an iconic Serengeti species (Journey, Pride, Coalition, Dazzle and Crash), sit within an open, expansive villa built for large groups of friends or families, complete with a 10-person cinema room, fully equipped gym, and a wine cellar to rival any restaurant.

Location

Sasakwa Hill,
Grumeti Reserve

Singita Milele sits on the slopes of Sasakwa Hill within the private Singita Grumeti Reserve, on the western edge of the Serengeti ecosystem.

  • The Grumeti Fund, a not-for-profit partner, carries out wildlife conservation and community development across the 350,000-acre reserve.
  • Milele sits alongside Singita’s other Sasakwa-area villas, Kilima and Serengeti House, each offering a different scale of private-use stay.
  • From Kilimanjaro, the reserve is roughly an hour and ten minutes away by light aircraft, landing on Singita’s own private airstrip.
  • All profits from Singita Grumeti are returned to the reserve and the communities who live alongside it.
Fly
