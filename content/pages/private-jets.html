How This Works

On request. Quoted for you. Never off a price list.

We do not publish private jet prices, and we would be misleading you if we did. Charter cost depends on the aircraft, the routing, the number of flying days, the airstrips involved and when you travel. Two parties on the same itinerary a month apart can be quoted very differently.

So this is a request service. You tell us where you want to go and roughly when, we price the aircraft against that specific plan, and you see one figure covering the flying and the safari together before you commit to anything.

By request only

Private jets are arranged when you ask for them. They are not attached to a published itinerary or offered as a standing upgrade.

Quoted, not listed

Every charter is priced against your actual routing and dates. There is no rate card, and no figure until we have built the plan.

One arrangement

The aircraft and the safari are quoted and run together — the flying, the camps, the guiding and the ground, from one team.

Why It Changes The Journey

What you are actually buying is days.

The case for a private aircraft is rarely the aircraft. It is what happens to the itinerary once scheduled flights stop setting the shape of it.

—

Days back

A multi-country East African circuit that takes fourteen days on scheduled flights often runs to eight or nine on a charter. The difference is almost entirely waiting.

—

Camps that were out of reach

The most remote concessions are served by airstrip and nothing else. A charter puts them inside a normal-length trip.

—

Your hours, not the airline’s

Depart after the morning game drive rather than before it. Flight times are set around the safari instead of the other way round.

—

Luggage that fits

Light safari aircraft carry strict soft-bag limits. On a private charter the allowance is set by the aircraft we choose for you.
