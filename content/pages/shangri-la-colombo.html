Overview

Colombo's
Seafront Landmark

A five-star high-rise hotel on the Galle Face seafront, in the heart of Colombo's business and shopping district.

Shangri-La Colombo rises above the Galle Face Promenade, with 541 rooms, suites and serviced apartments looking out over the Indian Ocean and Beira Lake. Every room has a marble bathroom with a separate bath and walk-in shower, and the Horizon Club on the 32nd floor offers a private lounge with sweeping views. Seven restaurants and bars, Chi, The Spa, a pool and direct access to the One Galle Face Mall make it a comfortable, fully-equipped base at the start or end of a Sri Lanka journey.
