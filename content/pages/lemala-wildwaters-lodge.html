Overview

A Private Island
Mid-Rapids

Nestled in lush rainforest on a private island mid-stream in the mighty Nile, Wildwaters Lodge is only reached by boat — one of the most singular settings of any lodge in East Africa.

Kalagala Island covers roughly six hectares of thick riverine forest, perched on granite rock some 25km downstream from Jinja, the source of the Nile and Uganda's self-styled adventure capital. The lodge's ten timber-floored suites are linked to the restaurant and bar by elevated wooden walkways winding through the forest canopy, each opening onto a private deck with a freestanding bath and wash basin carved from local pink granite. The main lodge sits on a solid rock peninsula amid the rapids themselves, close enough that their thunder is a constant, calming presence throughout a stay.

Location

Mid-Stream in
the River Nile

Lemala Wildwaters Lodge sits on Kalagala Island, roughly 25km downstream from Jinja and 1.5 hours from Kampala, accessible only by boat.

  • The lodge is reached by road to a riverside parking area, followed by a boat crossing to the island itself.
  • The property is not wheelchair accessible, and children aged 12 and over are welcome but remain the responsibility of accompanying adults given the surrounding rapids.
  • The island has its own helipad, making helicopter transfers possible in addition to the standard road-and-boat route.
  • Wildwaters Lodge is part of the Lemala portfolio, sister to a collection of luxury safari camps in Tanzania.
Drive

Roughly 1.5 hours from Kampala or 45 minutes from Jinja by road to the riverside parking area.

A short boat crossing from the mainland completes the journey onto the private island itself.
