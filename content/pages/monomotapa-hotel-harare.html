Overview

Standing Against
Harare's Skyline

Standing proudly against the Harare skyline, the Monomotapa's unique concave shape reflects the splendour of the ancient Munhumutapa Kingdom from which it takes its name.

Located 100 metres from Harare's main financial and corporate district, right on the doorstep of the city's Central Park and Gardens, the Monomotapa Hotel has been a fixture of the capital for decades. Its 243 rooms — a mix of Standard Twins, King Leisure Rooms and suites — all overlook Harare Gardens, giving guests a rare pocket of green in the heart of the commercial district. Extensive conference and banqueting facilities across seven dedicated suites make it one of Harare's most established venues for business gatherings, weddings and receptions.
