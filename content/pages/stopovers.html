Introduction

More than a road to the safari.

Some of Kenya’s most memorable moments happen between destinations.

A dramatic view across the Great Rift Valley. A quiet moment watching birds around Lake Nakuru. Cycling through the landscapes of Hell’s Gate. A peaceful stop beside Lake Naivasha.

Our road journeys are designed to make room for these experiences. Depending on your itinerary, route and available time, selected stopovers can become part of your journey to or from safari.

How Stopovers Work

Woven into the route, not bolted on.

Stopovers are incorporated according to the route, timing and safari itinerary. Some are naturally positioned on the way to your destination, while others work especially well on the return journey.

The Stopovers

Four places worth slowing down for.

On The Way To Safari · Also Possible On Return

Great Rift Valley Viewpoint

Pause above one of Kenya’s most spectacular landscapes and take in the sweeping views of the Great Rift Valley.

The viewpoint provides a natural break from the road and an opportunity to appreciate the scale of the landscape before continuing toward the safari.

Best experienced on the outbound journey

  • Scenic viewing
  • Photography
  • Short refreshment stop
  • Enjoying the landscape

Primarily an outbound stop, though it can also be included on the return journey where the route and schedule allow.

Outbound Or Return Stopover

Lake Nakuru

Lake Nakuru offers a chance to slow down and experience one of Kenya’s remarkable lakes before continuing your journey.

Depending on your route and itinerary, Lake Nakuru can be incorporated as a stopover while travelling toward your safari or as part of the return journey.

Boat Riding

Where available and appropriate, enjoy time on the lake from the water.

Bird Watching

Take time to observe the diverse birdlife around the lake.

Scenic Experience

Enjoy the surrounding landscapes and wildlife environment.

Photography

Capture the lake, landscapes and moments along the journey.

Return Journey Experience

Hell's Gate

On the return journey, the road can take you through the dramatic landscapes of Hell’s Gate — a place where the journey becomes an adventure of its own.

Bicycle Riding

Explore sections of the park by bicycle where permitted and appropriate.

Geothermal Experience

Visit the geothermal area and experience the naturally heated environment.

Geothermal Pool

Include time around the geothermal pool where the selected experience and current conditions allow.

Scenic Landscapes

Experience the dramatic cliffs, gorges and Rift Valley scenery.

Wildlife

Look out for wildlife along the route.

Activities are subject to park regulations, operating conditions, weather and availability. Access to specific activities or pools cannot be guaranteed.

Return Journey Stopover

Lake Naivasha

Slow down beside Lake Naivasha as the safari journey begins to wind its way back toward Nairobi.

The lake provides a peaceful contrast to the intensity of the safari, giving travellers a chance to enjoy the landscape and reflect on the experiences of the journey.

  • Lakeside scenery
  • Wildlife viewing
  • Bird watching
  • Photography
  • Relaxed stopover
