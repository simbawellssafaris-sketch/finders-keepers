Overview

Where the Rainforest
Meets the Tea Fields

Set within a working tea plantation bordering Nyungwe National Park, One&Only Nyungwe House pairs contemporary One&Only luxury with access to one of Africa's oldest and largest mountain rainforests.

Nyungwe National Park, declared in 2012, protects a vast montane rainforest home to roughly 20% of Africa's primate species — including chimpanzees, Ruwenzori colobus and owl-faced monkeys — alongside orchids, butterflies and hundreds of bird species. One&Only Nyungwe House's 22 suites and villas sit on a hillside within the neighbouring Gisakura tea estate, each with a private terrace looking out over the plantation toward the forested mountains beyond. The resort's homestead building houses all-inclusive dining, a spa using Africology products, and a helipad connecting guests directly to Kigali in around 35 minutes.

Experiences

Chimpanzees,
Tea and Canopy Walks

Activities centre on Nyungwe's rainforest and the resort's own tea plantation, arranged through African Parks and the resort's guiding team.

Signature

Chimpanzee Discovery Trek

Track one of Nyungwe's habituated chimpanzee groups deep into the forest with expert local trackers. Permits are arranged via African Parks.

Wildlife

Colobus Monkey Trekking

Follow troops of black-and-white Ruwenzori colobus through Nyungwe’s forest trails, often numbering in the hundreds.

Adventure

Canopy Walkway

Cross Nyungwe's suspended canopy walkway for a rare, elevated view over the rainforest treetops.

On Site

Tea Plantation Tour

Pick tea leaves on the resort's own working plantation, then taste the finished Gisakura black tea over a guided tasting.

Learn the history of Imigongo — traditional Rwandan cow-dung artwork — and create a piece of your own, or visit the nearby village of Banda.

Location

On the Edge of
Nyungwe Forest

One&Only Nyungwe House sits in Gisakura, southwestern Rwanda, bordering Nyungwe National Park — one of Africa's oldest and largest mountain rainforests.

  • Nyungwe is home to roughly 20% of all primate species found in Africa, including chimpanzees and several colobus monkey species.
  • The resort sits within its own working tea plantation, one of Rwanda's most scenic and productive tea-growing regions.
  • Chimpanzee trekking permits must be arranged in advance through African Parks and are separate from the resort rate.
  • The minimum age for chimpanzee treks is 15 years old, in line with national park regulations.
