Itinerary

Hanoi

Day 1 | Nairobi → Hanoi — Welcome to Vietnam

Depart Nairobi aboard Qatar Airways or Emirates, connecting into Hanoi's Noi Bai International Airport. A private luxury transfer escorts you into the city through French colonial streets to Peridot Grand Luxury Boutique Hotel. After settling in, a luxury Vietnamese welcome dinner beside Hoan Kiem Lake is followed by a guided night walk through the Old Quarter — lantern-lit alleys, coffee houses that open at midnight, and street food stalls that have occupied the same corner for generations. Hanoi after dark is one of Southeast Asia's most seductive cities.

Peridot Grand Luxury Boutique Hotel, Hanoi
Dinner

Day 2 | Hanoi — Cultural Landmarks & Hidden Cafés

A guided day through Hanoi's iconic cultural sites — Ho Chi Minh Mausoleum and its elegant lakeside surrounds, the Temple of Literature founded in 1070 as Vietnam's first university, Tran Quoc Pagoda on its West Lake peninsula, and the French colonial quarter's gracious boulevards. Afternoon brings the Oasis Café — one of Hanoi's most celebrated hidden aesthetic cafés, with tropical architecture and artistic interiors that feel entirely removed from the city's energy outside. The evening brings a traditional water puppet performance and dinner at a luxury rooftop restaurant overlooking the Hoan Kiem Lake skyline.

Peridot Grand Luxury Boutique Hotel, Hanoi
Breakfast, Dinner
Sapa

Day 3 | Luxury Sleeper Train to Sapa — The Night Journey

Board Vietnam's luxury sleeper train at Hanoi station for the overnight journey north toward Sapa. The train departs in the early evening, and as darkness falls, the urban landscape gives way to rice fields, river valleys, and the first limestone hills of the north. Private sleeping cabins are equipped with comfortable beds, climate control, and large windows. An onboard dining car serves Vietnamese dishes as the countryside passes in darkness. By morning, the train is climbing into the highlands — the valleys below filling with mist, the terraced hillsides of Lao Cai Province appearing in the grey light before sunrise.

Luxury Sleeper Train — Hanoi to Lao Cai
Breakfast, Dinner onboard

Day 4 | Sapa — Rice Terraces & Ethnic Village Walks

Arrive in Lao Cai and transfer to Sapa — a highland town at 1,500 metres where the cool air, the terraced hillsides, and the daily presence of Hmong and Dao villagers in traditional dress create an atmosphere unlike anywhere else in Vietnam. A guided walk through Muong Hoa Valley descends through rice terraces in every shade of green, past water buffaloes working the paddy fields, through Hmong villages where indigo-dyed fabrics hang to dry and children follow visitors with curiosity. The terraces themselves — some over 700 years old, carved by hand from the mountainside — are a feat of engineering and beauty that is simply impossible to fully photograph. Check into Topas Ecolodge, its stone bungalows perched on a ridge with panoramic views of the valley.

Topas Ecolodge, Sapa
Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner
Ha Giang

Day 5 | Ha Giang — Northern Wilderness Arrival

Travel north to Ha Giang by private luxury vehicle — a journey through increasingly dramatic terrain as the road leaves the Sapa highlands and enters the truly remote north. Villages become smaller and more widely spaced. The limestone peaks grow taller and more vertical. By the time P'apiu Resort Ha Giang appears, you are in a landscape that feels genuinely untouched. Check in and spend the afternoon absorbing the valley views from the resort's terraced gardens. Dinner as the highland darkness falls and the stars above the mountains emerge without any light pollution to diminish them.

P'apiu Resort Ha Giang
Breakfast, Dinner

Day 6 | Ha Giang Loop — Ma Pi Leng & Dong Van Plateau

The Ha Giang Loop: a circuit of mountain road that passes through some of the most dramatic scenery in Southeast Asia. The morning route takes you across the Dong Van Karst Plateau — a UNESCO Global Geopark where 450-million-year-old limestone formations create a landscape that looks more lunar than terrestrial — then along Ma Pi Leng Pass, where the road clings to a cliff face above a gorge that drops hundreds of metres to the Nho Que River below. The river at the bottom is a colour between jade and turquoise that appears to have been invented for a painting. Hmong villages appear at intervals. Photography stops are frequent because every few hundred metres reveals a view that demands to be recorded. Return to the resort for dinner.

P'apiu Resort Ha Giang
Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner
Cao Bang

Day 7 | Cao Bang — Ban Gioc Waterfalls

Travel to Cao Bang Province for the Ban Gioc Waterfalls — among the largest waterfalls on the Asian continent, dropping in three tiers through emerald-green landscape beside the Vietnam-China border. The approach by bamboo raft across the river toward the base of the falls is one of Vietnam's most memorable experiences: the sound building as you draw closer, the mist rising above the treeline before the falls themselves come into view. Nearby, Nguom Ngao Cave offers a limestone cave system of extraordinary scale and formation. Overnight at Saigon Ban Gioc Resort where the sound of the falls is audible from the rooms.

Saigon Ban Gioc Resort, Cao Bang
Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner
Ninh Binh

Day 8 | Ninh Binh — The Land Ha Long Bay Forgot

Travel south to Ninh Binh — the province known as "Ha Long Bay on land" for its limestone karsts rising directly from flooded rice fields. A luxury sampan boat ride through Trang An Landscape Complex (a UNESCO World Heritage Site) drifts through cave systems and karst valleys on water that reflects the mountains above. At Hang Mua viewpoint, a climb of 500 steps rewards with a panorama of the entire Ninh Binh landscape — rice fields, rivers, and limestone peaks stretching to every horizon. Ancient Hoa Lu, the tenth-century capital of Vietnam, adds historical depth to a day already full of visual spectacle. Check into Tam Coc Garden Resort for dinner.

Tam Coc Garden Resort, Ninh Binh
Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner
Ha Long Bay

Day 9 | Ha Long Bay — Overnight Luxury Cruise

Transfer to Ha Long Bay and board the Indochine Premium Cruise. As the vessel moves between Ha Long Bay's 1,969 limestone islands, kayaks are lowered for exploration of hidden lagoons and sea caves. Sunset is marked by cocktails on the upper deck as the limestone towers turn golden. A gourmet seafood dinner follows on board, and the night is spent at anchor in absolute silence — the bay completely still, the stars reflected in the water between the islands.

Indochine Premium Cruise, Ha Long Bay
Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner
Quang Binh

Day 10 | Quang Binh — Paradise Cave & Jungle Rivers

Disembark the cruise and board a domestic flight to Quang Binh — Vietnam's cave province, home to Son Doong (the world's largest cave) and its more accessible neighbour, Paradise Cave. Paradise Cave stretches for 31 kilometres underground, its main tourist section revealing chambers of extraordinary scale — some 150 metres high, filled with stalactites that took millions of years to form. The lighting has been carefully designed to reveal the cave's drama without diminishing its natural character. The Chay River runs through the jungle around the cave, and an afternoon kayaking through the forest on emerald water provides a complete contrast to the limestone interior. Check into Chay Lap Farmstay for dinner.

Chay Lap Farmstay & Resort, Quang Binh
Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner
Da Nang

Day 11 | Da Nang — Golden Bridge & Beachfront Luxury

Fly to Da Nang, check into TIA Wellness Resort on the beachfront, and head to Ba Na Hills and the Golden Bridge. Two giant stone hands hold the pedestrian bridge 1,400 metres above sea level, above the mist that regularly obscures the mountains below. The cable car journey to reach it — among the world's longest — passes through cloud layers, the coast disappearing below and reappearing at intervals. Return to the resort for dinner by the South China Sea.

TIA Wellness Resort Da Nang
Breakfast, Dinner
Hoi An

Day 12 | Hoi An — Ancient Town, Basket Boats & Lantern River

Transfer to Hoi An. Morning coconut basket boat rides through the Thu Bon River's palm forest. Afternoon in the Ancient Town — lantern-making workshops, the sixteenth-century merchant houses, the Japanese Covered Bridge, tailoring shops that can produce garments within a day. As evening falls, the city transforms. Lanterns in every colour illuminate every street. A lantern boat drifts along the Thu Bon as the floating candles pass beneath. Check into Anantara Hoi An Resort for a final riverside dinner.

Anantara Hoi An Resort
Breakfast, Dinner
Quy Nhon

Day 13 | Quy Nhon — Vietnam's Hidden Coastal Gem

Travel south to Quy Nhon — a city that has remained largely unknown to international tourism because it lacks a UNESCO listing or a famous landmark, which is precisely what makes it extraordinary. The beaches are long and pale and calm, backed by granite headlands. The fishing villages are authentic in a way that is increasingly rare on Vietnam's coast. Eo Gio — a wind-carved coastal formation of dramatic rock faces and narrow inlets — provides one of the day's photographic highlights. Check into Anya Premier Hotel Quy Nhon for dinner on the beachfront terrace.

Anya Premier Hotel Quy Nhon
Breakfast, Dinner
Ho Chi Minh City, Mekong Delta, Mui Ne

Day 14 | Ho Chi Minh City, Mekong Delta & Mui Ne

Fly to Ho Chi Minh City — Vietnam's largest and most energetic metropolis. Check into The Reverie Saigon, then a Mekong Delta excursion takes you south of the city by private boat through the floating markets, coconut candy factories, and river villages of one of Southeast Asia's most extraordinary water landscapes. Return to the city in the late afternoon, then transfer to Mui Ne — the coastal resort town of sand dunes and kite surfers. The red dunes glow as the sun sets behind them. Check into Anantara Mui Ne Resort for dinner on the beach.

Anantara Mui Ne Resort
Breakfast, Dinner
Phu Quoc Island

Day 15 | Phu Quoc Island — Tropical Finale & Departure

Board a domestic flight to Phu Quoc — Vietnam's island paradise in the Gulf of Thailand. White sand beaches, coral gardens, tropical beach clubs, and the Sun World cable car crossing three kilometres of open ocean create a finale that is deliberately slow and beautiful. Check into Salinda Resort Phu Quoc Island. The afternoon is for swimming and resting. As your international departure time approaches, private transfers escort you to Phu Quoc International Airport for your connecting flights back to Nairobi. Fifteen days of Vietnam — from its northern mountains to its southern islands — conclude.

Phu Quoc → Ho Chi Minh City → Nairobi

Note

North-to-south itineraries cross several climate zones

Vietnam spans a long north–south geography, so weather can differ significantly between Hanoi, Hue/Hoi An and Ho Chi Minh City on the same dates — layers are recommended.

  • Early starts — The best of this journey happens at dawn — before the heat, the crowds and the haze. Your guide will confirm what time you will be collected from your hotel.
  • Tailoring this journey — If you would like this package with more or fewer days, arranged for a group, a family or a solo traveller, or with add-ons, reach out and we will tailor it for you.
  • Route changes — Your guide or driver may alter the route in certain circumstances, but every destination set out in the itinerary will be covered.

Documents

e-Visa required for most nationalities

  • Passport — Valid for at least six months beyond your arrival date, with at least two blank pages for stamps.
  • eVisa — Vietnam issues a 90-day multiple-entry eVisa to all nationalities through the national portal. Allow three to five working days. Some European nationals also qualify for a separate short visa exemption.
  • Your eVisa names its gates — The eVisa specifies the exact port of entry and exit. Arriving or departing through a different airport or land crossing invalidates it — which is why we finalise your routing before applying.
  • Digital arrival card — Since 15 April 2026 all international arrivals at the main airports must submit Vietnam’s digital arrival card online before landing.
  • We handle the paperwork — We complete and submit your visa, eVisa or travel-authorisation applications on your behalf. You supply the documents once; we do the filing, track the approval and hold the confirmations for you.
  • Where you must appear in person — A few consulates insist on biometrics or a short interview that nobody can sit on your behalf — China requires fingerprinting for some nationalities, as do European and United States visas. We still prepare the entire file; we simply book you a single appointment and brief you beforehand so one visit settles it.
  • Before you travel — Entry rules change frequently and without much notice. Your specialist confirms the current requirements for every passport in your party at the time of booking, and checks them again before you fly.
