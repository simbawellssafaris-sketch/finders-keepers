Overview

Don’t just wait. Explore Kenya.

We’re dedicated to crafting out-of-this-world safari experiences, even when you only have a few hours between flights. With our Layover Safaris, your time between connections becomes an opportunity to experience Kenya — from wildlife roaming beneath Nairobi’s skyline to the dramatic landscapes of the Great Rift Valley and the legendary plains of the Maasai Mara.

Every experience is thoughtfully designed around your available time, your flight schedule and the way you want to experience Kenya. From airport pickup and transfers to flights, helicopter journeys, park entry and activities, we coordinate the experience around your connecting flight so you can explore with confidence.
