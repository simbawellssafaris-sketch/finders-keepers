OverviewThis is not merely a wildlife safari. This is an immersion into one of Africa's greatest conservation triumphs — the return of the critically endangered black rhino to the wilds of northern Kenya. Over ten unforgettable days, journey from the community-run Sera Rhino Sanctuary to the sacred forested peaks of the Mathews Range, and the golden plains of Samburu National Reserve. Track rhino on foot, ride horseback beside giraffe, and sit with Samburu elders beside ancient singing wells. Every charter flight, every transfer, every signature experience is wrapped into one seamless, all-inclusive price.

Itinerary

Nairobi

Day 1 | Arrival in Nairobi & City Exploration

Upon your arrival at Jomo Kenyatta International Airport (NBO), you will be met by your private safari guide and transferred to the JW Marriott Hotel Nairobi. You will look forward to the stunning views of the city and the peaceful serenity of your luxury suite. If your international flight lands before 4:00 PM, you will embark on a guided Nairobi city tour. You will visit the Nairobi National Museum, which offers an overview of Kenya's history, culture, and art, with exhibits on archaeology, paleontology, and ethnography. You will also see iconic landmarks including the Parliament Buildings, the Kenyatta International Convention Centre (KICC), and the historic railway station. If you arrive after 4:00 PM, you will enjoy a relaxed evening at the hotel, with the city tour shifting to Day 10.

Sera Wildlife Conservancy

Day 2 | Fly to Sera Wildlife Conservancy & Evening Sundowner

After breakfast, you will be transferred to Wilson Airport (WIL) for a morning private charter or scheduled flight to the remote Sera airstrip in northern Kenya. You will look forward to the spectacular aerial views of the changing landscapes, from the green highlands to the arid northern frontier. Upon arrival, you will be transferred to Saruni Rhino in time for a gourmet lunch. You will look forward to the stunning views of the vast northern landscape and the rugged beauty of the conservancy. In the late afternoon, you will set out on a game drive ending at a spectacular rocky outcrop. You will look forward to scenic sundowners as the sun sets over the vast northern frontier, with the golden light illuminating the landscape.

Day 3 | On-Foot Black Rhino Tracking & Bonfire Dinner

Today, you will embark on a thrilling, highly exclusive on-foot tracking safari accompanied by expert rangers to locate the critically endangered Black Rhinos in the sanctuary. You will look forward to the adrenaline of walking through the bush, with the rangers reading the tracks and signs of the rhinos. You will learn about the successful conservation efforts that have brought the black rhino back from the brink of extinction. You will look forward to the moment you come face-to-face with these magnificent creatures, experiencing the profound connection that only a walking safari can provide. In the evening, you will gather around a roaring open-air campfire in the dry riverbed for a luxury bonfire dinner under a canopy of stars.

Namunyak, Mathews Range

Day 4 | Morning Walking Safari & Transfer to Namunyak

Today, you will wake up early for an interpretive walking safari to track smaller wildlife footprints and learn about desert flora. You will look forward to the intimate connection with nature, discovering the smaller details of the ecosystem that are often missed from a vehicle. Your guide will explain the unique adaptations of the desert wildlife. After breakfast, you will take a scenic mid-morning drive through community lands to the spectacular Mathews Range in Namunyak. You will look forward to the dramatic change in landscape, with the lush green hills of the Mathews Range rising from the arid plains. You will check into Sarara Camp. You will look forward to the stunning views of the mountains and the peaceful serenity of the camp.

Day 5 | The Samburu Singing Wells & Bush Breakfast

Today, you will visit the famous local Singing Wells, where Samburu warriors chant unique family songs to call their cattle down to drink from deep hand-dug wells. You will look forward to the mesmerizing sight of the warriors singing and the cattle gathering, with the ancient traditions of the Samburu people on full display. Your guide will explain the cultural significance of the wells and the deep connection between the Samburu and their cattle. You will enjoy a chef-prepared bush breakfast set up under the shade of massive acacia trees, allowing you to dine in the wild surrounded by the sounds of the bush.

Day 6 | Horseback Wilderness Ride & Reteti Elephant Sanctuary

Today, you will explore the Mathews Range foothills on a morning horseback safari, riding alongside giraffes and zebras. You will look forward to the unique perspective of viewing wildlife from horseback, with the animals accepting you as part of the landscape. Your guide will lead you through the scenic trails, sharing insights into the local flora and fauna. In the afternoon, you will visit the Reteti Elephant Sanctuary. You will look forward to seeing the rescue and rehabilitation of orphaned elephant calves by local community keepers, learning about the inspiring conservation efforts that have made this sanctuary a success.

Samburu National Reserve

Day 7 | Drive to Samburu National Reserve & River Game Drive

After breakfast, you will take a morning game-drive transfer south into the iconic Samburu National Reserve. You will look forward to the scenic drive through the changing landscapes, with wildlife sightings along the way. You will check into Elephant Bedroom Camp, a luxury tented camp along the Ewaso Nyiro River. You will look forward to the stunning views of the river and the lush riverine vegetation. In the afternoon, you will head out on a game drive to spot the “Samburu Special Five”—the Reticulated Giraffe, Grevy's Zebra, Somali Ostrich, Beisa Oryx, and Gerenuk. You will look forward to sightings of these unique species, found only in this region.

Day 8 | Deep Wildlife Tracking & Luxury Lunch in the Wild

Today, you will set out on a comprehensive full-day game drive tracking Samburu's famous leopard and lion populations. You will look forward to the thrill of tracking these apex predators, with the dramatic landscapes of Samburu providing a stunning backdrop. Your expert guide will position your vehicle for the best viewing opportunities. You will indulge in a private luxury lunch set up directly in the wild along a shaded bend of the river, with the sounds of the bush surrounding you.

Day 9 | Maasai & Samburu Cultural Immersion Day

Today, you will dedicate your final full day to a deep cultural immersion at a traditional local village. You will look forward to learning about the pastoralist way of life, beadwork art, ancient fire-making techniques, and traditional dances. You will be welcomed by the village elders, who will share stories and insights into their traditions and their coexistence with wildlife. You will also learn about the warriors' traditions, the role of cattle in their culture, and their spiritual connection to the land. The day concludes with a final, celebratory multi-course farewell dinner, paired with vintage wines.

Day 10 | Fly to Nairobi & Flexible City Tour / Departure

On your final morning, you will enjoy a game drive before breakfast, then check out and transfer to the Samburu airstrip for your morning flight back to Wilson Airport in Nairobi. Upon arrival, if the city tour was missed on Day 1, you will now embark on a guided tour of Nairobi, visiting historical sites or local markets. You will then be transferred to your city hotel, where you have been booked for a complimentary spa session to relax and rejuvenate after your incredible journey. You will enjoy this time at your leisure as you unwind from the expedition. Journey Details

Minimum Age

Varies by camp, activity and national park

  • Camps and lodges — Most safari camps accept children from six years, with shared game-drive vehicles usually from eight to twelve. Many properties will take younger children on a privately booked vehicle.
  • Walking safaris — Guided walking safaris carry a firm minimum of fifteen or sixteen years across most reserves. This is set by the licensing authority, not the camp, and is never waived.
  • Gorilla and chimpanzee trekking — Uganda and Rwanda both enforce a strict minimum of fifteen years. It is a national park regulation and there are no exceptions.
  • Hot-air balloon safaris — Operators generally require children to be at least seven, and tall enough to brace unaided in the basket on landing.
  • Family and exclusive-use camps — A number of our camps run dedicated family units, child-minding and tailored activity programmes, and will relax their usual age limit for a sole-use booking.
  • Talk to us first — Age limits vary from property to property and from activity to activity. Speak to our experts before you book, and we will pre-arrange the details with each lodge around the ages of the children travelling with you.

Safari Duffel

Duffel, safari hat, vest and hooded jumper

  • Your duffel — A soft-sided {{brand}} duffel, sized for the 15 kg limit on the light aircraft that move you between camps. Rigid suitcases cannot be carried on those flights at all.
  • What is inside it — A branded {{brand}} safari hat, a safari vest, and a hooded jumper for cold dawn starts and balloon mornings.
  • Yours to keep — The bag and everything in it is a gift, not a loan. Nothing is returned at the end of your journey.
  • Packing around it — Soft luggage, neutral colours, and layers. Laundry is included at nearly every camp, which is why 15 kg goes further than it sounds.

Note

Best timed around wildlife movement patterns

Kenya's game viewing is exceptional year-round, though the Great Migration river crossings in the Maasai Mara peak between July and October. Malaria prophylaxis is recommended for all safari areas — please consult your doctor before travel.

  • Early starts — Safaris begin very early in the morning. Your guide will confirm what time you will be collected from your camp or lodge.
  • Tailoring this journey — If you would like this package with more or fewer days, arranged for a group, a family or a solo traveller, or with add-ons, reach out and we will tailor it for you.
  • Route changes — Your guide or driver may alter the route in certain circumstances, but every destination set out in the itinerary will be covered.

Documents

Kenya eTA required before arrival

  • Passport — Valid for at least six months beyond your arrival date, with at least two blank pages for stamps.
  • Kenya eTA — Kenya replaced its tourist visa with an Electronic Travel Authorisation in January 2024. Apply online before you fly — approval normally lands within 48 to 72 hours, but allow a week.
  • African passport holders — Citizens of almost every African country now enter Kenya without an eTA. East African Community nationals — Burundi, DR Congo, Rwanda, South Sudan, Tanzania and Uganda — may stay up to six months; other African nationals up to two.
  • Yellow fever — A vaccination certificate is required if you are arriving from, or have transited, a country with yellow fever risk.
  • We file it for you — Whether you are flying in from overseas or travelling from elsewhere on the continent, we complete and submit your visa application on your behalf. You hand us your documents once. We do the filing, chase the approval, and carry the confirmations in your travel pack so nothing rests on you remembering a reference number at a counter.
  • Most of Africa is open to you — The great majority of our African destinations are now either visa-free or issue a visa on arrival, so an embassy queue is rarely in your future at all. Where a visa on arrival does apply, we prepare and lodge the paperwork ahead of you — you reach the desk with a finished file rather than a blank form and a biro.
  • Before you travel — Entry rules change frequently and without much notice. Your specialist confirms the current requirements for every passport in your party at the time of booking, and checks them again before you fly.

Guaranteed Departures

Private, tailor-made departures on your dates, or join a small group

  • Once confirmed, you travel — When your departure date is confirmed, it is guaranteed. We do not cancel, merge or reschedule a confirmed departure for commercial reasons, however the final numbers fall.
  • What confirms a departure — A departure becomes confirmed once the agreed number of travellers has been reached and paid for in line with the agreed payment schedule. Places held without payment do not count towards it.
  • If the minimum is not reached — Where a group departure has not reached its agreed numbers, we tell you early rather than late, and you choose: move to the next available date, continue at a revised small-group rate, or take a full refund of everything you have paid.
  • Solo journeys — Solo and tailor-made itineraries carry no group minimum at all. Your dates are confirmed the moment your deposit is received.
  • Visas — We prepare and submit your visa application on your behalf. Please note that the granting of a visa rests solely with the immigration authority of the country concerned, and is never guaranteed by us.
  • Medication — We arrange malaria prophylaxis, together with anti-nausea and anti-anxiety tablets, for your journey. Any prescription medicine should be confirmed with your own doctor before you travel — they remain the final authority on what is suitable for you.
  • Africa is not always hot — Despite the stereotype, Africa moves through distinct seasons and mornings at altitude can be genuinely cold. A hooded jumper is provided for cold conditions, and for every guest joining a hot-air balloon safari.
  • Dietary requirements — If you have strict dietary needs, please tell us at the time of booking. This includes vegans, guests in recovery, non-drinkers, and anyone following a prescription from their doctor.
  • Travel insurance — We facilitate travel insurance for our clients. If you are already covered, please say so during booking so we can take it into account in your final quote.
  • Tailoring your quote — Some of our inclusions — hot-air balloon safaris, for example — may not be what you want. Point these out and we will adjust your final offer accordingly.
  • The Maasai massage & spa recovery — Guests travelling the safari circuit by road rather than by air should expect long and tiring days. The rough terrain of the jungle gives the vehicle a constant jolting ride, known affectionately as the Maasai massage. A spa session is provided at the end of your safari to recover.
  • What to pack — Neutral colours are the rule on safari: khaki, olive, stone and brown. Avoid black and dark blue, which draw tsetse flies, and leave camouflage at home entirely — it is reserved for the military and illegal for civilians in several African countries. Bring layers for cold dawn game drives, long sleeves and trousers for dusk when the mosquitoes are out, closed walking shoes, a sun hat and sunglasses. Lodge dinners are smart-casual; nothing formal is needed.
  • Your safari duffel & kit — The duffel bag is used when flying into the parks, where luggage is limited to 15 kg per person. A branded {{brand}} safari hat and safari vest are also provided.
