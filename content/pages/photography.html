The Idea

Your journey, captured along the way.

Some of the best memories aren’t planned.

They’re the laugh at a roadside stop. The excitement when you see your first elephant. The view from a mountain lookout. The moment your family gathers together after a long day on safari.

That’s why, on our road safari journeys, your guide travels with a camera and captures these moments as they happen.

From Nairobi and throughout your safari, photographs become part of the journey — without you having to arrange a separate photographer or pay an additional fee.

Who It’s For

Made for the road.

Our photography experience is especially designed for guests travelling by road. Because your guide accompanies you throughout the journey, there are more opportunities to capture the little moments between destinations — from the first morning in Nairobi to the final drive back.
