Introduction

One journey. Different starting points.

Every traveller begins their journey from somewhere different. Your departure city, travel dates, preferred airline, cabin class and connecting routes can all influence the cost and availability of international airfare.

That is why we do not attach one international flight price to every African safari. Instead, we personalise the air ticketing around your journey and provide a separate quotation based on your actual departure point and travel requirements.

And once you arrive in Africa, your safari continues seamlessly — with regional and internal flights required by your itinerary included in the package, unless otherwise stated.

Why

Why is your international flight quoted separately?

An international flight from New York is not priced the same way as a flight from London, Dubai, Frankfurt or Mumbai. Airfare changes according to:

Airfare Varies By

  • Departure city
  • Travel dates
  • Airline
  • Availability
  • Cabin class

And Also By

  • Number of connections
  • Baggage requirements
  • Flexibility of the ticket
  • Seasonal demand

Rather than building an arbitrary flight allowance into every African safari, we prefer to quote your international journey based on your actual travel requirements. That means your safari price reflects the experience itself, while your international flight is tailored specifically to you.
