How This Works

Woven into an itinerary, or arranged on request.

Helicopter flying sits inside our safaris in three different ways, and it matters which one applies to which experience below. One flight comes with a Zimbabwe itinerary automatically. Some camp-to-camp transfers are already costed into your quote. Everything else on this page is arranged on request, priced against the specific flight, and added to whichever itinerary it best fits.

Always included

Victoria Falls by air comes standard with every Zimbabwe itinerary. There is nothing to add and nothing to request.

Already in your quote

Where an itinerary calls for a helicopter between two camps, that leg is priced in from the first quote — not billed again later.

On request

The remaining flights — Mount Kenya’s lakes, the far north, the migration by air — are arranged and quoted against your dates and route.
