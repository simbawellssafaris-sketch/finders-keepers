Day by Day

Your Itinerary

Singapore

Day 1 | Arrival — Marina Bay Introduction

Private transfer from Changi Airport to Hotel Mi Rochor, beside Bugis and a short ride from Marina Bay. After check-in and rest, the evening begins with a gentle introduction to Singapore: the Cloud Forest and Flower Dome at Gardens by the Bay showcase indoor waterfalls and tropical ecosystems unlike anything else on earth. As night falls, the illuminated Supertree Grove light show creates a cinematic first impression. A peaceful stroll across the Marina Bay waterfront closes the evening — the skyline reflected in the water, the city alive with light.

Hotel Mi Rochor
Dinner

Day 2 | Marina Bay Sands SkyPark & Singapore Flyer

Breakfast overlooking the city before ascending to Marina Bay Sands SkyPark for panoramic views across Singapore's futuristic skyline — one of the most extraordinary urban vistas in the world. Merlion Park for photography, then luxury shopping and café experiences around Marina Bay. At sunset, board the Singapore Flyer for breathtaking aerial views as the city transforms into a glowing spectacle of lights and waterfront beauty.

Hotel Mi Rochor
Breakfast

Day 3 | Sentosa Sensoryscape & Cable Car Sky Dining

Head to Sentosa Sensoryscape: immersive gardens, scented walkways, glowing art installations, and interactive sensory environments that create an entirely futuristic escape from the city. Later, board the Singapore Cable Car for panoramic views across Mount Faber and Sentosa Island before the Cable Car Sky Dining experience — a luxurious multi-course dinner served inside a moving cable car cabin as the sun sets over the harbour. One of Singapore's most distinctive and romantic dining experiences.

Hotel Mi Rochor
Breakfast, Dinner

Day 4 | Adventure & Skyline Thrills — SkyHelix, Mega Zip & iFly

Morning at SkyHelix Sentosa: an open-air rotating gondola rising above the island with uninterrupted 360-degree ocean views and drinks in hand. The afternoon shifts into adventure: the Giant Swing, Mega Zip across Sentosa's coastline, or indoor skydiving at iFly Singapore — one of the world's largest indoor skydiving wind tunnels. The evening concludes with beach relaxation and oceanfront dining beside Sentosa's luxury coastline as the sun disappears into the Andaman Sea.

Hotel Mi Rochor
Breakfast

Day 5 | Singapore Zoo, River Wonders, Bird Paradise & Night Safari

A full day of Singapore's most extraordinary wildlife experiences. Singapore Zoo's open rainforest habitats allow close encounters with exotic animals in natural environments — orangutans move through trees overhead, white tigers pace open plains, and African penguins occupy a dedicated island. River Wonders follows with giant pandas and immersive tropical river exhibits. After dinner, the legendary Night Safari: tram rides through illuminated jungle landscapes as nocturnal wildlife moves freely in the darkness around you. An experience that is genuinely unlike anything else in the world.

Hotel Mi Rochor
Breakfast

Day 6 | Wellness & Luxury Spa Escape — Joya Onsen & Marina Barrage

A deliberately slower day. Morning at Joya Onsen Cafe: traditional Japanese-style stone baths, Zen gardens, aromatherapy steam rooms, and wellness treatments creating deep physical and emotional relaxation. Luxury massages and spa treatments in the afternoon. As the sun drops, head to Marina Barrage for a rooftop sunset picnic overlooking Singapore's skyline — a peaceful, unhurried conclusion to a genuinely restorative day.

Hotel Mi Rochor
Breakfast

Day 7 | ArtScience Museum, National Gallery & Cultural Districts

ArtScience Museum's immersive digital exhibitions combine art, science, technology and culture in installations that feel genuinely otherworldly. Continue to the National Gallery and National Museum before the afternoon takes you into the colourful streets of Haji Lane and Little India. Boutique cafés, vibrant murals, street photography, temples, and the layered multicultural cuisine of Singapore make this one of the richest cultural days of the entire journey.

Hotel Mi Rochor
Breakfast

Day 8 | Southern Islands Escape — Lazarus, St John's & Kusu Island

Escape the city entirely through a private island-hopping adventure across Singapore's Southern Islands. Lazarus Island: wide white sand beaches and calm turquoise water that feels nothing like the city just minutes away. St John's Island and Kusu Island follow — peaceful lagoons, picnic experiences, swimming and kayaking in tropical scenery that most visitors to Singapore never see. The sunset ferry ride back provides extraordinary views of the city emerging from the horizon.

Hotel Mi Rochor
Breakfast

Day 9 | Royal Albatross Luxury Sunset Cruise

Board the Royal Albatross — Singapore's iconic luxury tall ship — for an evening of sunset ocean views, gourmet multi-course dining, live music, and champagne service as the ship sails Singapore's southern waters. The skyline appears on the horizon as the sun drops, then transforms into a sea of lights as night falls. Ocean breezes, the sound of the water against the hull, and one of the most beautiful dining experiences in Southeast Asia. An evening that stays with you long after you leave Singapore.

Hotel Mi Rochor
Breakfast, Dinner

Day 10 | Jewel Changi & Final Leisure Day

Jewel Changi Airport's Rain Vortex indoor waterfall — the world's tallest indoor waterfall at 40 metres — cascades through a vast glass-and-steel dome surrounded by a canopy forest and luxury retail. Explore the indoor forest walks, luxury boutiques, and cafés at a completely unhurried pace. The remainder of the day is yours: optional shopping, photography sessions, a final café stop in Haji Lane, or simply returning to the hotel for a final evening of Singapore's extraordinary rooftop atmosphere.

Hotel Mi Rochor
Breakfast

Day 11 | Departure from Singapore

Final breakfast and relaxation before private transfer to Changi International Airport for your return flight to Nairobi. Changi is consistently voted the world's best airport — allow extra time simply to wander. You leave Singapore having experienced its finest moments: the skyline, the wildlife, the islands, the culture, and the singular luxury of a city that does everything extraordinarily well.

Documents

Visa-free for most nationalities

  • Passport — Valid for at least six months beyond your arrival date, with at least two blank pages for stamps.
  • Visa — Visa-free for up to 90 days for UK, EU and US passport holders, and 30 days for most other nationalities.
  • SG Arrival Card — File the SG Arrival Card online within three days of arrival. It is free on the official ICA site — the many paid look-alike sites are not official.
  • What Singapore does not allow in — Chewing gum, vaping devices and e-cigarettes are banned outright and possession carries heavy fines. There is no duty-free tobacco allowance either: every cigarette must be declared and duty paid.
  • We handle the paperwork — We complete and submit your visa, eVisa or travel-authorisation applications on your behalf. You supply the documents once; we do the filing, track the approval and hold the confirmations for you.
  • Where you must appear in person — A few consulates insist on biometrics or a short interview that nobody can sit on your behalf — China requires fingerprinting for some nationalities, as do European and United States visas. We still prepare the entire file; we simply book you a single appointment and brief you beforehand so one visit settles it.
  • Before you travel — Entry rules change frequently and without much notice. Your specialist confirms the current requirements for every passport in your party at the time of booking, and checks them again before you fly.

Guaranteed Departures

Private, tailor-made departures on your dates

  • Once confirmed, you travel — When your departure date is confirmed, it is guaranteed. We do not cancel, merge or reschedule a confirmed departure for commercial reasons, however the final numbers fall.
  • What confirms a departure — A departure becomes confirmed once the agreed number of travellers has been reached and paid for in line with the agreed payment schedule. Places held without payment do not count towards it.
  • If the minimum is not reached — Where a group departure has not reached its agreed numbers, we tell you early rather than late, and you choose: move to the next available date, continue at a revised small-group rate, or take a full refund of everything you have paid.
  • Solo journeys — Solo and tailor-made itineraries carry no group minimum at all. Your dates are confirmed the moment your deposit is received.
