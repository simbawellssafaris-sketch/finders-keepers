Itinerary

Cairo

Day 1 | Arrival in Cairo & Cosmopolitan Grandeur

VIP tarmac handling at Cairo International Airport, then a private guide transfer to Four Seasons Hotel Cairo at Nile Plaza. If arriving before 4:00 PM, a private guided tour of Islamic Cairo, the Mosque of Muhammad Ali, and the bustling Khan el-Khalili bazaar.

The day culminates in an exclusive welcome dinner overlooking the illuminated Nile, where you meet your private Egyptologist guide and pilot for a briefing on the journey ahead.

Four Seasons Hotel Cairo at Nile Plaza
Dinner

Day 2 | Saqqara Necropolis Exploration

A dedicated day exploring the Saqqara Necropolis, standing before the Step Pyramid of Djoser — the oldest colossal stone building in history, designed by Imhotep over 4,600 years ago. Private access into newly excavated noble tombs not open to the general public, their wall paintings preserved in extraordinary condition.

In the late afternoon, single malt whiskies and premium wines are served on a private platform overlooking the desert dunes of Saqqara, with the Step Pyramid silhouetted against the setting sun.

Four Seasons Hotel Cairo at Nile Plaza
Breakfast, Dinner
Siwa Oasis

Day 3 | Charter Flight to the Secret Oasis

A private charter flight west to the remote Siwa Oasis near the Libyan border, with dramatic aerial views of the Sahara's shifting dunes before descending into lush green palm groves. You check into the ultra-luxury eco-lodge Adrère Amellal, carved entirely from salt-rock and clay.

The afternoon is yours to relax in profound isolation — a refreshing dip in a natural spring or a walk through the palm groves. Dinner at the lodge features traditional Siwan cuisine.

Adrère Amellal
Breakfast, Dinner

Day 4 | Shali Fortress & Cleo's Spring

You explore the ancient ruins of the Shali Fortress, a mud-brick citadel built from kershef, eroded over centuries into hauntingly beautiful sculptural forms. You also visit the Oracle Temple of Alexander the Great, where he was declared the son of Amun in 331 BC.

A luxury breakfast spread with fresh olives, dates and Siwan bread is set up on the shores of a crystal-clear, hyper-saline desert lake, where you float effortlessly in the mineral-rich waters surrounded by the Great Sand Sea.

Adrère Amellal
Breakfast, Dinner
Luxor

Day 5 | Desert Flight to Upper Egypt

Rising before dawn, you board a hot air balloon or private scenic charter to glide silently over the olive groves, palm canopies, and shimmering salt pans of the Siwa basin. After landing, a celebratory champagne toast and gourmet brunch follow.

In the afternoon, a private charter flight carries you directly across the country to Luxor. Upon arrival, you board your luxury Nile cruise ship, the Sonesta Star Goddess, welcomed with chilled champagne.

Sonesta Star Goddess (Nile Cruise)
Breakfast, Dinner

Day 6 | The West Bank & Luxor Temple

You cross to the West Bank to explore the Temple of Hatshepsut, carved into the cliffs of Deir el-Bahri, and the towering Colossi of Memnon. You also visit the Valley of the Kings, exploring the royal tombs of the New Kingdom pharaohs.

In the evening, you visit Luxor Temple, dedicated to Amun-Ra, beautifully illuminated at night with its grand columns glowing in golden light.

Sonesta Star Goddess (Nile Cruise)
Breakfast, Dinner
Kom Ombo

Day 7 | Sailing to Kom Ombo

You relax on the sun deck as the ship sails upriver, watching the timeless landscape of feluccas, water buffalo, and farmers along the banks. In the afternoon, you dock for a private tour of the Kom Ombo Temple, dedicated to Sobek and Haroeris.

You explore the fascinating collection of mummified crocodiles and the ancient medical instruments depicted on the temple walls. Dinner is served as you continue sailing toward Aswan.

Sonesta Star Goddess (Nile Cruise)
Breakfast, Dinner
Aswan

Day 8 | The Cataracts of Aswan

You arrive in Aswan, set against the dramatic backdrop of the Nile cataracts. In the afternoon, a private sailing cruise on a traditional felucca around Kitchener's Island, gliding past lush botanical gardens.

The day culminates in a majestic open-air dinner around a central fire pit on a panoramic stone terrace overlooking the First Cataract of the Nile, the sound of rushing water and brilliant desert stars above.

Sonesta Star Goddess (Nile Cruise)
Breakfast, Dinner

Day 9 | Exclusive Siwan Amazigh Heritage Tour

A deeply respectful cultural immersion day spent privately with elders of the indigenous Amazigh (Siwan) heritage, learning about the Siwi Berber language and traditional silver jewelry crafting passed down through generations of desert nomads.

The highlight is a private performance of historic desert folk music. The day concludes with a celebratory multi-course farewell dinner on board paired with vintage wines.

Sonesta Star Goddess (Nile Cruise)
Breakfast, Dinner

Day 10 | Return to Cairo & Complementary Spa Day

A relaxed luxury breakfast on deck watching the Nile flow gently by. You check out and board your private charter aircraft back to Cairo, where you are transferred to your city hotel for a complementary spa session.

Your airport transfer for the connecting international flight home is timed precisely to your departure schedule.

Four Seasons Hotel Cairo at Nile Plaza
Breakfast

Journey Details

Minimum Age

No fixed minimum — varies by activity

  • No formal minimum — These touring itineraries carry no fixed minimum age. Hotels and riads across Morocco and Egypt welcome children, and several of our properties have connecting family rooms.
  • Hot-air balloon flights — Balloon operators over the Valley of the Kings and Marrakech generally require children to be at least seven, and tall enough to brace unaided in the basket on landing.
  • Inside the tombs and kasbahs — Some tomb chambers and kasbah towers are reached by steep ladders and low, unlit passages, and are unsuitable for very young children or anyone unsteady on their feet. Your guide will always offer an alternative.
  • Desert nights — Camel treks and desert camps are usually set at six years and above, largely because of the cold and the distance from a clinic.
  • Talk to us first — Limits vary from property to property and from activity to activity. Speak to our experts before you book, and we will pre-arrange the details with each hotel and riad around the ages of the children travelling with you.

Safari Duffel

Duffel, safari hat, vest and hooded jumper

  • Your duffel — A soft-sided {{brand}} duffel, sized for the 15 kg limit on the light aircraft that move you between camps. Rigid suitcases cannot be carried on those flights at all.
  • What is inside it — A branded {{brand}} safari hat, a safari vest, and a hooded jumper for cold dawn starts and balloon mornings.
  • Yours to keep — The bag and everything in it is a gift, not a loan. Nothing is returned at the end of your journey.
  • Packing around it — Soft luggage, neutral colours, and layers. Laundry is included at nearly every camp, which is why 15 kg goes further than it sounds.

Note

Nile cruise schedules are fixed by the operator

River cruise itineraries between Luxor and Aswan run to fixed weekly schedules set by the cruise operator, so touring days are set in advance rather than fully flexible. Modest dress is appreciated at religious and cultural sites.

  • Early starts — Touring days begin very early in the morning. Your guide will confirm what time you will be collected from your hotel.
  • Tailoring this journey — If you would like this package with more or fewer days, arranged for a group, a family or a solo traveller, or with add-ons, reach out and we will tailor it for you.
  • Route changes — Your guide or driver may alter the route in certain circumstances, but every destination set out in the itinerary will be covered.

Documents

Visa on arrival or e-Visa, from $25

  • Passport — Valid for at least six months beyond your arrival date, with at least one blank page.
  • Visa — Most visitors, including UK, EU, US, Canadian and Australian passport holders, take either the US$25 single-entry eVisa online (about three working days) or a visa on arrival at Cairo and the other main airports. Both allow a 30-day stay.
  • Handled for you on group arrivals — Where we meet your group through our licensed ground agent, entry formalities are completed on your behalf while you clear the terminal.
  • Camera permits — Several tombs and museums charge separately for photography, and a few interiors prohibit it outright. Your guide carries the permits your itinerary needs.
  • Dress at religious sites — Shoulders and knees covered at mosques, churches and monasteries, and a scarf for women entering a mosque.
  • We file it for you — Whether you are flying in from overseas or travelling from elsewhere on the continent, we complete and submit your visa application on your behalf. You hand us your documents once. We do the filing, chase the approval, and carry the confirmations in your travel pack so nothing rests on you remembering a reference number at a counter.
  • Most of Africa is open to you — The great majority of our African destinations are now either visa-free or issue a visa on arrival, so an embassy queue is rarely in your future at all. Where a visa on arrival does apply, we prepare and lodge the paperwork ahead of you — you reach the desk with a finished file rather than a blank form and a biro.
  • Before you travel — Entry rules change frequently and without much notice. Your specialist confirms the current requirements for every passport in your party at the time of booking, and checks them again before you fly.

Guaranteed Departures

Private, tailor-made departures on your dates, or join a small group

  • Once confirmed, you travel — When your departure date is confirmed, it is guaranteed. We do not cancel, merge or reschedule a confirmed departure for commercial reasons, however the final numbers fall.
  • What confirms a departure — A departure becomes confirmed once the agreed number of travellers has been reached and paid for in line with the agreed payment schedule. Places held without payment do not count towards it.
  • If the minimum is not reached — Where a group departure has not reached its agreed numbers, we tell you early rather than late, and you choose: move to the next available date, continue at a revised small-group rate, or take a full refund of everything you have paid.
  • Solo journeys — Solo and tailor-made itineraries carry no group minimum at all. Your dates are confirmed the moment your deposit is received.
  • Visas — We prepare and submit your visa application on your behalf. Please note that the granting of a visa rests solely with the immigration authority of the country concerned, and is never guaranteed by us.
  • Medication — Anti-nausea and anti-anxiety tablets can be arranged for your journey. This is not a malaria-risk destination, so prophylaxis is not required. Any prescription medicine should be confirmed with your own doctor before you travel.
  • Not always hot — Despite the stereotype, North Africa moves through distinct seasons. Desert nights, mountain passes and pre-dawn starts can be genuinely cold. A hooded jumper is provided for cold conditions, and for guests joining a sunrise hot-air balloon flight.
  • Dietary requirements — If you have strict dietary needs, please tell us at the time of booking. This includes vegans, guests in recovery, non-drinkers, and anyone following a prescription from their doctor.
  • Travel insurance — We facilitate travel insurance for our clients. If you are already covered, please say so during booking so we can take it into account in your final quote.
  • Tailoring your quote — Some of our inclusions — a sunrise hot-air balloon flight, for example — may not be what you want. Point these out and we will adjust your final offer accordingly.
  • What to pack — Modest, breathable layers. Shoulders and knees covered at mosques, churches and through the older medinas, with a scarf for women entering a mosque. Desert nights and mountain passes turn genuinely cold even in summer, so pack a warm layer whatever the forecast says. Closed, broken-in shoes matter more than you would expect: medina lanes and tomb interiors are uneven, and often unlit.
  • Your travel duffel & kit — A {{brand}} duffel is provided ahead of departure, sized for charter and light-aircraft transfers where luggage is often limited to 15 kg per person. Inside it you will find a branded sun hat, a safari vest and a hooded jumper for cold desert mornings.
