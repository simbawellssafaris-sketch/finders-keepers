Overview

Built by the
Sarkies Brothers

Established in 1885 by the Armenian Sarkies brothers, whose landmarks also include Raffles Singapore and The Strand in Yangon, the Eastern & Oriental — the 'E&O' to generations of travellers — remains the only hotel with sea frontage inside George Town's UNESCO World Heritage core.

Martin and Tigran Sarkies opened the Eastern in 1884 and the adjoining Oriental in 1885; combined, they became the largest hotel in Penang, later adding a grand ballroom and, in 1922, a Victory Annexe that brought the total room count higher still. Advertised in 1927 as 'the premier hotel east of Suez,' the E&O counted Charlie Chaplin, Rudyard Kipling, Somerset Maugham and Herman Hesse among its guests through Penang's most storied decades. Today, the original Heritage Wing's soaring echo-dome lobby and 100 suites sit alongside the 2013-built Victory Annexe's 132 sea-fronting suites, together forming a 232-suite hotel that remains, in the words of one long-time visitor, 'the grand dame of heritage hotels.'

An all-suite hotel since 1885. Every room at the Eastern & Oriental is a suite — there are no standard rooms — a distinction the hotel has maintained since its earliest incarnation under the Sarkies brothers.

Experiences

Chaplin, Kipling

Heritage

George Town UNESCO Core

A five-minute walk brings guests to George Town's colonial villas, Chinese shophouses and celebrated street art, recognised as a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 2008.

History

The E&O Gallery

A dedicated gallery within the Victory Annexe retail arcade tracing Penang's social and cultural history through the hotel's own story.

Location

George Town's
Only Seafront Hotel

The Eastern & Oriental sits at the corner of Lebuh Farquhar, in the heart of George Town, the only hotel within the UNESCO World Heritage Site with direct sea frontage.

  • Located at 10 Lebuh Farquhar, within George Town's UNESCO World Heritage core.
  • A five-minute walk to George Town's colonial villas, Chinese shophouses and street art.
  • Roughly 30 minutes, or 17km, from Penang International Airport.
  • The only hotel in George Town's historic centre with direct Andaman Sea frontage.
