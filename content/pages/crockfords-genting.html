Overview

The Highlands'
Best-Kept Secret

Consciously designed as one of Genting Highlands' swankiest hidden gems, Crockfords takes its name and British grandeur from the exclusive Mayfair gaming club of the same name, positioned as the pinnacle of stays at Resorts World Genting.

A discreet entrance keeps Crockfords separate from the bustle of SkyAvenue next door, opening into a lobby with a floral installation by British artist Rebecca Louise Law that sets the tone for the 140 rooms and suites beyond. Every accommodation offers unobstructed views of the surrounding mountainous landscape, letting guests take in sunrise over the highlands without leaving bed, in a pastel and neutral palette of grey, teal, rose and deep red. All Crockfords suites feature an in-room private steam bath and Greek Calacatta marble restroom fixtures made exclusively for the brand, while a trio of 2,900-square-foot Executive Suites, each with a private gym and spa room, form the crème de la crème of luxury living at 1,860 metres above sea level.

No pool or gym within the hotel itself. Crockfords' guests share the swimming pool and other resort facilities with RW Hotel and Genting Grand nearby — worth knowing when planning a stay focused purely on relaxation.

Location

Genting Highlands,
Malaysia's Cloud Resort

Crockfords sits at the summit of Genting Highlands, roughly an hour from Kuala Lumpur, a mountaintop resort destination in its own right since the 1960s.

  • Located within Resorts World Genting, at an elevation of roughly 1,860 metres.
  • Directly adjacent to SkyAvenue, home to SkyCasino and Skytropolis Funland.
  • Roughly an hour's drive from Kuala Lumpur, or reachable via the Genting SkyWay cable car.
  • A certified Chinese Friendly Hotel, offering enhanced service for Chinese-speaking guests.
Fly

Kuala Lumpur International Airport connects to Genting Highlands by road, roughly 1.5 hours.
