The Principle

A journey booked a year out shouldn’t be paid for like one booked next month.

Most of our travellers book somewhere between six and eighteen months ahead — because the camps worth staying in fill that far in advance, and because the best months to travel are known long before they arrive.

That gap is an opportunity rather than a problem. Rather than asking for the full amount up front, or leaving one large payment to land shortly before you fly, we spread the balance across the time you already have. The schedule costs nothing extra, carries no interest, and can be shaped around your circumstances.

Two things are fixed: a 30% deposit confirms the booking, and the journey is settled in full 60 days before departure. Everything between those two points is arrangeable.

The Schedule

How the balance is spread.

The further ahead you book, the more the balance divides. These are our standard schedules — your specialist can adjust the middle payments around your own timing.

12+ months ahead
30% Deposit35%35%

30% at booking · 35% six months before departure · 35% at 60 days. Three payments, roughly half a year apart.

6 – 12 months ahead
30% Deposit40%30%

30% at booking · 40% at 90 days · 30% at 60 days. The most common shape, and the one most travellers use.

3 – 6 months ahead
30% Deposit70% at 60 days

30% at booking, 70% at 60 days. Two payments, because there is not enough runway for a sensible third.

Under 60 days
100% at booking

Payable in full at the time of booking, since the balance date has already passed. Late bookings are welcome — we simply need to settle the suppliers immediately.

Prefer to pay it all at once? That is entirely acceptable and you are welcome to do it at any stage — at booking, or partway through the schedule. Paying early costs no more and no less. It simply closes the matter.

The schedules above are our standard shapes. Where a different rhythm would suit you better, say so when you enquire and your specialist will build one around the same two fixed points.
