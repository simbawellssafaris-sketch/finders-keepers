Overview

Nineteen
Oh-Six

A hundred and thirty-six rooms and suites, most of them with a balcony over the lake.

The Hotel Palace opened on the Lucerne lakefront in 1906 and spent a century as the address in the city before closing for a restoration that took years and reopened it as a Mandarin Oriental at the end of 2020. Every guest room was rebuilt, the public rooms redesigned and the grounds relandscaped; 136 rooms and suites now look out at the lake and the Alps behind it, and most of them have a private balcony or terrace. One room was deliberately left alone. The Salon Alpine still carries its original 1906 decoration, which is the clearest evidence of what the rest of the building used to be.

Find the Salon Alpine. A single hidden room kept its original 1906 interior through the restoration. It is the reference point for everything else in the building.
