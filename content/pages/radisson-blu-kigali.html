Overview

Kigali's
Convention Address

Home to Rwanda's largest convention centre, the Radisson Blu sits five kilometres from Kigali city centre — the country's most established address for business and leisure alike.

Opened in 2016 in a joint venture between the Rwandan government and private investors, the Radisson Blu Hotel & Convention Centre pairs a distinctive golden dome — inspired by traditional Rwandan huts — with banded facades that echo local basketwork. The adjoining Kigali Convention Centre spans 32,200 square metres, with 18 meeting rooms and an auditorium seating up to 2,600, making the hotel Rwanda's default base for conferences and summits. Its 292 rooms and suites are finished in calm, earth-toned interiors, each with a private balcony over the city or the convention centre's landmark silhouette.

Location

Between the City
and the Airport

The Radisson Blu sits five kilometres from Kigali's city centre and roughly the same distance from Kigali International Airport, within Rwanda's diplomatic and government district.

  • The hotel sits within 2km of several government embassies, the British High Commission, Parliament and the Supreme Court.
  • Kigali International Airport is roughly an 8-minute drive, making the hotel a convenient first or last stop on a Rwanda itinerary.
  • The adjoining Kigali Convention Centre is Rwanda's largest, hosting international summits and conferences year-round.
  • 650 on-site parking bays and a 24-hour front desk support both business and leisure travellers.
