Set on a hillside above Karon, one of Phuket's quieter, longer beaches on the island's southwest coast.

Dinso Resort & Villas sits on a hillside above Karon Beach, one of Phuket's longest and least developed stretches of sand on the island's southwest coast. Rooms and villas are arranged around tiered pools with views toward the Andaman Sea, a short walk from Karon's beachfront restaurants and a comfortable drive from Phuket Old Town's Sino-Portuguese architecture and Patong's nightlife, without sitting directly within either.
