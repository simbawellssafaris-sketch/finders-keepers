The Occasion

A week beats an evening.

Milestone birthdays have an awkwardness to them. A party puts the person being celebrated in front of a room, which not everybody wants, and the day is over in an evening. A journey spreads the occasion across a week and puts the attention on the experience rather than on the age.

We arrange the day itself properly: a balloon flight at dawn, a private dinner set up somewhere unexpected, Maasai warriors performing traditional songs and dance around the table, and a cake that comes out the way a birthday cake should — with the full chorus. Almost everyone wants to be sung to on their birthday, whatever they claim beforehand.

Tell us what they would actually like all the same. For the handful who genuinely prefer it quieter, we still mark the moment properly — a lion at first light and dinner under a tree instead of a crowd — just without the singing.
