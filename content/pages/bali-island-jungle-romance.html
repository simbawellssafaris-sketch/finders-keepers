Day by Day

Your Itinerary

Ubud

Day 1 | Arrival in Bali — The Emerald Canopy

VIP tarmac greeting at Ngurah Rai International Airport. Private luxury SUV transfer into the misty valleys of Ubud. Check-in at Kuwarasan A Pramana Resort — a rustic tree-wrapped architectural sanctuary hidden inside dense jungle foliage with stunning canopy views and the peaceful sound of the river below. Early arrivals enjoy a private guided tour of Ubud: vibrant art galleries, traditional markets, and the sacred Monkey Forest with hundreds of long-tailed macaques. Welcome dinner at the resort.

Dinner

Day 2 | River Chasm Waterfalls & Omma Ubud Sundowner

Private guided exploration of the roaring basin of Tegenungan Waterfall — cascading into a deep turquoise pool surrounded by lush jungle. Swimming in the cool refreshing water and exploring hidden coves and scenic viewpoints. Late afternoon at Omma Ubud: premium craft cocktails served from a private draped daybed on the clifftop, watching the waterfall turn golden as the jungle canopy lights up — a truly romantic sundowner.

Kuwarasan A Pramana Resort →
Breakfast, Dinner

Day 3 | Terraced Rice Gorges & River Pools

Morning exploring the dramatic layered emerald amphitheatre of the Cretya Ubud terraced valleys. Walking private ridges with stunning views of rice terraces cascading down the hillside, and swimming in multi-tiered infinity pools overlooking the Ayung River gorge. Guide explains the UNESCO World Heritage Subak irrigation system that has sustained Balinese agriculture for centuries. Local farm visit for traditional rice cultivation.

Breakfast, Dinner
North Bali

Day 4 | Sekumpul Waterfall & Hanging Restaurant Breakfast

Drive into the northern highlands to view Sekumpul Waterfall — the tallest cascade in Bali, plunging over 80 metres into a deep valley. Jungle hike to the viewpoint through mist-cooled air. A luxury white-linen hot breakfast served from the edge of the Hanging Restaurant & Bar at Sekumpul, looking down at the immense waterfall emerging from the mountain mist — an unforgettable dining experience. Check-in at Munduk Cabins Luxury Resort.

Breakfast, Dinner
Sidemen

Day 5 | Handcrafted Bamboo Flow — Lantern Riverbank Dinner

Drive down into the quiet valleys of Sidemen — one of Bali's most beautiful and unspoiled regions with terraced rice fields and Mount Agung rising in the distance. Check-in at Hideout Flow: an exclusive-use handcrafted bamboo luxury villa designed to flow over a rushing volcanic river with organic curves, natural materials, and the soothing sound of the river below. Elegant multi-course lantern dinner beside the riverbank, warmed by a roaring bonfire and hundreds of hanging lanterns.

Travel Kit

Duffel, sun hat, rain shell and day pack

  • Your duffel — A soft-sided {{brand}} duffel, sized for domestic flights and easier than a hard case on train platforms, ferry jetties and old-town lanes.
  • What is inside it — A wide-brim sun hat, a lightweight packable rain shell for tropical downpours, and a foldable day pack for temple mornings and market afternoons.
  • Yours to keep — The bag and everything in it is a gift, not a loan.
  • Packing around it — Slip-on shoes save time at temples, where you remove them repeatedly, and one set of clothing covering shoulders and knees is essential for religious sites.

Note

Popular sites are best visited early

Iconic spots such as rice terraces and temple complexes are significantly quieter — and cooler — before mid-morning. Your guide will recommend early starts where it makes a meaningful difference.

  • Early starts — The best of this journey happens at dawn — before the heat, the crowds and the haze. Your guide will confirm what time you will be collected from your hotel.
  • Tailoring this journey — If you would like this package with more or fewer days, arranged for a group, a family or a solo traveller, or with add-ons, reach out and we will tailor it for you.
  • Route changes — Your guide or driver may alter the route in certain circumstances, but every destination set out in the itinerary will be covered.

Documents

Visa on arrival for most nationalities

  • Passport — Valid for at least six months beyond your arrival date, with at least one blank page.
  • Visa on Arrival — UK, EU, US, Canadian, Australian and most other visitors need a Visa on Arrival for 30 days, extendable once. Take the e-VOA online before you fly to bypass the payment queue at Ngurah Rai.
  • Customs declaration — The All Indonesia electronic customs declaration must be filed within two days of arrival. The QR code is scanned as you leave baggage reclaim.
  • Bali tourist levy — Bali charges every foreign visitor a one-off levy of IDR 150,000, paid online through the Love Bali portal before arrival. Keep the voucher — it is spot-checked at temples and attractions.
  • We handle the paperwork — We complete and submit your visa, eVisa or travel-authorisation applications on your behalf. You supply the documents once; we do the filing, track the approval and hold the confirmations for you.
  • Where you must appear in person — A few consulates insist on biometrics or a short interview that nobody can sit on your behalf — China requires fingerprinting for some nationalities, as do European and United States visas. We still prepare the entire file; we simply book you a single appointment and brief you beforehand so one visit settles it.
  • Before you travel — Entry rules change frequently and without much notice. Your specialist confirms the current requirements for every passport in your party at the time of booking, and checks them again before you fly.
