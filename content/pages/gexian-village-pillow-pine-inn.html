Overview

The Elegance
of Chinese Literati

Zhenshansu winds along the mountainside in three connected rows, its antique rooflines resembling a small ridge, giving the whole inn the character of a mountainous landscape painting brought to life.

The inn sits within Gexian Village, a resort built around the legend of the Taoist immortal Ge Xuan, where lantern-lit streets, nightly light shows and a cable car draw visitors into a landscape of mist-wrapped peaks and ancient pine forest. Zhenshansu introduces the natural landscape indoors wherever possible, taking the return to nature as a form of spiritual comfort — its architecture and interiors designed around the aesthetic sensibilities of Chinese literati scholars. Rooms across its three tiers each offer views over the surrounding village and mountains, with higher rows commanding the widest outlook across Gexian's peaks.

Part of the Gexian Village Resort. Zhenshansu is one of several distinctive inns within the wider Gexian Village Resort, known for its nightly Zhongmiao Pavilion light show and lantern-lit Lingbao Immortal Street.

Experiences

Lanterns, Light Shows
& the Cloud Sea

Gexian Village transforms after dark, with lantern-lit streets, a light show and a water-screen film all part of a stay here.

By Day

Gexian Mountain Cloud Sea

A hike to the peak of Gexian Mountain rewards early risers with a sea of cloud rolling through the surrounding peaks.

By Night

Zhongmiao Pavilion Light Show

A nightly colour projection show themed around the seasons and dragon-phoenix motifs, staged on the water below the pavilion.

Evenings

Lingbao Immortal Street

Thousands of lanterns light this street each night, creating one of the village's most atmospheric evening walks.

Story

Water Screen Film — Return to Truth

A high-tech nightly water-screen show recounting the story of the Taoist immortal Ge Xuan, staged on the Lake of Illusory Realm.

Views

The Sky Mirror Platform

A circular viewpoint overlooking the whole village, reputed among visitors to be especially effective for love wishes.

Culture

Handicraft Workshops

Interactive craft stalls and performances throughout the village bring Jiangxi folk traditions to life for visiting families.

Location

Gexian Village,
Jiangxi's Immortal Realm

Gexian Village sits on Gexian Mountain in Yanshan County, Shangrao, a themed mountain resort built around the legend of the Taoist immortal Ge Xuan.

  • Located within the Gexian Village Resort Area, Gexianshan Township, Shangrao, Jiangxi.
  • Reached via a free sightseeing bus into the scenic area, with a cable car connecting to the mountain village.
  • Roughly 50km from Sanqingshan Airport and Hengfeng Railway Station.
  • Part of a themed resort experience blending performance, folklore and mountain scenery.
Fly

Sanqingshan Airport is roughly 50km from Gexian Village.

Rail

Hengfeng Railway Station connects to the wider Jiangxi rail network, roughly 49km away.

Enter

A free shuttle bus and cable car carry guests from the visitor centre up into the village and inn.

Mountain & Myth

Gexian Village's nightly light shows and lantern-lit streets draw on the legend of Ge Xuan, a Taoist immortal said to have cultivated wisdom on this very mountain.
