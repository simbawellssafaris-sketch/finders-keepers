Overview

A Balcony
Over Nakuru

Built into the cliffside above Lake Nakuru National Park, the lodge trades ground-level intimacy for genuinely spectacular views.

Lake Nakuru Cliff Lodge sits on the escarpment rim overlooking Lake Nakuru National Park, one of Kenya's most important rhino sanctuaries and, in the right season, home to vast flocks of flamingos along the shoreline. Rooms and public spaces are oriented outward across the Rift Valley floor, so the lake, the acacia woodland and the animals moving through them are visible without leaving your room. The Park itself is compact enough to explore thoroughly in a day or two, making the lodge a popular, easy add-on to a Rift Valley or Maasai Mara circuit.

Location

Lake Nakuru
National Park

A compact Rift Valley park roughly midway between Nairobi and the Maasai Mara.

  • Set on the escarpment above Lake Nakuru National Park in Kenya's central Rift Valley.
  • Around 3-3.5 hours by road from Nairobi via Naivasha.
  • A popular stop en route between Nairobi and the Maasai Mara.
  • The Park is fully fenced, protecting one of Kenya's most successful rhino programmes.
Fly
