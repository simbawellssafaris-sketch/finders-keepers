Overview

Khao Sok's
Ancient Forest

Khao Sok's rainforest is estimated to be over 160 million years old, among the oldest on Earth.

Jungle House Resort sits at the edge of Khao Sok National Park, home to rainforest estimated at over 160 million years old — older than the Amazon — and to Cheow Lan Lake, a limestone-karst reservoir often compared to Halong Bay. Bungalows are set among the forest canopy, with the park's raft houses on the lake, night safaris, and treks to waterfalls and cave systems all arranged from the resort. It's a genuine wilderness counterpoint to Thailand's beach-resort coastline.
