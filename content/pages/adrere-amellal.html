Overview

Sand Castle,
By the Salt Lake

Adrere Amellal — White Mountain in the local Amazigh language — rises from the desert floor like a fortress, its kershef walls built from sun-dried salt rock, clay and straw.

Founded by environmentalist Dr Mounir Neamatalla, the lodge revived Siwan building techniques nearly lost to time, employing local craftsmen to shape every wall, door and piece of furniture from materials found within the oasis itself. There is no electricity anywhere on the property — after dark, hundreds of beeswax candles and oil lamps light the rooms, corridors and courtyards, with coal braziers warming the cooler desert nights. A spring-fed pool, shaded by palms, and an organic garden supplying nearly every meal complete a lodge built, in its founder's words, to prove that luxury can rhyme with simplicity.

A sister lodge in Siwa Town. Adrere Amellal's owners also run Albabenshal, an 11-room heritage lodge built from restored kershef houses on the edge of Siwa's historic Shali Fortress.

Location

Siwa Oasis,
Egypt's Far West

Siwa sits roughly 50km from the Libyan border, an isolated oasis inhabited for over 10,000 years, reached only by a long desert drive or private charter.

  • 16km from Siwa Town, at the foot of the White Mountain overlooking Lake Siwa.
  • Reached by a roughly 8 to 10-hour drive from Cairo, or a private charter flight to a nearby airstrip.
  • The oasis retains its own language, Siwi, alongside Arabic, and a distinct tradition of embroidery and silver jewellery.
  • Notable past guests have included Prince Charles and the Duchess of Cornwall.
Drive

The most common route is an 8 to 10-hour private drive west from Cairo across the desert.

Charter

A private plane charter to a nearby airstrip cuts the journey to around an hour, at a higher cost.

Arrive

Guests are met and driven the final stretch around Siwa Lake before arriving at the lodge's gates.

A World Apart

Luxury Without Electricity

Adrere Amellal remains one of the world's most complete demonstrations that true luxury can mean simplicity, silence and candlelight rather than more amenities.
