Overview

One of KL's
Largest Hotel Rooms

Overlooking the Petronas Twin Towers and adjoining Kuala Lumpur Convention Centre, Grand Hyatt Kuala Lumpur offers 411 rooms and suites that rank among the most spacious of any five-star hotel in the city.

Refined modernity runs through the interiors, with natural materials and local artistic accents reflecting Malaysia's layered culture, while every room's bathroom features a distinctive liquid crystal glass cube that turns opaque at the touch of a switch for privacy. Beyond the rooms, the hotel anchors itself with an outdoor pool offering a resort-like escape in the middle of the city, complete with a wading pool and Petronas Towers views from the water. ESSA Spa draws on ramuan, the traditional Malay preparation of herbal healing ingredients, for a sense of calm rarely found this close to KLCC's shopping and business district, all within walking distance of the hotel's front doors.

A five-minute walk to Suria KLCC. The hotel's Golden Triangle location places Suria KLCC Shopping Centre, Pavilion Kuala Lumpur and the Petronas Twin Towers all within a short, walkable distance.

Location

The Golden Triangle,
Beside the Towers

Grand Hyatt Kuala Lumpur sits in the city's Golden Triangle district, adjacent to the Kuala Lumpur Convention Centre and a short walk from the Petronas Twin Towers.

  • Located at No. 12, Jalan Pinang, adjoining the Kuala Lumpur Convention Centre.
  • A five-minute walk to Suria KLCC Shopping Centre and the Petronas Twin Towers.
  • Roughly 15 minutes' walk to Pavilion Kuala Lumpur and the Bukit Bintang nightlife district.
  • Raja Chulan and KLCC LRT stations are both a short walk from the hotel.
