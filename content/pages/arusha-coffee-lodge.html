Overview

A Homestead
Among the Coffee

Hidden amongst one of Tanzania’s largest coffee plantations on the outskirts of Arusha, this Elewana lodge is designed around an original landowner’s homestead dating to the early 1900s.

Lying on the rolling foothills that cascade down from Mount Meru, Arusha Coffee Lodge sits within a working coffee estate at the traditional gateway to Tanzania's northern safari circuit. Thirty Plantation Houses radiate out into the evergreen coffee fields, their colours and textures echoing the coffee theme throughout, while the main homestead houses an à la carte restaurant, café bistro and lounge warmed by open log fires. It's a deliberately homely, restorative stop — a haven for relaxation before or after the Serengeti, Ngorongoro or Tarangire.

Experiences

From Bean
to Cup

Days at Arusha Coffee Lodge move between the plantation itself, boutique shopping on the grounds, and day trips into nearby Arusha National Park.

Signature

Coffee Plantation Tour

A guided walk through the estate reveals how coffee is grown, harvested, dried and roasted, ending with a tasting.

Nearby

Arusha National Park

A day excursion (additional cost) to the nearby park offers game drives amid abundant birdlife and varied landscapes within its compact 137km².

On Foot

Plantation Walks

Guided walks around the grounds and surrounding coffee gardens suit those who prefer to explore on foot.

Shopping

Trader's Walk

Five boutique shops on the grounds include Shanga (recycled-material crafts), a curio shop, and the Tanzanite Experience jewellery store.

City

Arusha City Tour

A guided tour of Arusha's Maasai Markets, Tanzanite Museum and Cultural Heritage Centre is available at additional cost.

Dining

Location

Gateway to the
Northern Circuit

Arusha Coffee Lodge sits on the outskirts of Arusha town, at the foot of Mount Meru, the traditional starting point for a northern Tanzania safari.

  • The lodge sits within one of Tanzania’s largest coffee estates, a working plantation still in active production today.
  • Arusha town, roughly 20 minutes away, is the launch point for safaris into the Serengeti, Ngorongoro and Tarangire.
  • Kuro Airstrip, used for onward flights into the northern parks, is approximately 20 minutes from the lodge.
  • The lodge is family-friendly and welcomes children of all ages, with family suites available.
Fly

Kilimanjaro International Airport (JRO) is roughly a 45-minute drive from the lodge.

Drive

Arusha town centre is approximately 20 minutes away, with Kuro Airstrip a similar distance for onward flights.
