Overview

Wabi-Sabi,
By the River

A note on this listing: we were unable to verify a property named exactly "Wanakoto" in Takayama. The closest, well-documented match is Wanosato — an award-winning 160-year-old ryokan on the Miyagawa River — and this page is built on that property.

Perched in a forested area beside the Miyagawa River, twenty minutes from historic Takayama, Wanosato occupies a genuine gassho-zukuri minka — a farmhouse with a steeply pitched thatched roof unique to this small pocket of the Japanese Alps. Just eight individually designed rooms are spread across the main house and smaller outbuildings, each connected to the forest and mountains outside, with an irori open hearth at the centre of the main house where guests gather each evening for local sake and quiet conversation. There are no private in-room onsens, but two beautifully crafted communal baths — one hinoki cypress, one rock garden style — rotate between men's and women's use daily, drawing directly from the property's own Hida Kuraiyama hot spring source.

A 15,000-tsubo forest estate. Wanosato's grounds span roughly 15,000 tsubo (about 5 hectares) of forest along the Miyagawa River, giving each of its eight rooms genuine space and privacy despite the ryokan's small scale.

Experiences

Kaiseki by Firelight,
Forest Walks at Dawn

A stay at Wanosato moves at the pace of the seasons, with meals, baths and quiet forest walks the entire itinerary.

Culinary

Seasonal Kaiseki Dinner

A full multi-course Kaiseki dinner built around wild mountain vegetables, river fish and Hida beef, served near the sunken hearth.

Bathing

Hinoki & Rock Garden Onsen

Two distinct communal baths — a fragrant cypress bath and a rock garden-style pool — offer different characters across a single stay.

On Foot

A Walk Along the River

A quiet morning walk through the surrounding forest and along the Miyagawa River, with only birdsong and running water for company.

Evenings

Fireside Sake

After dinner, guests are invited to sit by the irori hearth for a nightcap of local sake and stories from the staff.

Nearby

Takayama's Old Town

A 20-minute shuttle ride brings guests into Takayama's Edo-period merchant streets, morning markets and museums.

Day Trip

Shirakawa-go

The UNESCO-listed thatched-roof village of Shirakawa-go lies roughly 70 minutes away, a natural pairing with Wanosato's own gassho-zukuri architecture.

Location

The Hida Mountains,
Beside a River

Wanosato sits in a forested valley of the Hida region, twenty minutes by shuttle from Takayama's historic centre, within reach of Shirakawa-go's UNESCO-listed villages.

  • Located beside the Miyagawa River, roughly 20 minutes by shuttle from Takayama Station.
  • A 70-minute drive from Shirakawa-go, the UNESCO World Heritage thatched-roof village.
  • No restaurants within walking distance — meals are best booked directly with the ryokan.
  • Built in the Hida region's distinctive gassho-zukuri style, with a rare steeply thatched roof.
Fly

Chubu Centrair International Airport connects to Takayama via train, roughly 2.5 hours.

Rail

Limited express trains from Nagoya reach Takayama Station in around 2.5 hours.

Arrive

A complimentary shuttle bus departs Takayama Station at set times for the 20-minute ride to the ryokan.
