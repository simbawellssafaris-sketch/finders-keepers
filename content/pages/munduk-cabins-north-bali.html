Overview

Six Cabins,
Turned West

By the time the road finishes its last lazy switchback, Munduk Cabins appears — sleek, dark timber pavilions pegged to the hillside, all turned toward the horizon for golden hour.

Munduk remains blissfully under the radar compared to Bali's southern hotspots, its cool climate and clove-scented air offering a genuine antidote for travellers who find the coast too busy. Munduk Cabins keeps things intimate with just six suites — five private cabins and one two-bedroom villa — reached along elevated wooden walkways winding through the trees, iron railings and hardwood underfoot giving the approach an upscale-treehouse feel. Inside, floor-to-ceiling glass does the talking: forest rolling toward blue mountains, sky that remembers to be dramatic, and on the clearest evenings, the Indian Ocean visible beyond Lovina in the distance.

Roughly two hours from Ubud. Munduk sits about two hours from Ubud and two and a half hours from Seminyak or Canggu — the distance itself is part of what keeps this highland region so quiet.

Experiences

Waterfalls, Coffee Trails
& a Silent Lake

Munduk's cool highlands are threaded with clove and coffee plantations, waterfalls and Bali's twin volcanic lakes.

Trekking

Munduk Plantation Trek

A complimentary three-hour guided trek through coffee, clove, chocolate and vanilla plantations, with insight into the Bali Aga people's traditions predating the Majapahit kingdom.

Waterfalls

Munduk & Banyumala Falls

Munduk Falls is a quick hop from the cabins; Banyumala, a little further, rewards the trip with its twin cascades.

On the Water

Lake Tamblingan by Canoe

Tamblingan's still water is best explored by canoe at first light, one of North Bali's quietest scenic experiences.

Culture

Village Lanes at Munduk's Pace

Village lanes hum at a pace that makes your shoulders drop three centimetres — a genuine slow-travel counterpoint to the south.

Evenings

The Fire Pit & Clove Cocktails

Copper Kitchen & Bar mixes clove, citrus and smoke into cocktails, best enjoyed as the jungle drops into silhouette.

Wellness

In-Cabin Spa Treatments

Massage and spa treatments can be arranged directly in the privacy of your own cabin deck.

Location

Munduk,
Bali's Quiet Highlands

Munduk sits in the cool hills of Buleleng Regency, above Bali's twin lakes, a region that remains largely untouched compared to the island's southern coast.

  • Located roughly 10 minutes from the centre of Munduk village.
  • Around two hours from Ubud, two and a half from Seminyak or Canggu.
  • Close to Lake Buyan and Lake Tamblingan, and the coastal town of Lovina.
  • Year-round highland temperatures of 19 to 26°C offer a cool contrast to Bali's coast.
Fly

Ngurah Rai International Airport (Denpasar) is the main gateway, roughly two and a half hours by road.

Drive

Arrive
