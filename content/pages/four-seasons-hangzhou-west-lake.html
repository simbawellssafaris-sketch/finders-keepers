Overview

A Village,
Not a Tower

Local height restrictions kept Four Seasons Hangzhou to just two storeys, and the result is a hotel that reads as a traditional Jiang Nan lakeside village rather than a conventional high-rise resort.

Black-tiled pagoda-style pavilions sit low against nine acres of bamboo forest, lily-strewn ponds and Chinese gardens, woven through with willow trees and mist-covered lagoons on the northwest corner of West Lake, a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Just 78 rooms and suites plus three standalone Song Dynasty-inspired villas make this one of the more intimate Four Seasons properties anywhere, despite opening as a newly built hotel in 2010. Nearly every room looks onto either the tranquil gardens or a quiet stretch of West Lake itself, with ground-floor rooms adding a private terrace, and the three-bedroom villas going further still with a private garden, indoor pool and cinema.

Just a five-minute walk to West Lake. Despite its secluded, garden-village feel, the hotel sits a five-minute walk from West Lake itself, with complimentary bicycles and an electric boat dock making the journey even shorter.

Experiences

Longjing Tea,
Picked by Hand

Hangzhou's West Lake and its surrounding tea hills have inspired poets and emperors for centuries — the hotel builds its experiences directly around that legacy.

Signature

Wooden Boat Arrival

Guests can arrive by traditional air-conditioned wooden boat, sipping tea and sampling gourmet snacks en route to the hotel's private dock.

Culture

West Lake by Bicycle

Complimentary bicycles let guests explore West Lake's UNESCO-listed shoreline, its bridges, pagodas and causeways at their own pace.

Culinary

Longjing Tea Picking

A drive into the hills south of the city to see Longjing tea hand-picked in its namesake village, followed by a chance to dry the leaves in a traditional iron wok.

Heritage

Lingyin Temple

One of China's oldest and wealthiest Buddhist temples sits within the same scenic area as the hotel.

Wellness

The Spa

A chinoiserie-filled spa offering treatments inspired by traditional Chinese wellness practices.

Nearby

Longjing Village & Six Harmonies Pagoda

Tea plantations and a historic riverside pagoda are both within easy reach of the hotel.

Location

West Lake's
Northwest Corner

The hotel sits on the northwest edge of West Lake, within the Lingyin Temple Scenic Area, a five-minute walk from the lake and a 45-minute train ride from Shanghai.

  • Located on the northwest corner of West Lake, a UNESCO World Heritage Site since 2011.
  • Within the Lingyin Temple Scenic Area, home to one of China's oldest Buddhist temples.
  • A five-minute walk to West Lake itself, with complimentary bicycles and an electric boat dock.
  • Just 5km from Hangzhou's commercial centre, and 45 minutes from Shanghai by high-speed train.
Fly

Hangzhou Xiaoshan International Airport is roughly 45 minutes from the hotel by car.

Rail

High-speed trains connect Hangzhou to Shanghai in as little as 45 minutes.

Arrive
