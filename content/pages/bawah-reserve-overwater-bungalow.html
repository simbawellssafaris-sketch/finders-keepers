Overview

Not Bali,
But Worth the Journey

Bawah Reserve sits roughly 500 miles northwest of Bali, in Indonesia's remote Anambas Islands — worth flagging clearly, since its geography is entirely distinct from Bali itself.

Spread across six privately owned islands and three lagoons in the South China Sea, Bawah Reserve is one of Southeast Asia's most isolated all-inclusive island retreats, its reef-fringed waters ranked among the clearest in the region. The reserve holds 35 suites, villas and bungalows across four categories, the most striking of which are its overwater bungalows — 105 square metres perched directly above turquoise water, with a sundeck leading straight into the lagoon below. Every rate is fully inclusive of meals, land and sea activities, wellbeing programming, spa treatments, Wi-Fi and laundry, in keeping with the reserve's ethos of a genuinely disconnected, all-covered escape.

Location

Bawah Reserve sits in the Anambas Islands, part of Indonesia's Riau Islands province, a remote archipelago in the South China Sea roughly 500 miles from Bali.

  • Located across six privately owned islands in the Anambas archipelago, Riau Islands province.

  • One of the more remote all-inclusive private island reserves in Southeast Asia.
