Overview

The Original
Bamboo Hideaway

Hidden deep in the jungles of East Bali near Sidemen, Hideout was the original bamboo eco-lodge in the region — once ranked the world's 4th most-wished-for Airbnb, with stays booked out months in advance.

Every structure is built from locally sourced bamboo, a fast-growing, low-carbon material that lets each hideout blend directly into its riverside jungle setting rather than intrude on it. No two hideouts are alike: multi-level treehouse villas with infinity pools sit alongside smaller, cosier cabins overlooking the river, from a budget-friendly rice-field villa to a grand treehouse commanding the site's best views. With limited Wi-Fi and minimal electricity by design, Hideout operates on a genuine digital-detox model — guests are encouraged to disconnect from technology and reconnect with the sound of the river, the jungle's night chorus, and each other.

Rustic by design, not by accident. Hideout's bamboo-and-open-air construction means no air conditioning and, in the more rustic villas, an outdoor bathroom — guests seeking a fully enclosed, climate-controlled stay should set expectations accordingly before booking.

Culture

Sidemen's Rice Terraces

Some of Bali's most picturesque stepped rice paddies surround the wider Sidemen region, best explored on foot or by scooter.

Craft

Silver Workshops

Local artisans in Sidemen teach visitors to melt, shape and polish their own silver jewellery, a hands-on souvenir unlike any other.

Nature

Gembleng Waterfall

A three-tiered waterfall near Sidemen village, its cascading pools flowing into one another, a popular half-day trip.

Adventure

Telaga Waja River Rafting

White-water rafting down the Telaga Waja River, a short drive from the hideouts, for a more active day out.

Location

Selat,
Beneath Gunung Agung

Hideout sits in Selat village, roughly 10 minutes north of Sidemen village proper, deep in the jungle foothills of Gunung Agung in East Bali's Karangasem Regency.

  • Located on the banks of a river in Selat, Karangasem Regency, East Bali.
  • Roughly 10 minutes from Sidemen village and its market, silver workshops and restaurants.
  • About 70km, or roughly two hours, from Ngurah Rai International Airport.
  • Uber and standard ride-hailing apps do not reliably operate this far from the city — a private taxi or driver is recommended.
