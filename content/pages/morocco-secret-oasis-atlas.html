Your Itinerary

Marrakech

Day 1 | Arrival in Marrakech — The Red City Oasis

VIP tarmac handling at Marrakech Menara Airport. Transfer to the world-renowned Royal Mansour — ultra-luxurious retreats with private multi-storey riads and lush gardens. Early arrivals enjoy a private city tour: the vibrant Jemaa el-Fnaa square (UNESCO World Heritage site), the Koutoubia Mosque with its stunning 77-metre minaret. Intimate orientation dinner at the hotel.

Day 2 | Secret Medina Passages — Hidden Riads & Ben Youssef Madrasa

Private exploration of hidden architectural gems of the Marrakech medina. The Ben Youssef Madrasa: a 14th-century Islamic college with stunning zellij tilework and intricate cedar wood carvings. The Dar Si Said Museum — a 19th-century palace showcasing Moroccan craftsmanship. Your guide shares the history of Marrakech from its 1062 founding by the Almoravid dynasty. Gourmet riad lunch.

Day 3 | Journey to the Rocky Desert — Agafay Camel Safari & Rocky Ridge Sundowner

45-minute drive to the stark moon-like landscape of Agafay Desert — a rocky desert expanse offering a Sahara taste without long travel distances. Check-in at an exclusive luxury lodge with panoramic desert views and snow-capped Atlas Mountains as backdrop. Late afternoon private camel safari across the rugged Agafay plains. Premium cocktails and local delicacies from a private carpeted camp on a high rocky ridge watching the sun set over the distant Atlas.

Day 4 | Canyons & Quad Safaris — Stone Canyon Dinner

Private guided quad-biking or camel safari across rugged Agafay plains with the dramatic Atlas Mountains as backdrop. Exploring hidden canyons, dry riverbeds, and ancient Berber settlements. A traditional Berber village visit — mint tea and learning about traditional life in this landscape. The day culminates in a majestic open-air dinner around a roaring fire inside a secluded stone canyon, lit by hundreds of floating lanterns.

Snow-capped peaks filtering through morning light as the golden sun illuminates olive groves and Berber villages below. After landing: a hot farm-to-table breakfast served in an open mountain meadow. Afternoon drive into the lush Ourika Valley. Check-in at the ultra-luxury Kasbah Tamadot — a mountain fortress perched on a hilltop with panoramic valley views.

Journey Details

Minimum Age

No fixed minimum — varies by activity

  • No formal minimum — These touring itineraries carry no fixed minimum age. Hotels and riads across Morocco and Egypt welcome children, and several of our properties have connecting family rooms.
  • Hot-air balloon flights — Balloon operators over the Valley of the Kings and Marrakech generally require children to be at least seven, and tall enough to brace unaided in the basket on landing.
  • Inside the tombs and kasbahs — Some tomb chambers and kasbah towers are reached by steep ladders and low, unlit passages, and are unsuitable for very young children or anyone unsteady on their feet. Your guide will always offer an alternative.
  • Desert nights — Camel treks and desert camps are usually set at six years and above, largely because of the cold and the distance from a clinic.
  • Talk to us first — Limits vary from property to property and from activity to activity. Speak to our experts before you book, and we will pre-arrange the details with each hotel and riad around the ages of the children travelling with you.

Safari Duffel

Duffel, safari hat, vest and hooded jumper

  • Your duffel — A soft-sided {{brand}} duffel, sized for the 15 kg limit on the light aircraft that move you between camps. Rigid suitcases cannot be carried on those flights at all.
  • What is inside it — A branded {{brand}} safari hat, a safari vest, and a hooded jumper for cold dawn starts and balloon mornings.
  • Yours to keep — The bag and everything in it is a gift, not a loan. Nothing is returned at the end of your journey.
  • Packing around it — Soft luggage, neutral colours, and layers. Laundry is included at nearly every camp, which is why 15 kg goes further than it sounds.

Atlas Mountain routes can be affected by weather

Mountain passes and desert transfers are occasionally affected by winter snow (Dec–Feb) or summer heat (Jul–Aug) — your specialist will advise on the best seasonal routing for your dates.

  • Early starts — Touring days begin very early in the morning. Your guide will confirm what time you will be collected from your hotel.

  • Route changes — Your guide or driver may alter the route in certain circumstances, but every destination set out in the itinerary will be covered.

Documents

Visa-free for most nationalities

  • Passport — Valid for at least six months beyond your arrival date.
  • Visa — Morocco is visa-free for stays of up to 90 days for UK, EU, US, Canadian, Australian and most Commonwealth and Arab League passport holders. Other nationalities apply through the Moroccan e-Visa service.
  • Carry your passport on desert days — The road south to Erg Chebbi passes several police checkpoints where passport details are logged. Keep it on you rather than in a locked case.
  • Drones are prohibited — Morocco bans drones outright. They are routinely confiscated on arrival at the airport and are difficult to recover.
  • We file it for you — Whether you are flying in from overseas or travelling from elsewhere on the continent, we complete and submit your visa application on your behalf. You hand us your documents once. We do the filing, chase the approval, and carry the confirmations in your travel pack so nothing rests on you remembering a reference number at a counter.
  • Most of Africa is open to you — The great majority of our African destinations are now either visa-free or issue a visa on arrival, so an embassy queue is rarely in your future at all. Where a visa on arrival does apply, we prepare and lodge the paperwork ahead of you — you reach the desk with a finished file rather than a blank form and a biro.
  • Before you travel — Entry rules change frequently and without much notice. Your specialist confirms the current requirements for every passport in your party at the time of booking, and checks them again before you fly.

Guaranteed Departures

Private, tailor-made departures on your dates, or join a small group

  • Once confirmed, you travel — When your departure date is confirmed, it is guaranteed. We do not cancel, merge or reschedule a confirmed departure for commercial reasons, however the final numbers fall.
  • What confirms a departure — A departure becomes confirmed once the agreed number of travellers has been reached and paid for in line with the agreed payment schedule. Places held without payment do not count towards it.
  • If the minimum is not reached — Where a group departure has not reached its agreed numbers, we tell you early rather than late, and you choose: move to the next available date, continue at a revised small-group rate, or take a full refund of everything you have paid.
  • Solo journeys — Solo and tailor-made itineraries carry no group minimum at all. Your dates are confirmed the moment your deposit is received.
  • Visas — We prepare and submit your visa application on your behalf. Please note that the granting of a visa rests solely with the immigration authority of the country concerned, and is never guaranteed by us.
  • Medication — Anti-nausea and anti-anxiety tablets can be arranged for your journey. This is not a malaria-risk destination, so prophylaxis is not required. Any prescription medicine should be confirmed with your own doctor before you travel.
  • Not always hot — Despite the stereotype, North Africa moves through distinct seasons. Desert nights, mountain passes and pre-dawn starts can be genuinely cold. A hooded jumper is provided for cold conditions, and for guests joining a sunrise hot-air balloon flight.
  • Dietary requirements — If you have strict dietary needs, please tell us at the time of booking. This includes vegans, guests in recovery, non-drinkers, and anyone following a prescription from their doctor.
  • Travel insurance — We facilitate travel insurance for our clients. If you are already covered, please say so during booking so we can take it into account in your final quote.
  • Tailoring your quote — Some of our inclusions — a sunrise hot-air balloon flight, for example — may not be what you want. Point these out and we will adjust your final offer accordingly.
  • What to pack — Modest, breathable layers. Shoulders and knees covered at mosques, churches and through the older medinas, with a scarf for women entering a mosque. Desert nights and mountain passes turn genuinely cold even in summer, so pack a warm layer whatever the forecast says. Closed, broken-in shoes matter more than you would expect: medina lanes and tomb interiors are uneven, and often unlit.
  • Your travel duffel & kit — A {{brand}} duffel is provided ahead of departure, sized for charter and light-aircraft transfers where luggage is often limited to 15 kg per person. Inside it you will find a branded sun hat, a safari vest and a hooded jumper for cold desert mornings.
