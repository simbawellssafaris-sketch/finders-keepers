Itinerary

Kuala Lumpur

Day 1 | Arrival in Kuala Lumpur — Welcome to Malaysia

Upon arrival at Kuala Lumpur International Airport, a {{brand}} representative greets you in the arrivals hall and escorts you to a waiting private transfer vehicle. The drive carries you into the heart of Kuala Lumpur — past gleaming towers, busy markets, and the first glimpses of the Petronas Twin Towers rising above the city skyline. At the Grand Hyatt Kuala Lumpur, you are checked in immediately. The remainder of the afternoon is at leisure — rest, explore the hotel's facilities, or step outside to the nearby KLCC and Suria KLCC shopping mall. As evening falls, a welcome dinner is served at the hotel's signature restaurant, where your guide briefs you on the extraordinary days ahead.

Grand Hyatt Kuala Lumpur
Dinner

Day 2 | Kuala Lumpur — City Highlights

After breakfast, depart for a private city tour of Kuala Lumpur with your dedicated guide. The tour begins at the Petronas Twin Towers — for twenty-two years the tallest buildings on earth, and still the defining symbol of modern Malaysia. The skybridge connecting the two towers at the forty-first floor offers panoramic views of the city stretching to the horizon. From here, continue to Aquaria KLCC — one of Southeast Asia's most impressive aquariums, home to marine life from Malaysia's coastlines. The afternoon takes you to Bukit Bintang, Kuala Lumpur's premier shopping and entertainment district. Dinner is served at a carefully selected local restaurant showcasing the city's extraordinary culinary diversity — Malay, Chinese, Indian and Nyonya cuisines all within one vibrant block.

Grand Hyatt Kuala Lumpur
Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Day 3 | Kuala Lumpur — Batu Caves & Cultural Immersion

After breakfast, depart for the Batu Caves — one of Malaysia's most iconic landmarks, a series of limestone caves and Hindu temple shrines dedicated to Lord Murugan. A 272-step climb leads to the main cathedral cave, the interior soaring forty metres to the cave ceiling, light filtering through natural openings. The caves are also home to a colony of long-tailed macaques and a variety of cave-adapted bird species. After the caves, the afternoon is given over to further cultural exploration — Merdeka Square, the colonial Sultan Abdul Samad Building, the heritage Central Market, and the lively Petaling Street Chinatown where the smells of street food hang in the air. This evening, a final Kuala Lumpur dinner at the hotel. Tomorrow, the mountains.

Grand Hyatt Kuala Lumpur
Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner
Genting Highlands

Day 4 | Kuala Lumpur → Genting Highlands — The Hilltop Escape

After breakfast, check out of the Grand Hyatt and depart for Genting Highlands. The drive takes approximately ninety minutes, the road climbing steadily from the tropical lowlands through secondary rainforest to the cool mountain air at 1,800 metres above sea level. The temperature drops noticeably as you ascend — a welcome relief from the heat of the city below. Upon arrival, experience the Genting Highlands Cable Car — one of the world's fastest and longest gondola systems, sweeping passengers over a magnificent canopy of ancient dipterocarp rainforest, with views across the valleys to distant mountain ridges. Check into Crockfords — Genting's most exclusive and private hotel — where butler service, spacious suites and mountain views set the tone for the evening. Dinner at one of the resort's fine dining restaurants, high above the clouds.

Crockfords Genting
Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner
Cameron Highlands

Day 5 | Genting Highlands → Cameron Highlands — The Tea Country

After breakfast, check out of Crockfords and depart for Cameron Highlands. The drive takes approximately three hours, winding through mountain roads past small towns and villages, the landscape changing from tropical to temperate. As you enter the Cameron Highlands, the hills reveal their famous green carpet — row upon row of tea bushes stretching to every horizon, a landscape that feels more like a painting than a place. Your first stop is a working tea estate, where a guide walks you through the fields explaining cultivation, harvesting and processing. You watch the pickers at work. You learn to distinguish the different grades of leaf. A full tea tasting follows, the range of flavours extraordinary from a single plant. Lunch is served at the estate. Check-in at Cameron Highlands Resort — a colonial-era property set among the gardens and hills. Dinner at the resort restaurant.

Cameron Highlands Resort
Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Day 6 | Cameron Highlands — Tea Estates, Strawberry Farms & Nature

A full day given over to the Cameron Highlands and its extraordinary highland atmosphere. After breakfast at the resort, depart for a morning on the tea plantations — a deeper exploration than yesterday, walking further into the fields, understanding the seasonal cycles, tasting again with a more educated palate. From the tea estate, continue to a strawberry farm where guests can pick their own berries from the rows of raised beds, the fruit sweet and heavy from the cool mountain air. A flower garden follows — Cameron Highlands is famous for its cultivated flowers, orchids and temperate blooms thriving in the mountain climate. The afternoon is at leisure. Walk the highland trails, explore the resort's gardens, take tea on the veranda as mist rolls across the hills. Dinner at the resort's restaurant. The night air is cool enough for a blanket.

Cameron Highlands Resort
Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner
Penang

Day 7 | Cameron Highlands → Penang — The Pearl of the Orient

After breakfast, check out of Cameron Highlands Resort and depart for Penang. The drive takes approximately three and a half hours, descending from the cool highlands through the foothills, across the coastal plains, and over the Penang Bridge — one of the longest bridges in Southeast Asia, stretching four and a half kilometres across the Strait of Malacca. The island announces itself through its architecture — Chinese shophouses, colonial buildings, temple rooftops visible from the bridge. Your destination is the Eastern & Oriental Hotel, one of Malaysia's most legendary properties, its white facade rising above the George Town waterfront. Known throughout Asia simply as "the E&O," the hotel has hosted Somerset Maugham, Noel Coward and generations of travellers. Check in. The remainder of the afternoon is at leisure. Walk to the waterfront. Watch the ferries cross to Butterworth. Dinner at the hotel.

Eastern & Oriental Hotel, Penang
Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Day 8 | Penang — George Town UNESCO Heritage Tour

After breakfast, depart for a private heritage tour of George Town — a UNESCO World Heritage Site since 2008 and one of Southeast Asia's most culturally layered cities. Your guide walks you through the UNESCO-listed core zone, explaining the history behind every facade. The heritage shophouses were built by immigrant Chinese clans who arrived from Fujian and Guangdong provinces — their architecture a fusion of Southern Chinese and European styles unique to this part of the world. At Khoo Kongsi, one of Penang's most magnificent clan houses, you enter a courtyard of extraordinary carved stonework and painted ceilings. At the Pinang Peranakan Mansion, the opulent home of a nineteenth-century Straits Chinese tycoon reveals itself room by room. Fort Cornwallis marks the spot where Francis Light landed in 1786. Lunch is served at a local restaurant — Penang's famous char kway teow, nasi kandar and assam laksa. In the evening, street food at the famous hawker stalls of Gurney Drive — considered by food writers among the finest outdoor eating in Asia.

Eastern & Oriental Hotel, Penang
Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner
Langkawi

Day 9 | Penang → Langkawi — Island Arrival

After breakfast, transfer to Penang International Airport for your domestic flight to Langkawi — a short thirty-minute journey over the Strait of Malacca. Below, the sea shifts from green to deep turquoise, and then the 99 islands of the Langkawi archipelago appear, rising from the Andaman Sea in great limestone formations draped with rainforest. Upon arrival at Langkawi International Airport, a private transfer takes you through the island's interior — past rice paddies, fishing villages and rainforest — to The Ritz-Carlton Langkawi, a property set in a beachfront rainforest that is unlike any conventional beach resort. Suites and villas nestle among the trees at the forest edge, looking out to the Andaman Sea. Check in. The remainder of the day is entirely at leisure — swim in the Andaman Sea, rest on the beach, explore the property's forest. Dinner at the hotel's beachfront restaurant as the sky turns violet over the islands.

The Ritz-Carlton Langkawi
Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Day 10 | Langkawi — Sky Bridge, Island Hopping & Mangrove Safari

A full day devoted to Langkawi's natural wonders. The morning begins with the Langkawi Cable Car, ascending to the summit of Gunung Mat Cincang — the cable car one of the steepest in the world, the views from the top stretching across the archipelago to Thailand. At the summit, the Sky Bridge awaits — a curved pedestrian bridge suspended high above the rainforest canopy at 700 metres, the Andaman Sea visible on both sides, the forest canopy a sea of green below your feet. From the Sky Bridge, descend and board a private boat for island hopping — visiting the beaches of Pulau Beras Basah and the caves of Pulau Dayang Bunting, swimming in waters so clear the sea floor is visible twelve metres below. Lunch is served on a beach. In the late afternoon, a mangrove safari by smaller boat through the channels of Kilim Karst Geoforest Park — watching brahminy kites and white-bellied sea eagles soaring above, monitor lizards on the banks, the channels narrowing between ancient limestone karsts. Return to the hotel. A farewell dinner at the Ritz-Carlton under the stars.

The Ritz-Carlton Langkawi
Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner
Kuala Lumpur — Departure

Day 11 | Langkawi → Kuala Lumpur — A Final Morning & Departure

After a final breakfast at The Ritz-Carlton, a morning at leisure on Langkawi — one last swim, one last walk along the beach, one last look at the Andaman Sea stretching to the horizon. At the scheduled time, transfer to Langkawi International Airport for the domestic flight back to Kuala Lumpur, approximately one hour over the sea and the Malaysian peninsula. Upon arrival at Kuala Lumpur International Airport, a {{brand}} representative meets you and transfers you to a day room at Grand Hyatt Kuala Lumpur, where the hotel's full facilities are available until your international departure time. Freshen up, rest, take a final meal, and reflect on eleven days that moved from the towers of the capital to the mountains of the highlands, from the heritage streets of Penang to the islands of the Andaman Sea. A {{brand}} representative transfers you to the airport for your international departure. Until next time.

Documents

Visa-free for most nationalities

  • Passport — Valid for at least six months beyond your arrival date, with at least two blank pages for stamps.
  • Visa — Visa-free for up to 90 days for UK, EU, US, Canadian and Australian passport holders, and 30 days for Chinese and Indian nationals.
  • Digital Arrival Card — The MDAC must be submitted online no more than three days before you land. It is free and takes a few minutes.
  • Sabah and Sarawak — Both states run their own immigration. You clear passport control again on arrival from Peninsular Malaysia even on a domestic flight, so keep your passport in hand luggage.
  • We handle the paperwork — We complete and submit your visa, eVisa or travel-authorisation applications on your behalf. You supply the documents once; we do the filing, track the approval and hold the confirmations for you.
  • Where you must appear in person — A few consulates insist on biometrics or a short interview that nobody can sit on your behalf — China requires fingerprinting for some nationalities, as do European and United States visas. We still prepare the entire file; we simply book you a single appointment and brief you beforehand so one visit settles it.
  • Before you travel — Entry rules change frequently and without much notice. Your specialist confirms the current requirements for every passport in your party at the time of booking, and checks them again before you fly.
