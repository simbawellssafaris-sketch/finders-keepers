Overview

Nineteen
Twenty-Five

Two hundred and eight rooms in the most prestigious address in the Croatian capital.

The Esplanade opened in 1925 for one specific reason: the Orient Express stopped in Zagreb, and its passengers needed somewhere worthy of the journey. Designed in Art Deco by the architect Dionis Sunko, it became the symbol of luxury in the city almost immediately and has been a haven for film stars, politicians, singers and artists ever since. The 208 rooms and suites carry Art Deco detailing and marble-clad bathrooms, with lavish fabrics, original furniture, historic photographs and a palette of beige, cream and violet.

A Michelin-starred kitchen. Zinfandel’s, the hotel’s principal restaurant, holds Michelin recognition — and its lineage runs straight back to the Orient Express dining car.
