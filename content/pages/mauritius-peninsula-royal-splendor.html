Itinerary

Belle Mare, East Coast

Day 1 | Nairobi → One&Only Le Saint Géran — Arrival at the Royal Peninsula

VIP handling at Jomo Kenyatta International Airport, then a direct flight to Sir Seewoosagur Ramgoolam International Airport in Mauritius. VIP tarmac greeting, private luxury SUV transfer to the East Coast.

Check into the legendary One&Only Le Saint Géran — an iconic palatial estate tucked between a calm turquoise lagoon and the open Indian Ocean. Private guided tour of the East Coast if arriving before 4:00 PM. Welcome dinner at the resort.

One&Only Le Saint Géran, Belle Mare
Dinner

Day 2 | Private Lagoon Water-Skiing & Coral Reef Snorkelling

Step from the suite's private deck onto a luxury speedboat for a morning of water-skiing on the glassy Belle Mare lagoon. After, snorkelling over the vibrant Belle Mare Coral Reef Gardens — clownfish, sea turtles, and tropical reef fish moving through coral formations.

Your guide explains the reef ecology. Afternoon at leisure at the resort. Gourmet dinner.

One&Only Le Saint Géran
Breakfast, Dinner
Port Louis

Day 3 | UNESCO Ruins, Imperial Bazaar & Citadel Sundowner

Drive to Port Louis. The UNESCO-listed Aapravasi Ghat stone ruins with a private historian — the arrival point for indentured labourers from India in the 19th century. Then the Port Louis Central Bazaar, its stalls dense with spices, textiles, and Creole crafts.

As the light fades, Mauritian spiced rums and local snacks (Roti, Millionaire's Salad) served from a private overlook at Citadel Fort, watching the harbour lights awaken — a perfect sundowner. Return for gourmet dinner.

One&Only Le Saint Géran
Breakfast, Dinner
Pamplemousses

Day 4 | Giant Waterlilies & Sugar Estate History

Private morning tour of Sir Seewoosagur Ramgoolam Botanical Garden at Pamplemousses — the giant circular Amazonica waterlily ponds, extraordinary tropical palms, and spice collections.

Then L'Aventure du Sucre — the island's sugar factory museum, tracing Mauritius' most defining economic history. Return to the resort for a relaxed evening.

One&Only Le Saint Géran
Breakfast, Dinner
Ile aux Cerfs

Day 5 | Ile aux Cerfs Catamaran & Bonfire Dinner

Board a private 55-foot luxury catamaran heading south to Ile aux Cerfs, sailing through the spectacular turquoise shades of the eastern lagoon. Swimming and snorkelling at secluded coves.

A magnificent seafood barbecue bonfire dinner with fresh local lobster on a private beach cove, warmed by a roaring bonfire pit under hanging lanterns. Return to the resort.

One&Only Le Saint Géran
Breakfast, Dinner
Mahebourg

Day 6 | Mahebourg — The Cradle of Mauritian History

Travel south to Mahebourg to explore Vieux Grand Port — the site of the 1810 naval battle that gave Mauritius its coat of arms. A private crowd-free tour of the 18th-century National Naval Museum, its artifacts and colonial architecture completely undisturbed.

The historic waterfront with its colonial buildings and bay views. Local market with fresh produce and island crafts. Return to the resort for dinner.

One&Only Le Saint Géran
Breakfast, Dinner

Day 7 | Helicopter Island Loop & Disappearing Sandbar Breakfast

Rising before dawn, a private helicopter flight looping over the mountain peaks of the east and the intricate web of barrier reefs — the turquoise lagoon, coral formations, and green mountains from above.

After landing, a white-linen hot breakfast buffet set up by the private crew on a completely isolated sandbar that disappears with the rising tide — dining in absolute privacy surrounded by turquoise water. Relaxed afternoon at the resort.

One&Only Le Saint Géran
Breakfast, Dinner

Day 8 | Barrier Reef Deep Sea Walk

A specialised glass diving helmet allows you to walk along the sandy ocean floor of the outer barrier reef — face-to-glass with schools of blue-fin trevally and giant anemone beds without getting your hair wet.

Your guide leads you along the reef explaining the coral ecosystem. Afternoon at leisure. Gourmet dinner.

One&Only Le Saint Géran
Breakfast, Dinner

Day 9 | Exclusive Creole Sega Cultural Evening

A private vehicle to a remote coastal village to spend the day with indigenous Creole master musicians and instrument crafters — learning the history of hand-stretching goat-hide over Ravanne drums and Maravanne rattles.

A private hypnotic evening of traditional Sega music and oral storytelling around a grand beachside bonfire. Celebratory farewell dinner at the resort paired with vintage wines.

One&Only Le Saint Géran
Breakfast, Dinner

Day 10 | Return to Nairobi — Departure

A relaxed luxury breakfast watching the sunrise over the peninsula. Check out, chauffeur transfer back to Sir Seewoosagur Ramgoolam International Airport, and international flight back to Nairobi.

Complementary spa session at a city hotel upon arrival if time permits before the onward flight home.

Note

Cyclone season falls between November and April

While rare, tropical storms are most likely between November and April; the drier, cooler months of May to October offer the most reliable weather for outdoor activities and water sports.

Documents

Visa-free for most nationalities

  • Passport — Valid for at least six months beyond your arrival date, with at least two blank pages for stamps.
  • Visa — Visa-free on arrival for up to 90 days for UK, EU, US, Canadian, Australian, Indian, South African and most African and Commonwealth passport holders.
  • All-in-One Travel Form — Every traveller completes the Mauritius All-in-One Travel Form online before departure. It combines the immigration, customs and health declarations into one submission.
  • Strict biosecurity — Mauritius protects its agriculture closely. Declare all food, plant material, seeds and untreated wooden items; undeclared goods attract on-the-spot fines.
  • We handle the paperwork — We complete and submit your visa, eVisa or travel-authorisation applications on your behalf. You supply the documents once; we do the filing, track the approval and hold the confirmations for you.
  • Where you must appear in person — A few consulates insist on biometrics or a short interview that nobody can sit on your behalf — China requires fingerprinting for some nationalities, as do European and United States visas. We still prepare the entire file; we simply book you a single appointment and brief you beforehand so one visit settles it.
  • Before you travel — Entry rules change frequently and without much notice. Your specialist confirms the current requirements for every passport in your party at the time of booking, and checks them again before you fly.
