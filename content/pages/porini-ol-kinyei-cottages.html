Overview

Wilderness,
Held in Trust

Ol Kinyei was the first community conservancy of its kind in the Mara — an 18,700-acre wildlife sanctuary leased entirely to Porini, so no other operator's vehicles ever enter its boundaries.

Tucked away on a hillside above a wildlife-rich dam, the four Ol Kinyei Safari Cottages are the newest and most private way to experience this conservancy — a "home away from home" built for families and small groups who want the run of their own four-bedroom house, private chef included, without giving up an inch of the wilderness outside the door. Ol Kinyei sits at the northernmost edge of the Loita wildebeest migration, with calving typically seen every February and March, and carries a resident pride of over twenty lions along with regular leopard and cheetah sightings.

One of three Porini addresses in Ol Kinyei. The Safari Cottages share the conservancy with the six-tent Porini Mara Camp and the six-tent Porini Cheetah Camp — all three reserved exclusively for Porini guests, with no other tourist vehicles permitted inside the 18,700-acre boundary.

Location

The Loita
Migration's Edge

Ol Kinyei sits at the top of the Serengeti-Mara ecosystem, where open savannah plains meet rolling hills, springs, streams and rivers — and where the Loita wildebeest migration calves every year.

  • 18,700 acres set aside in 2005 as the first community conservancy of its kind in the Mara ecosystem.
  • A recognised IUCN Green List site — one of a handful worldwide meeting the standard for well-managed protected areas.
  • Vehicles are limited to one per 1,400 acres, keeping sightings uncrowded even in high season.
  • Every cottage stay pays directly into the community land-lease that keeps Ol Kinyei protected.
Fly

Transfer

A short drive from the airstrip brings guests directly into the conservancy and up to the hillside cottages.

Combine

The Safari Cottages pair easily with a stay at Porini Mara or Porini Cheetah Camp for a longer circuit.
