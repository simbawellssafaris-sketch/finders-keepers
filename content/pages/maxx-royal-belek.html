Overview

Every Stay
Is a Suite

Suites and villas on the Turkish Riviera, with a European Tour golf course attached.

Maxx Royal Belek is an all-suite resort on the Mediterranean coast at Belek, with generous living space and views to either the sea or the parkland. The golf is the headline: an 18-hole par-72 course of 6,522 metres designed by Ryder Cup captain Colin Montgomerie with European Golf Design, which hosted Turkey’s inaugural European Tour event in 2013 and returned in 2014, 2015 and 2019. The back nine is floodlit for night golf. Fourteen distinct dining venues include three gourmet restaurants and ten bars.

Your concierge is on your phone. The Maxx Assistant service runs through a chat app, handling restaurant bookings, pillow menu requests, room fragrance and anything else, without a trip to a desk.
