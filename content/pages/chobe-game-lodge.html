Overview

A Pioneer,
Since 1972

Chobe Game Lodge opened in 1972 as Botswana's first five-star lodge and remains the only property built inside Chobe National Park's boundary, right on the banks of the Chobe River.

The Moorish-inspired architecture — high arches, barrel-vaulted ceilings, arched walkways and palm-shaded courtyards — gives the lodge a sense of grandeur that has drawn royalty and celebrities for over fifty years, including the wedding of Richard Burton and Elizabeth Taylor in 1975. In 2014, Chobe Game Lodge introduced the first solar-powered boats and electric safari vehicles in the region, and its guiding team is entirely female — one of the only all-women teams of its kind in Africa. The Chobe River itself supports the world's densest population of Kalahari elephants, alongside significant herds of buffalo and zebra.

An Ecotourism-Certified icon. Recently refurbished throughout, Chobe Game Lodge holds an Ecotourism Certification and remains part of the original wave of Botswana safari lodges that helped define the country's high-value, low-impact tourism model.

Location

Inside Chobe
National Park

Chobe Game Lodge sits directly within Chobe National Park, roughly 100km west of Victoria Falls, on the banks of a river renowned for its concentration of elephants.

  • The only permanent lodge built inside Chobe National Park's boundary, on the shore of the Chobe River.
  • Chobe River supports the world's densest population of Kalahari elephants, alongside large herds of buffalo and zebra.
  • Predator populations here include lion, leopard, wild dog and cheetah, all regularly sighted.
  • Roughly 100km from Victoria Falls, making it a natural pairing with a Zambia or Zimbabwe extension.
