Overview

Coron's First
Eco-Luxury Island

Set on the Malaroyroy Peninsula of Bulalacao Island, 22 kilometres south of Coron Town, Two Seasons was Coron's first eco-friendly luxury island resort, entirely self-sustaining and built as a turtle and giant clam sanctuary.

The peninsula's narrow shape means guests are never more than a few metres from water on either side — look east for sunrise, west for sunset, with a 200-metre beach facing each direction and a sandbar connecting to a neighbouring islet. Because the island sits far from any utility grid, Two Seasons runs its own desalination plant, sewage treatment facility and solar water heating, producing 80 cubic metres of fresh water daily as a zero-waste-discharge operation. Ten bungalow and casita-style accommodations, from Mountain and Seaview to Hilltop and Island Tip categories, are spread through lush tropical gardens with high ceilings and warm contemporary Filipino design, while the resort's Pawikan Aqua Sports Center and Molokini transparent kayaks make the vibrant house reef immediately accessible.

A genuinely off-grid operation. Every drop of fresh water, all electricity and all waste treatment on the island is produced and managed on-site — a rare degree of self-sufficiency for a resort of this scale.

Experiences

Wreck Diving,

Coron's famous WWII shipwrecks and vibrant reefs sit within reach of the resort's own dive centre and water sports programme.

Diving

Coron's WWII Wrecks

Legendary Japanese shipwreck dive sites, among the most celebrated wreck diving destinations in Southeast Asia, reachable by boat from the resort.

On the Water

Molokini Transparent Kayaks

Clear-bottomed kayaks let guests see directly into the reef below while paddling around the peninsula.

Conservation

Turtle & Giant Clam Sanctuary

The resort's house reef doubles as a protected sanctuary for sea turtles and giant clams, visible right off the beach.

Water Sports

Pawikan Aqua Sports Center

Windsurfing, banana boating and a full range of non-motorised water sports are based at the resort's own sports centre.

Wellness

Hilltop Spa

Perched on a small peak with impressive views, the spa offers massage and treatments overlooking the surrounding sea.

Excursions

Island Hopping Tours

Guided tours to Coron's other famous lagoons and lakes, including Kayangan Lake and Twin Lagoon, arranged through the resort.

Location

Bulalacao Island,
South of Coron Town

Two Seasons sits on the Malaroyroy Peninsula of Bulalacao Island, roughly 22 kilometres, or a scenic boat ride, south of Coron Town, Palawan.

  • Located on Bulalacao Island's Malaroyroy Peninsula, 22km south of Coron Town.
  • Reached by scenic boat transfer from Coron Town, included with every reservation.
  • Roughly 80 minutes' drive from Francisco B. Reyes Airport to the boat departure point.
  • A self-contained, off-grid island with no other settlements nearby.
Fly

Francisco B. Reyes Airport in Busuanga is the main gateway, connecting to Manila and Cebu.

Drive & Boat

An 80-minute drive from the airport reaches Coron Town, followed by a scenic boat transfer to the island.
