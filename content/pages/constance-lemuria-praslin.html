Overview

The Only Golf
in the Seychelles

A hundred and five suites and villas across a peninsula bordered by three beaches.

Constance Lemuria occupies a peninsula on Praslin’s northwestern coast, and it did something no other resort in the Seychelles managed: it secured two of the island’s finest beaches. Anse Kerlan runs directly along the property, and Anse Georgette — regularly named among the most beautiful beaches in the world — can only be reached through the resort grounds or by sea. Between them sits Petite Anse Kerlan. The 18-hole championship course is the only one in the country, weaving along the shoreline before climbing above the treetops.

Turtles nest on the beach here. Grand Anse Kerlan is an active sea turtle nesting site, and the resort runs a conservation programme with daily beach patrols and hatchling assistance through the season.

Golf

The 18-Hole Championship Course

The only golf course in the Seychelles, running along the shoreline then climbing above the treetops. Complimentary for guests.

Beach

Anse Georgette

Reached on foot through the golf course or by buggy, and effectively private. Among the most beautiful beaches anywhere.

Nature

A short drive away — the UNESCO-listed palm forest where the Coco de Mer grows and the black parrot survives.

Location

Anse Kerlan,
Praslin

The northwestern corner of the Seychelles’ second-largest island.

  • Set on a peninsula on Praslin’s northwest coast, bordered by three beaches.
  • Anse Georgette is accessible only through the resort grounds or by sea.
  • A short drive from the UNESCO-listed Vallée de Mai and Anse Lazio.
  • Boat crossings to La Digue and Curieuse leave from nearby Baie Ste Anne.
