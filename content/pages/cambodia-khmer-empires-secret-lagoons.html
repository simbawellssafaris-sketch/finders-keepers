Itinerary

Siem Reap, Cambodia

Day 1 | Nairobi → Siem Reap — Arrival at the Royal Estate

VIP handling at Nairobi's Jomo Kenyatta Airport, international flight to Siem Reap-Angkor International Airport (SAI). VIP tarmac reception, private luxury SUV to the estate.

Check into Amansara — Pool Suite — historically used as the guest villa of King Norodom Sihanouk, its private lotus pond at the centre of everything. Private guided tour of Siem Reap if arriving before 4:00 PM. Elegant welcome dinner overlooking the lotus pond.

Amansara, Siem Reap (Pool Suite)
Dinner
Angkor Wat

Day 2 | Dawn over Angkor Wat

Rising before dawn to experience Angkor Wat as the sun rises behind the iconic five towers — their reflection in the lotus pond creating one of the world's most extraordinary sights. A private historian leads you inside the monumental stone galleries before the public gates fully open.

The world's longest continuous bas-relief stone carvings, depicting scenes from the Ramayana and Mahabharata, illuminated by the morning light. Afternoon at leisure at the estate. Gourmet dinner.

Amansara
Breakfast, Dinner
Angkor Thom & Ta Prohm

Day 3 | Bayon's Stone Faces & Ta Prohm Roots

The giant, serene stone faces of Bayon Temple inside Angkor Thom — 216 massive stone faces of Avalokiteshvara, each with a serene smile, rising above the jungle canopy. Then Ta Prohm — the mandatory sight of massive silk-cotton tree roots wrapping completely around the collapsed stone doorways.

Chilled champagne and local lemongrass-infused canapés from a private wooden boat drifting along the ancient moat of Angkor Thom at sunset. Return for gourmet dinner.

Amansara
Breakfast, Dinner
Beng Mealea

Day 4 | Hidden Gem — Beng Mealea

An early luxury SUV to the hidden gem of Beng Mealea — a massive 12th-century sandstone temple where giant vines and trees have completely reclaimed the sandstone blocks. A private wooden boardwalk suspended over the collapsed galleries, walking in total silence.

The temple remains largely unrestored, offering one of the most authentic glimpses of the jungle's power over human architecture in Southeast Asia. Return for a relaxed evening.

Amansara
Breakfast, Dinner
Tonle Sap Lake

Day 5 | Tonle Sap Floating Villages & Mangrove Canoeing

Drive south to the massive Tonle Sap Lake — Southeast Asia's largest freshwater lake. A private luxury speedboat navigates past the towering stilt houses of Kompong Phluk, the floating village rising on tall wooden stilts above the water.

A private canoe through the flooded mangrove forest — birds in the roots, the water perfectly still, the forest completely silent. A white-linen hot breakfast served inside a luxury wooden pavilion floating on a quiet river channel. Return for dinner.

Amansara
Breakfast, Dinner

Day 6 | Tethered Balloon & Banteay Srei Pink Sandstone

Before dawn, a tethered balloon flight ascending smoothly over the Western Baray reservoir — the geometric outlines of ancient Khmer water management canals stretching across the landscape below.

In the afternoon, the 10th-century temple of Banteay Srei — exceptionally intricate pink sandstone carvings dedicated to Shiva, considered some of the finest in all Angkorian art. Return for a relaxed evening.

Amansara
Breakfast, Dinner
Phnom Kulen

Day 7 | Phnom Kulen — The River of a Thousand Lingas

Hike the shaded jungle paths of sacred Kulen Mountain — the River of a Thousand Lingas, sacred symbols carved directly into the rocky riverbed beneath crystal-clear flowing water, the spiritual birthplace of the Angkorian empire.

The reclining Buddha statue and beautiful waterfalls of this sacred mountain. Return for gourmet dinner.

Amansara
Breakfast, Dinner

Day 8 | Forest Spa & Torch-Lit Khmer Banquet

A peaceful day at the estate — traditional massage using local organic coconut oil and Cambodian herbs by skilled therapists.

The day culminates in a grand torch-lit Khmer banquet on a private open-air deck surrounded by stone ruins, warmed by a roaring central fire pit.

Amansara
Breakfast, Dinner

Day 9 | Exclusive Khmer Silk Weaving Heritage Tour

A rural atelier with indigenous master silk weavers — the deep history of extracting natural dyes from tree bark, hand-looming traditional Hol patterned silks worn by royalty.

A private performance of historic Pinpeat folk string music and oral storytelling. Celebratory farewell dinner at Amansara paired with vintage wines.

Amansara
Breakfast, Dinner

Day 10 | Return to Nairobi — Departure

A relaxed luxury breakfast watching the water lilies bloom. Smooth chauffeur transfer to Siem Reap-Angkor International Airport. Complementary spa session at a city hotel if time permits. International flight back to Nairobi.

Travel Kit

Duffel, sun hat, rain shell and day pack

  • Your duffel — A soft-sided {{brand}} duffel, sized for domestic flights and easier than a hard case on train platforms, ferry jetties and old-town lanes.
  • What is inside it — A wide-brim sun hat, a lightweight packable rain shell for tropical downpours, and a foldable day pack for temple mornings and market afternoons.
  • Yours to keep — The bag and everything in it is a gift, not a loan.
  • Packing around it — Slip-on shoes save time at temples, where you remove them repeatedly, and one set of clothing covering shoulders and knees is essential for religious sites.

Note

Temple visits are best planned around the heat

Angkor Archaeological Park is at its most comfortable in the early morning — most itineraries schedule temple visits for sunrise or late afternoon to avoid the midday heat.

  • Early starts — The best of this journey happens at dawn — before the heat, the crowds and the haze. Your guide will confirm what time you will be collected from your hotel.
  • Tailoring this journey — If you would like this package with more or fewer days, arranged for a group, a family or a solo traveller, or with add-ons, reach out and we will tailor it for you.
  • Route changes — Your guide or driver may alter the route in certain circumstances, but every destination set out in the itinerary will be covered.

Documents

Visa on arrival or e-Visa, from $30

  • Passport — Valid for at least six months beyond your arrival date, with at least one blank page.
  • Visa — The Type T tourist eVisa costs US$30 for a 30-day single entry; a visa on arrival is also available at Phnom Penh and Siem Reap. ASEAN nationals enter free.
  • e-Arrival Card — Since January 2025 every air arrival must submit the Cambodia e-Arrival Card within the seven days before landing.
  • Your Angkor pass — Angkor passes carry your photograph, taken on site, cannot be transferred, and are checked at every temple gate. Your guide arranges this on your first morning.
  • Temple dress — Shoulders and knees must be covered to climb to Angkor Wat’s upper level. Visitors are turned away for this daily.
  • We handle the paperwork — We complete and submit your visa, eVisa or travel-authorisation applications on your behalf. You supply the documents once; we do the filing, track the approval and hold the confirmations for you.
  • Where you must appear in person — A few consulates insist on biometrics or a short interview that nobody can sit on your behalf — China requires fingerprinting for some nationalities, as do European and United States visas. We still prepare the entire file; we simply book you a single appointment and brief you beforehand so one visit settles it.
  • Before you travel — Entry rules change frequently and without much notice. Your specialist confirms the current requirements for every passport in your party at the time of booking, and checks them again before you fly.
