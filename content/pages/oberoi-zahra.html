Overview

An Oasis
of Calm

With just 27 cabins and suites, The Oberoi Zahra is one of the most spacious and private ways to see Upper Egypt, evoking the glamour of old-world Nile cruising.

Timber floors, elegant interiors and large picture windows fill every cabin, most spread over more than 26 square metres — generous by the standards of any Nile cruiser. Private docking at each stop lets guests bypass crowded landings entirely, while an onboard spa with four dedicated therapy rooms, each with its own steam room, remains unmatched on the river. Sailing between Luxor and Aswan on 3-, 4-, 6- or 8-night itineraries, the Zahra threads together Karnak, the Valley of the Kings, Kom Ombo, Edfu and Philae in an unhurried, boutique-scale journey.

A sister ship to the Oberoi Philae. The Oberoi Zahra sails alongside its sister vessel, the Oberoi Philae, both part of the Oberoi Hotels & Resorts fleet on the Nile — this page covers the Zahra alone.

Experiences

Temples,
Tombs & the Aswan High Dam

Every itinerary threads together Upper Egypt's essential sites, from Karnak's colossal columns to the rock-cut grandeur of Philae.

Luxor

Karnak & Luxor Temples

Guided visits to the largest religious complex ever built and the temple that once hosted ancient Opet Festival processions.

West Bank

Valley of the Kings

A guided walk through the royal tombs of Thebes, including the resting places of some of Egypt's best-known pharaohs.

Kom Ombo

The Twin Temple

A rare double temple honouring Sobek and Horus the Elder side by side, with ancient medical instruments carved into its walls.

Edfu

Temple of Horus

One of Egypt's best-preserved temples, reached by horse-drawn carriage from the riverbank.

Aswan

Philae Temple & the High Dam

A boat crossing to Agilkia Island for the Temple of Isis, followed by a look at the engineering feat that tamed the Nile.

Onboard

Astronomy Lectures & a Cigar Lounge

Evenings bring astronomy talks, a games room and a cigar lounge, alongside the ship's library and theatre.

Location

Luxor
to Aswan

The Zahra's route follows the Nile's most storied stretch, connecting the temples of ancient Thebes to Aswan's granite quarries and island temples.

  • A 3-, 4-, 6- or 8-night route between Luxor and Aswan, roughly 240km apart by river.
  • Stops include Esna, Edfu and Kom Ombo, each home to a major temple complex.
  • Private docking at each port lets guests avoid the crowds at busier public landings.
  • Complimentary pickup and drop-off is included from the airport, train station or East Bank hotels in Luxor and Aswan.
Fly

Scheduled flights connect Cairo to both Luxor and Aswan airports, close to each embarkation point.

Embark

Guests are met and transferred directly to the ship, with lunch served shortly after boarding.

Disembark

Cruises end with a final breakfast onboard before transfer to the airport or onward journey.
