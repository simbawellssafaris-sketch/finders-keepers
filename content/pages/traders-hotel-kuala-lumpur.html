Overview

Face to Face
with the Twin Towers

Standing directly opposite the Petronas Twin Towers in the heart of KLCC, Traders Hotel Kuala Lumpur is best known for one thing above all: its rooftop SkyBar infinity pool, angled to put the Towers squarely in your line of sight from the water.

A Shangri-La Group hotel with a more relaxed, contemporary feel than its five-star neighbours, Traders trades old-world formality for clean, functional design and unbeatable positioning — every guest is a short covered walk from Suria KLCC, the Kuala Lumpur Convention Centre and the base of the Towers themselves. Rooms are efficient and comfortable rather than opulent, with floor-to-ceiling windows in the higher categories that frame the skyline directly from bed. It's the hotel regulars return to specifically for the SkyBar view, and for how easy everything else in the Golden Triangle is to reach on foot.

Book a Twin Tower View Room. Only a portion of rooms face the Towers directly — request this category at booking, as it makes a meaningful difference to the experience and is often the entire reason guests choose this address.

Experiences

The SkyBar,
and the Towers Beyond

Traders' address does the heavy lifting — everything in KLCC's Golden Triangle is reachable on foot, with the hotel's own rooftop the best vantage point of all.

Signature

SkyBar Infinity Pool

A 33rd-floor infinity-edge pool and bar angled directly at the Petronas Twin Towers — one of the most photographed views in Kuala Lumpur, especially at sunset.

Iconic

Petronas Twin Towers

The Towers and their skybridge stand directly across from the hotel, reachable in minutes on foot.

Nature

KLCC Park

A landscaped park with a musical fountain and jogging track sits between the hotel and the Towers, a pleasant walk either way.

Shopping

Suria KLCC & Pavilion KL

Connected by a covered walkway, Suria KLCC and, a little further on foot, Pavilion Kuala Lumpur's Bukit Bintang shopping strip.

Wellness

Amrita Spa

A Shangri-La Group spa offering traditional Malay and pan-Asian treatments a short lift ride from the pool deck.

Business

KL Convention Centre

Directly adjoining the hotel, making Traders a favoured base for conference and business travellers alongside leisure guests.

Location

KLCC,
Opposite the Towers

Traders sits in the Golden Triangle district directly across from the Petronas Twin Towers, connected to Suria KLCC and the Kuala Lumpur Convention Centre.

  • Located on Jalan Binjai, KLCC, directly facing the Petronas Twin Towers across KLCC Park.
  • A covered walkway connects the hotel to Suria KLCC Shopping Centre and the Convention Centre.
  • Roughly 15 minutes' walk to Pavilion Kuala Lumpur and the Bukit Bintang nightlife district.
  • KLCC LRT station is a short walk from the hotel.
Fly

Kuala Lumpur International Airport (KLIA) is roughly 45 minutes from the hotel by car.

Rail

KLCC LRT station is a short walk from the hotel's Suria KLCC-facing entrance.
