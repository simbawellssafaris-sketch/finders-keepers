Your Itinerary

Stone Town

Day 1 | Arrival in Stone Town — Sultanate Welcome

VIP tarmac welcome at Abeid Amani Karume Airport. Private luxury SUV into the heart of the historic capital. Check-in at Park Hyatt Zanzibar — elegant oceanfront palace blending Swahili architecture with modern luxury. Indian Ocean views and the serene beauty of the historic harbour. Early arrivals explore Stone Town's narrow winding streets with carved wooden doors and historic waterfront. Welcome dinner at the hotel.

Day 2 | Old Sultanate Palaces & Forodhani Gardens

Private guided exploration of the old sultanate palaces and historic Forodhani Gardens: the Sultan's Palace — a 19th-century residence of the Sultan of Zanzibar with elegant architecture and stunning ocean views. The House of Wonders: once the tallest building in East Africa. The Forodhani Gardens: a historic waterfront park with beautiful gardens and harbour views. The Old Fort with its massive coral-stone walls and panoramic city views.

Eastern Coast

Day 3 | Jozani Forest Red Colobus Monkeys & Eastern Coast Check-In

Drive south to walk through the mahogany trails of Jozani Chwaka Bay National Park. Wooden boardwalks through dense groundwater forests and saltwater mangroves. Close-up views of the rare endangered Zanzibar Red Colobus Monkeys leaping through the canopy — endemic to this island. Also spotting the Zanzibar duiker and exotic bird species. Check-in at The Zawadi Hotel on the eastern coast.

The Zawadi Hotel →
Breakfast, Dinner

Day 4 | The Rock Restaurant & Kuza Cave Sacred Pools — Beach Bonfire Dinner

Afternoon excursion to Pingwe for The Rock Restaurant — private crowd-free lunch on the lone coral rock outcrop with waves at the windows and panoramic Indian Ocean views. Afternoon: the sacred Kuza Cave — walking down into a prehistoric limestone cavern to swim in crystalline subterranean freshwater pools revered for centuries. The day culminates in an elegant multi-course bonfire dinner on the Pingwe coast lit by hanging lanterns.

Boarding a short private charter flight north to Pemba Island — the true hidden gem of the Zanzibar Archipelago. Spectacular aerial views of turquoise water and lush green forests as you approach. Check-in at The Manta Resort: famous for its exclusive Underwater Room built on a permanent floating anchor — a private underwater suite with glass bedroom windows looking directly into the ocean. Gourmet dinner at the resort.

A full day of absolute relaxation in your private underwater suite. Day 7 | Deep-Sea Wall Walk — Pemba Channel Marine Adventure

Donning a specialised diving helmet for a Sea Walk along the sheer vertical underwater walls of the Pemba Channel — walking safely on the ocean floor viewing giant sea fans, manta rays, and a dazzling array of tropical fish along the dramatic drop-off. Guide leads along the wall explaining the ecology of this unique deep-ocean environment. One of the finest sea walk locations in the entire Indian Ocean.

Tides govern the best beach and water experiences

Zanzibar's beaches are tidal — swimming conditions vary significantly between high and low tide depending on which coast you're staying on. Your specialist will recommend the best-suited beach for your priorities.

Documents

Visa on arrival or e-Visa, from $50

  • Passport — Valid for at least six months beyond your arrival date, with at least two blank pages for stamps.
  • Visa — Zanzibar is part of Tanzania, so the Tanzanian eVisa or visa on arrival covers you. Single entry is US$50; US passport holders are issued the US$100 multiple-entry visa.
  • Compulsory island insurance — Since October 2024 every non-resident visitor must hold Zanzibar’s own inbound travel policy, sold only by the Zanzibar Insurance Corporation at US$44 per person for 92 days — half price for ages 3 to 17, free under 3. Your own international policy does not replace it, and the QR code is checked at the airport. We arrange this for you.
  • Dress away from the beach — Stone Town and the fishing villages are conservative. Shoulders and knees covered once you leave the resort grounds.
  • Yellow fever — A vaccination certificate is required if you are arriving from, or have transited, a country with yellow fever risk.
  • We handle the paperwork — We complete and submit your visa, eVisa or travel-authorisation applications on your behalf. You supply the documents once; we do the filing, track the approval and hold the confirmations for you.
  • Where you must appear in person — A few consulates insist on biometrics or a short interview that nobody can sit on your behalf — China requires fingerprinting for some nationalities, as do European and United States visas. We still prepare the entire file; we simply book you a single appointment and brief you beforehand so one visit settles it.
  • Before you travel — Entry rules change frequently and without much notice. Your specialist confirms the current requirements for every passport in your party at the time of booking, and checks them again before you fly.
