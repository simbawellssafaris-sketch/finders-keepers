Itinerary

Colombo, Sri Lanka

Day 1 | Nairobi → Colombo — Arrival in Sri Lanka

VIP handling at Nairobi's Jomo Kenyatta Airport, direct flight to Bandaranaike International Airport in Colombo. VIP tarmac reception, private guide to the Shangri-La Colombo, overlooking Galle Face Green.

Private guided tour of Colombo if arriving before 4:00 PM — historic landmarks, vibrant markets, beautiful waterfront. Elegant welcome dinner overlooking the Indian Ocean.

Shangri-La Colombo
Dinner
Sigiriya

Day 2 | Sigiriya — The Fortress of the Sky King

Drive into the Cultural Triangle. Sigiriya Rock Fortress — climbing past the giant carved Lion's Paws to the 5th-century royal sky palace and frescoes of King Kashyapa, a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Panoramic views from the summit over the surrounding jungle and ancient water gardens. Check into Water Garden Sigiriya — Luxury Plunge Pool Villa — or Heritance Kandalama. Gourmet dinner.

Water Garden Sigiriya
Breakfast, Dinner
Polonnaruwa & Minneriya

Day 3 | Polonnaruwa & The Minneriya Elephant Gathering

The monumental stone stupas and giant rock-carved Buddha statues of Polonnaruwa — the massive Gal Vihara with its four figures carved from a single granite boulder.

Afternoon in an open-top luxury 4x4 inside Minneriya National Park as the Gathering unfolds — hundreds of wild elephants converging around the ancient reservoir. Champagne and spiced cashews from the vehicle deck as the sun drops. Gourmet dinner.

Water Garden Sigiriya
Breakfast, Dinner
Ritigala

Day 4 | Hidden Gem — Ritigala Sunken Jungle Monasteries

Trek deep into Ritigala Nature Reserve — massive, beautifully preserved ancient stone paths and bathing pools buried beneath giant strangler fig trees, completely crowd-free. A 1st-century BC Buddhist monastery hidden in the jungle.

Rich biodiversity — wild elephants and rare birds. A multi-course Sri Lankan fusion bonfire dinner at the Sigiriya resort, lit by hundreds of hanging lanterns.

Water Garden Sigiriya
Breakfast, Dinner
Trincomalee, East Coast

Day 5 | Balloon over Sigiriya & Helicopter to Trincomalee

Before dawn, a hot air balloon drifting silently over the rock monoliths of Sigiriya and the emerald lake networks — the ancient fortress and surrounding jungle from above. Champagne toast and gourmet breakfast after landing.

A short private helicopter to the coastal shores of Trincomalee — the spectacular aerial coast and turquoise water. Check into Uga Jungle Beach — Ocean Villa. Gourmet dinner.

Uga Jungle Beach, Trincomalee
Breakfast, Dinner

Day 6 | Deep-Sea Blue Whale Tracking Safari

A private luxury speedboat from the harbour into the deep ocean canyon of Trincomalee — tracking the largest creatures on Earth, wild blue whales and sperm whale pods patrolling the deep water.

Hot gourmet breakfast and Ceylon coffee served on the speed boat deck floating in the open deep blue ocean. Afternoon at leisure at the resort. Gourmet dinner.

Uga Jungle Beach, Trincomalee
Breakfast, Dinner
Pigeon Island

Day 7 | Pigeon Island Marine Sanctuary

A private boat to Pigeon Island National Park — world-class snorkelling over untouched live coral reefs, swimming alongside harmless black-tip reef sharks and sea turtles in crystal-clear water.

The island's pristine beaches and tropical marine life. Return to the resort for a relaxed evening.

Uga Jungle Beach, Trincomalee
Breakfast, Dinner
Trincomalee

Day 8 | Koneswaram Clifftop Temple Architecture

A private architectural walk to the sacred Koneswaram Temple — perched on a sheer basalt cliff dropping vertically into the Indian Ocean, the colourful spires and 2,000-year history of this important Hindu site.

Your guide explains the history. The extraordinary cliff views. Return for gourmet dinner.

Uga Jungle Beach, Trincomalee
Breakfast, Dinner

Day 9 | Exclusive Indigenous Vedda Heritage Tour

Into the eastern coastal forests with community elders of the indigenous Vedda heritage — Sri Lanka's original hunter-gatherers. Ancient bow-and-arrow engineering, forest medicinal botany, bark-cloth crafting.

A private performance of jungle storytelling and ritual dances around a grand beachside bonfire. Celebratory farewell dinner at Uga Jungle Beach paired with vintage wines.

Uga Jungle Beach, Trincomalee
Breakfast, Dinner

Day 10 | Return to Colombo & Departure

A relaxed luxury breakfast watching the ocean waves. Check out, chauffeur transfer back along the national highway to Colombo. Complementary spa session at a city hotel. Airport transfer for international flight back to Nairobi.

Journey Details

Travel Kit

Duffel, sun hat, reef-safe sun cream and dry bag

  • Your duffel — A soft-sided {{brand}} duffel, sized for the luggage limits on seaplane and inter-island transfers, where 20 kg per person is common and hard cases are often refused.
  • What is inside it — A wide-brim sun hat, a reef-safe sun cream that meets the marine park rules across the region, and a roll-top dry bag for boat days and sandbank picnics.
  • Yours to keep — The bag and everything in it is a gift, not a loan.
  • Packing around it — Light, breathable layers, and one modest set covering shoulders and knees for Stone Town, villages, temples and local islands.

Note

The island has two distinct monsoon seasons

The west/south coast and hill country are driest from December to March, while the east coast is driest from May to September — your itinerary is routed to favour the best conditions.

  • Early starts — The best of this journey happens at dawn — before the heat, the crowds and the haze. Your guide will confirm what time you will be collected from your hotel.
  • Tailoring this journey — If you would like this package with more or fewer days, arranged for a group, a family or a solo traveller, or with add-ons, reach out and we will tailor it for you.
  • Route changes — Your guide or driver may alter the route in certain circumstances, but every destination set out in the itinerary will be covered.

Documents

ETA required in advance

  • Passport — Valid for at least six months beyond your arrival date, with at least two blank pages for stamps.
  • Electronic Travel Authorisation — An ETA is required before arrival. Since 25 May 2026 it is issued free of charge to nationals of 40 countries for a 30-day stay; other nationalities pay the standard fee.
  • At the temples — Shoulders and knees covered, shoes and hats removed. Never pose with your back to a Buddha image, and be aware that visible Buddha tattoos have caused travellers to be refused entry at Bandaranaike.
  • We handle the paperwork — We complete and submit your visa, eVisa or travel-authorisation applications on your behalf. You supply the documents once; we do the filing, track the approval and hold the confirmations for you.
  • Where you must appear in person — A few consulates insist on biometrics or a short interview that nobody can sit on your behalf — China requires fingerprinting for some nationalities, as do European and United States visas. We still prepare the entire file; we simply book you a single appointment and brief you beforehand so one visit settles it.
  • Before you travel — Entry rules change frequently and without much notice. Your specialist confirms the current requirements for every passport in your party at the time of booking, and checks them again before you fly.
