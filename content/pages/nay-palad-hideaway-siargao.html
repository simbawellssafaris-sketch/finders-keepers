Overview

A Barefoot
State of Mind

Formerly Dedon Island Resort, Nay Palad Hideaway was dreamed up by former Bayern Munich goalkeeper Bobby Dekeyser, whose innovative fibre furniture designs shaped a resort where guests are asked to do exactly one thing: treat this palm-fringed paradise as home.

Just ten freestanding villas make up the entire resort, each set a good distance from its neighbours amid tropical gardens planted with orchids and spider lilies, and finished with a nest-like design language carried through from the bar to the sea-perched pagodas. Every rate is genuinely all-inclusive — full-board dining, all beverages except champagne and premium wines, every excursion and water sport, yoga, spa treatments and overland airport transfers are included, with no bills ever signed during a stay. The resort has earned recognition across Condé Nast's Gold List, a Michelin Key, Tatler's Best and Travel + Leisure's World's Best, alongside its ranking as the #1 hotel on Siargao Island.

Landmark

Cloud 9

The world-famous barreling wave sits a short drive away, viewable from the resort's own three-storey wooden watchtower.

On the Water

The Sea Pagoda

An 800-metre boat, kayak or paddle trip reaches this thatch-roofed platform, a picturesque staging point for snorkelling.

Location

Siargao's
Southern Coast

Nay Palad Hideaway sits in Malinao, General Luna, on Siargao Island's southern coast, a short drive from the surfing hotspot of Cloud 9.

  • Located in Malinao, General Luna, on Siargao's laid-back southern coast.
  • A short drive from Cloud 9, Siargao's world-famous surf break.
  • Sayak Airport serves flights to Manila and Cebu, roughly 40 minutes from the resort.
  • Free overland airport transfers are provided in a traditional Filipino jeepney.
Fly

Sayak Airport on Siargao connects to Manila and Cebu, with Cebu offering onward links to Hong Kong and Singapore.

Transfer

A complimentary jeepney transfer, included in every stay, covers the roughly 40-minute drive from the airport.
