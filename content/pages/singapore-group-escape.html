Day by Day

Your Itinerary

Singapore — Hotel Mi Rochor

Day 1 | Arrival — Marina Bay Group Welcome

Private group transfers from Changi to Hotel Mi Rochor. After check-in, the evening begins with a relaxed group introduction to Singapore: the Supertree Grove at Gardens by the Bay creates an extraordinary first impression with glowing trees, indoor waterfalls, and futuristic domes illuminated against the night sky. Waterfront dining with cocktails, music, and Singapore's luxury atmosphere sets the tone for eleven days of shared experiences.

Hotel Mi Rochor
Dinner

Day 2 | Skyline Luxury — Marina Bay Sands, Merlion & Singapore Flyer

Marina Bay Sands SkyPark panoramic views showcase Singapore's extraordinary skyline, harbour, and futuristic architecture. Group photography at Merlion Park. Luxury shopping and rooftop café experiences. At sunset, the entire group boards the Singapore Flyer — the city slowly transforming into a glittering spectacle beneath golden evening light. The evening continues with rooftop nightlife around Clarke Quay and Marina Bay.

Hotel Mi Rochor
Breakfast

Day 3 | Sentosa Island Escape — Beach Clubs & Cable Car

Transfer to Sentosa Island and check-in at Village Hotel Sentosa. Sentosa Sensoryscape's interactive gardens and futuristic art installations. Beach clubs, infinity pools, and tropical cocktails on Siloso Beach. The afternoon energy builds at one of Sentosa's most vibrant oceanfront settings. At sunset, the group boards the Singapore Cable Car for panoramic views across the coastline and Mount Faber as the skyline glows beneath the evening sky.

Village Hotel Sentosa
Breakfast

Day 4 | Universal Studios Singapore — Full Group Adventure Day

The entire day dedicated to Universal Studios Singapore: thrilling roller coasters, movie-themed attractions, live stunt shows, cinematic rides, and immersive entertainment zones. Jurassic Park water adventure, Transformers, Revenge of the Mummy, and Hollywood Boulevard. VIP-style group exploration of the park with themed dining and luxury snack experiences. The evening continues with Sentosa nightlife and beachside dinners.

Village Hotel Sentosa
Breakfast

Day 5 | Wildlife Discovery & The Legendary Night Safari

Singapore Zoo's open rainforest habitats: orangutans, white tigers, elephants, and rare tropical animals in natural environments. Bird Paradise and River Wonders with giant pandas and immersive tropical river ecosystems. After dinner and rest, the Night Safari — one of the world's most unique wildlife experiences. Tram rides through illuminated jungle landscapes as nocturnal animals move freely in the darkness around the group. Genuinely extraordinary.

Hotel Mi Rochor
Breakfast

Day 6 | Southern Islands Escape & Luxury Yacht Experience

A complete escape from Singapore's urban atmosphere through a private island-hopping experience: Lazarus Island's wide white-sand beaches, St John's Island, and Kusu Island. Swimming, kayaking, picnic experiences, and tropical scenery that most Singapore visitors never reach. Late afternoon aboard the luxury yacht: music, cocktails, ocean breezes, sunset cruising, and skyline photography as the group sails back toward the city with Singapore glowing on the horizon.

Village Hotel Sentosa
Breakfast

Day 7 | Haji Lane, Chinatown & Singapore's Cultural Soul

Haji Lane: boutique cafés, hidden cocktail bars, murals, independent fashion, and the artistic energy that makes this Singapore's most photographed street. Chinatown's temples, heritage shophouses, and street food. Little India's markets, colour, and culture. Café hopping, local cuisine tasting, and shopping throughout the day. The evening at rooftop lounges overlooking the city — cocktails, conversation, and the view that never gets old.

Hotel Mi Rochor
Breakfast

Day 8 | Adrenaline Day — Skyline Luge, Mega Zip & iFly Singapore

The group's highest-energy day. Skyline Luge racing down Sentosa's purpose-built tracks. Mega Zip across Sentosa's coastline at speed. Giant swings with ocean views. iFly Singapore indoor skydiving — one of the world's largest wind tunnels. Optional GX-5 Slingshot for the true thrill-seekers. Balanced throughout with beach relaxation and cocktails to maintain energy. An unforgettable shared day of adrenaline and laughter.

Village Hotel Sentosa
Breakfast

Day 9 | Wellness & Relaxation Day — Joya Onsen & Marina Barrage

A deliberately slower day after the intensity of the adrenaline experience. Joya Onsen Cafe: Japanese-style stone baths, Zen gardens, aromatherapy massages, steam rooms, and wellness lounges. Rooftop pool relaxation. As the sun drops, the group heads to Marina Barrage for a sunset picnic overlooking Singapore's skyline — cocktails, conversation, and one of the most beautiful urban views in the world. Optional nightlife around the city to close the evening.

Hotel Mi Rochor
Breakfast

Day 10 | Jewel Changi & Group Farewell Celebration Dinner

Jewel Changi Airport's Rain Vortex indoor waterfall. Luxury shopping, final photography, and a last Singapore café experience at the group's own pace. The farewell celebration dinner has been carefully arranged: skyline views, live music, gourmet dining, cocktails, and photography moments celebrating eleven extraordinary days together. The kind of dinner that closes a journey properly.

Hotel Mi Rochor
Breakfast, Dinner

Day 11 | Departure from Singapore

Final breakfast and relaxation before private group transfers escort everyone to Changi International Airport for return flights to Nairobi. The group leaves Singapore carrying shared memories of nightlife, wildlife, adrenaline, islands, culture, and the particular luxury of experiencing a great city with people you love.

Documents

Visa-free for most nationalities

  • Passport — Valid for at least six months beyond your arrival date, with at least two blank pages for stamps.
  • Visa — Visa-free for up to 90 days for UK, EU and US passport holders, and 30 days for most other nationalities.
  • SG Arrival Card — File the SG Arrival Card online within three days of arrival. It is free on the official ICA site — the many paid look-alike sites are not official.
  • What Singapore does not allow in — Chewing gum, vaping devices and e-cigarettes are banned outright and possession carries heavy fines. There is no duty-free tobacco allowance either: every cigarette must be declared and duty paid.
  • We handle the paperwork — We complete and submit your visa, eVisa or travel-authorisation applications on your behalf. You supply the documents once; we do the filing, track the approval and hold the confirmations for you.
  • Where you must appear in person — A few consulates insist on biometrics or a short interview that nobody can sit on your behalf — China requires fingerprinting for some nationalities, as do European and United States visas. We still prepare the entire file; we simply book you a single appointment and brief you beforehand so one visit settles it.
  • Before you travel — Entry rules change frequently and without much notice. Your specialist confirms the current requirements for every passport in your party at the time of booking, and checks them again before you fly.

Guaranteed to operate with a minimum of 7 guests

  • Once confirmed, you travel — When your departure date is confirmed, it is guaranteed. We do not cancel, merge or reschedule a confirmed departure for commercial reasons, however the final numbers fall.
