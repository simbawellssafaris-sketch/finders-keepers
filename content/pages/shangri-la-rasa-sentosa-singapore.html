Overview

An Island Getaway,
15 Minutes from the City

Nestled beside Siloso Beach on the north-western tip of Sentosa Island, Shangri-La Rasa Sentosa remains Singapore's only true beachfront resort, close enough to the city for a day trip yet built to feel like an overseas escape.

All 454 guest rooms and suites open onto a private balcony with views of the South China Sea, landscaped gardens, hills or the resort's own pools, decorated in warm, natural tones with plush furnishings. Families gravitate to the resort's aquatic playground — three animal-themed water slides, a splash pad and a whirlpool bath — while the 24-hour gym's floor-to-ceiling windows look directly onto the surrounding greenery. Chi, The Spa, draws on time-honoured Asian wellness traditions for parents looking to steal a quiet hour, before regrouping for sunset drinks at the Siloso Beach Bar and a buffet dinner at Silver Shell Cafe, where a wandering peacock is not an unusual sight.

Complimentary shuttle to the city. The resort runs a complimentary shuttle service to and from central Singapore, alongside a Sentosa Island entry exemption for guests arriving by car or taxi with their booking confirmation.

Location

Siloso Beach,
Sentosa Island

Shangri-La Rasa Sentosa sits on the north-western tip of Sentosa Island, beside Siloso Beach, roughly 15 minutes from central Singapore.

  • Located at 101 Siloso Road, on Sentosa Island's north-western tip.
  • A 15-minute drive from Singapore's city centre, with a complimentary resort shuttle available.
  • Close to Universal Studios Singapore, the S.E.A. Aquarium, HydroDash and Mega Adventure Park.
  • Guests arriving by car or taxi can present their booking confirmation to enter Sentosa Island without the standard entrance fee.
