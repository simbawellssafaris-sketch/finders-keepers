Overview

The Newest Village
on Sentosa

The newest addition to the Village Hotels brand by Far East Hospitality, Village Hotel Sentosa is designed inside and out for families — sun-washed hues, four themed pools, and a lobby welcome that feels more carnival than check-in.

A Village Cart in the lobby hands out local sweets and snacks on arrival, setting a playful tone carried through to the resort's own Lazy River Pool, where guests float beneath lush foliage rather than rush between attractions. The 606 cosy rooms lean into an open, wardrobe-free layout with wall-mounted hanging hooks in place of a traditional closet, a small but telling sign of the hotel's efficient, design-led approach to family travel. Just minutes on foot from Imbiah Station, guests can walk to The Palawan @ Sentosa, Universal Studios Singapore, Singapore Oceanarium and KidZania, or step next door to Sensoryscape Station for a cable car ride through the island's rainforest canopy.

Part of a four-hotel Sentosa enclave. Village Hotel Sentosa sits within Far East Hospitality's cluster of four Sentosa properties, alongside The Barracks Hotel, Oasia Resort and the adults-only Outpost Hotel.

Location

The Centre of
Sentosa Island

Village Hotel Sentosa sits in the middle of Sentosa Island, within walking distance of Imbiah Station and several of Singapore's best-known family attractions.

  • Located near Imbiah Station, within walking distance of Wings of Time and Adventure Cove Waterpark.
  • Roughly 1.2 miles from the S.E.A. Aquarium and 1.4 miles from Universal Studios Singapore.
  • A short monorail ride or free shuttle from VivoCity and the causeway to mainland Singapore.
  • Guests arriving by car, taxi or the Sentosa Express monorail can enter the island without the standard entrance fee, with booking confirmation.
Fly

Singapore Changi Airport is roughly 30 to 40 minutes from the hotel by car.

Monorail

The Sentosa Express monorail connects from VivoCity, with a free ride available for booked guests.

Explore

Universal Studios, KidZania and The Palawan @ Sentosa are all within walking distance.
